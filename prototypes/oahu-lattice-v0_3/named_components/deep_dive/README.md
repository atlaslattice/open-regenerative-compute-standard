# Shreddernaut commercial integration: bottom ash, fly ash, products and costs

**October 23, 2026, America/Chicago. Research/decision support; no vendor endorsement, purchase, site clearance or new recovery credit.**

The strongest new finding is a division of duties: **mechanical recovery from bottom ash, contamination-specific treatment of fly ash/concentrates, and industrial product manufacturing are different branches**. A modular mobile front end can connect to a fixed or contracted treatment hub. Do not price or size the whole network as one screening machine.

## 1. Better commercial anchors for mechanical ash recovery

**Eggersmann / Knockharley, Ireland.** Its [supplier commissioning report](https://www.eggersmann-recyclingtechnology.com/en/news-events/eggersmann-delivers-new-processing-plant-near-dublin/) describes a 59 t/h bottom-ash plant, planned at more than 120,000 t/year on 255 eight-hour shifts. It includes impact liberation, magnetic/nonferrous separation and fines processing. This is a named installed industrial reference, not an independently audited yield. The account says a previous mobile/modular arrangement was replaced by a more integrated plant. It is particularly useful for asking when campaigns should graduate to a fixed hub.

**STEINERT / STORK, Leipzig.** The [named vendor case](https://steinertglobal.com/showcases-storage/stork/) reports a 140 t/h, 250,000 t/year installation using 11 STEINERT machines, with metal concentrate recovered from fractions of 2 mm and larger. Candidate equipment includes EddyC and ISS induction sorting; a later [MDSU case](https://steinertglobal.com/company/news/news-detail/a-german-flagship-project-for-bottom-ash-recycling/) adds XRT separation. Neither supplies an Oahu recovery guarantee or price. Whole-ash metal content and recovery of metal already present are different percentages.

**Blue Phoenix, United States.** Its [North American operator page](https://www.blue-phoenix.com/north-america/) reports 750,000 “tons” of IBA processed annually and advertises U.S. support. This is an operator-published aggregate footprint, not a named Hawaiian installation or offer. It provides a realistic service/partnership comparison alongside equipment ownership. Do not infer a transferable recovery rate from the page's differently scoped summary metrics or assume its “tons” are metric.

**Design implication (our inference):** investigate mechanical recovery and qualified mineral outlets before assigning the entire ash remainder to plasma melting. Bottom ash and fly/APC residues require separate assays and handling. These references strengthen named component choices without validating v0.3's 60% accepted ash-mass target.

## 2. Leer has multiple routes; the project/phase matters

The [supplier-submitted mineral-wool entry](https://www.ceett.org.cn/kjcg/jscg/698776837681221.shtml) identifies a train combining low-temperature treatment, washing, salt crystallization and melting/fiberization, with rock wool, salt and mineral outputs. It reports a 60,000 t/year Nantong project, but its phase/date/financial scopes require reconciliation. The company also has an [August 2026 Phase-I acceptance posting](https://haileer.com/newsinfo/11305793.html), whose indexed text identifies a **30,000 t/year low-temperature/brick phase**. The full posting text/attachments were not retrieved in this audit. Those figures cannot automatically describe the same commissioned line. A [2024 original field report hosted by Jiangsu government](https://www.jiangsu.gov.cn/art/2024/4/12/art_90315_11217665.html) independently provides a reported demonstration precedent for mineral wool, not audited current production or cost.

CEETT is a real national technology-transfer platform: the [MEE launch notice](https://www.mee.gov.cn/ywgz/kjycw/sthjkjcx/sthjkjcg/201908/t20190819_729195.shtml) establishes its identity. However, this particular entry explicitly says **enterprise self-submission**. Hosting there does not convert its performance or financial claims into a government guarantee.

A [2024 issuer response on the Shenzhen exchange disclosure host](https://reportdocs.static.szse.cn/UpFiles/rasinfodisc1/202403/RAS_202403_1821054C21DFA205E04C14B8C0BEC360B8E4C7.pdf), PDF pages 25–26/printed 24–25, identifies the mineral-wool route and estimates treatment cost near **CNY 2,200/t**, product revenue above **CNY 3,000/t**, plus treatment-fee revenue. These are issuer projections, not realized operating margins. They differ from the later complete-project entry in scope and amount; do not merge them into one quotation.

### Economic and material-accounting audit

The CEETT entry's full-project figures imply **CNY 3,483/t** cost on 60,000 t/year. In its lower-price case, treatment fees materially change the balance; the [reconciliation](reconciliation.json) independently sums the listed products. It also flags an approximately **CNY 0.453 million** discrepancy between the page's stated revenue total and its itemized numbers, and listed product quantities of approximately **137,770 t/year** versus 60,000 t/year of ash. Extra feed, additives, intermediate reuse or phase overlap must be resolved. These are not negative-waste or 230% ash-recovery findings. They are reasons to request the actual complete mass/cost balance. No Oahu cost is inferred.

At the listed lower product prices, our arithmetic yields **−CNY 26.90m/year before the stated treatment fees**, or **+CNY 45.10m/year with them**, against the reported annual costs. The difference is exactly CNY 72m of fees, not newly created public value. The approximate fee needed to close the itemized gap is CNY 448/t. Commercial waste generators can fund treatment through fees; public evaluation must compare full alternative costs and avoid counting a government payment as a net social gain.

## 3. Tianying remains a specialist branch, not the whole ash line

The earlier [operator-hosted 2020 acceptance opinion](https://www.cnty.cn/uploads/files/202011/4dec761e369557b66bb8a1872238b3ae.pdf) supports a 40 t/day / 13,320 t/year design and CNY 142m historical actual project investment. It includes an iron product, specialist heavy-metal-sludge disposal and extensive ancillary systems. It does not establish a zero-residue process or current market acceptance of every output.

Its suitability depends on the **eligible difficult fraction**, not total ash tonnage. V0.3's 57,578 t/year ash remainder is an original-mass-equivalent accounting quantity, not a qualified fly-ash feed assay. At a hypothetical 25% eligibility, the quantity would be about 14,395 t/year before correcting moisture/additives and uptime—already different from both total ash and the documented line's rating. No number of units is selected here.

We did not retrieve a verified Tianying full-train kWh/t value. The prior DeepSeek annual operating cost and payback remain attributed/unverified. No conversion from plasma-jet temperature to a process energy requirement is valid.

## 4. Another deployed route: cement-kiln co-processing, with a location constraint

The [national technology-directory case](https://www.ceett.org.cn/nowastecity/jscg/589802710011973.shtml) identifies Beijing BBMG Liushui ash washing/water treatment/cement-kiln co-processing at 100 t/day. It reports CNY 80m investment and CNY 1,337.57/t cost, **including depreciation**. This is a reported historical technology-case scope, not a current offer, and its electricity line is a monetary cost, not kWh.

The case requires a suitable clinker kiln, not merely a concrete batch plant. [Hawaiian Cement's own history](https://hawaiiancement.com/history/) says its manufacturing plant closed and its cement division now imports cement. Therefore no local operating kiln partner has been established; the case cannot be transferred to Oahu by connecting a washer to a ready-mix facility. Qualified aggregate/binder products and an actual new kiln are entirely different proposals.

## 5. Water integration and power remain design tasks

The [Alfa Laval/Jiarong case](https://www.alfalaval.com/media/stories/sustainability/raising-the-bar-to-protect-groundwater-in-china/) supports a multi-step leachate application, including specialist dry-residue disposal. [AlfaFlash](https://www.alfalaval.my/products/process-solutions/evaporation-systems/alfaflash/) is a forced-circulation flash evaporator and the vendor advertises pilot testing/rental. No Hawaii availability, capacity, energy rate or PFAS-destruction balance is confirmed.

Water duty must include Desen washing, ash washing, liquid-solid separation, leachate, contact water, recycle inventory, purge and concentrates. V0.3's inherent-water remainder alone cannot size a treatment train. Reclaimed irrigation water, contaminated process water and desal concentrate remain separately qualified streams.

The [electricity sensitivity](electricity_sensitivity.csv) uses hypothetical 100/500/1,000 kWh/t and $0.10/$0.30/$0.50 per kWh. It is not a supplier performance range. For example, **500 kWh/t × $0.30/kWh = $150/t of electricity alone**, before all other costs. Actual metered kWh/t and an applicable tariff are needed; a low-temperature reactor step does not prove low energy for the whole washing/evaporation/manufacturing train.

## 6. A more concrete Shreddernaut configuration

| Branch | Candidate building blocks | What to implement or investigate |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile intake and recovery | Desen screen; suitable magnets/eddy-current equipment; loaders/conveyors | Characterization, hazard isolation, whole items, controlled screening and accepted concentrates |
| Selected mineral washing | Desen DSLXC/01–04 or fixed equivalent | Treat only eligible fractions; close cake/water balances; test actual product acceptance |
| Bottom-ash mechanical recovery | Eggersmann integration; STEINERT separators; Blue Phoenix service comparator | Independent ash campaign/hub with liberation, grading and nonferrous recovery |
| Fly ash / salt / manufacturing | Leer Phase-I brick route and/or separate melting/mineral-wool route | Request phase-specific capacity, process, additive balance, emissions and buyer commitments |
| Difficult concentrate service | Tianying or authorized specialist alternatives | Reserve for qualified problematic fraction; compare treatment service versus owning a fixed installation |
| Water and concentrate | Desen process-water treatment plus Jiarong/Alfa Laval where compatible | Pilot actual liquor; quantify polishing, salt/concentrate and residual destinations |
| Secondary polymer recovery | Sesotec reference chain, STADLER integration alternative | Actual feed trials; sorting alone does not establish saleable polymer or remove all contamination |
| Shared utilities, records and support | Metering, controls, buffers, qualified fabrication and maintenance | One integration responsibility; complete operating ledger and final endpoints |

The initial mobile recovery package and the later industrial manufacturing hub need separate budgets. A large factory's historical investment is not the price of one optional trailer module. Conversely, the screen's reference price is not the price of complete remediation.

## 7. How this connects the wider lattice

Potential outputs now have more specific destinations: mineral wool for construction/fire/thermal products, qualified salts for industrial use, metal concentrates for refiners, and tested mineral products for construction. Calcium-sulfate or potassium-bearing outputs need contaminant/purity qualification before any agricultural or import-displacement claim. Manufacturing, heat and process-water integration can be evaluated without assuming they are automatically beneficial.

The lattice's useful output here is a set of allocation choices: sell a qualified material, consume it internally, upgrade it, or pay for safe treatment. Compare netbacks, demand, energy, added inputs, ownership and residual liabilities. Mineral wool, bricks, salts and washed intermediate ash cannot all be credited as if each came exclusively from the same tonne without a reconciled recipe.

## 8. The next supplier evidence to request

1. **Desen:** full line balance at the agreed feed basis, cake/water assays, 60 Hz package, factory-integrated interface and complete quote.
2. **Eggersmann/STEINERT/Blue Phoenix:** characterize H-POWER ash, distinguish bottom/fly/combined handling, quote a representative trial and compare service versus ownership. Ask what happens to the mineral remainder.
3. **Leer:** identify legal entity, exact phase/model, commissioned versus planned capacity; provide the Phase-I 30,000 t/year acceptance materials and clarify their relation to the 60,000 t/year whole-project listing. Reconcile all ash/additive/water/products, actual buyers, price assumptions, fees and cost scopes.
4. **Tianying:** test eligibility of the actual residual, meter full-train electricity/reagents, identify current product and heavy-metal-sludge outlets, and quote treatment service plus equipment alternatives.
5. **Jiarong/Alfa Laval:** test actual process liquor and provide full water/contaminant balance, energy/heat grade, PFAS suite fate and final concentrate/media disposal.
6. **All parties:** common interfaces, downtime buffers, wear, service, destination certification and witnessed acceptance tests; normalize quotes to actual annual throughput and the same endpoint.

**No v0.3 recovery percentage has changed.** These findings turn technology placeholders into better procurement choices and reveal where apparently comparable prices, phases or yields are not comparable. The remaining 38.69% requires assays and accepted endpoints before another defensible reduction can be credited.
