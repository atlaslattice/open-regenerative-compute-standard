## Purpose
Replace uncertain feedstock assumptions with operator-approved, dated and site-specific masses of recovered **food-grade fermentable sugars, salts, terpenes, esters, pectin and protein potential**, preserving prior food, feed and soil uses. Proposed stages: (1) source-separated organics and specialized upstream resource recovery; (2) qualification of distinct recovered sugars, salts and flavor fractions under human-food regulations; (3) **separate** hygienic Quorn-benchmark \`Fusarium venenatum\` mycoprotein fermentation on defined food-grade medium; (4) texture/flavor and palatability work at qualified food facilities; (5) actual commercial life-cycle and impact measurement. No raw mixed landfill matter, sewage sludge, biosolids, uncertified industrial nutrient salts or arbitrary postconsumer waste allowed to become human-food inputs by mere sterilization.

**Priority:** Extract, identify and validate Maui-origin food-grade **citrus/lilikoi/pineapple terpene plus ester/acid aroma blends and pectin texture**; fungal protein must satisfy fully separated strain/process/FDA and facility rules. Do not equate aroma doses to physiological performance doses.

## The requested source-by-source record (one line per lot class and month)
1. **Date and location:** calendar month, Maui-Island facility identifier, crop, stage (packing/juice/food process/retail), lot ID pseudonym. Avoid PII and commercially confidential identities unless separately cleared.
2. **Mass conservation:** incoming wet/dry kg, final food sold/donated, export/retail units, food-safe trim/offgrade kg, animal feed kg, existing compost kg, already discarded kg, other residues kg, inventory change. No overlapping allocation.
3. **Chemistry:** moisture, Brix, fermentable glucose/fructose/sucrose, starch, nitrogen, P/K/Mg, pH, citrus oil, linalool/limonene/citral, relevant ester profile, pectin content with actual GC-MS / chromatography CoAs where available.
4. **Safety:** documented food-grade handling, pesticides, trace metals, microbes, mycotoxin and cleaning-agent residues, any mixed/industrial exposure; qualify recovered media independently. Owner consent is required.
5. **Rights and cost:** existing food, soil/feed purchase/contract destination and opportunity price; measured disposal/haul/energy/cold storage, environmental and indigenous/community constraints; no unpriced extraction from local ecosystems.
6. **Pilot outcomes:** actual recoverable kg certified sugar/kg source, terpene/ester and pectin kg/kg dry residue, food-grade mycoprotein dry mass and true protein per substrate type under approved strain/conditions, RNA reduction, energy/water/COA results, validated taste/texture and consumer acceptance.
7. **UNKNOWN is an acceptable answer**. Never turn observed fruit exports into hypothetical discarded peel, annualize isolated weeks, apply outside laboratory sugar yields to Maui, or count one ton as competing products twice.
