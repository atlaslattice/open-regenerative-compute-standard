## November 1, 2026 — produced brine & UK site reporting update

The [ORNL/Element3 oilfield-brine lithium report (Oct 7)](https://www.ornl.gov/news/national-lab-partnership-helps-launch-u-s-lithium-supply-chain/) adds a *distinct* selective lithium recovery source. Do not transfer seawater chemistry, Saltworks results or any sorbent selectivity into produced water without direct assays. New minimum data: aqueous ion assay with uncertainty, daily brine flow, feed temperature, sorbent loss/cycle, competing sodium/calcium/magnesium, concentration gradients, energy/reagents, returned/disposed water and waste solids, qualified lithium product grade and actual sales.

The [UK Data Centre Covenant (Oct 28)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/data-centre-covenant/the-data-centre-covenant) calls for responsible water and energy accounting, public reporting for strategic projects, PUE 1.3 design aspirations, closed-loop systems, community investment and practical waste-heat reuse. This is a **UK policy benchmark**, not a U.S. legal requirement, and does not itself mandate an explicit rainwater-first hierarchy. For ORCS Maui/Corpus/Dongjiakou: report withdrawal, internal circulation, evaporative consumption, water returned, rain/reuse capture, heat reuse and **net site water draw** as independent ledgers. Never equate "zero operational cooling water" with zero absolute campus water footprint.
