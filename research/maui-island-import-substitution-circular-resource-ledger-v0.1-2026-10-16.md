## Important data-integrity finding
There is **no verified comprehensive Maui-Island imports-by-product or landed-value ledger** in this baseline. The model's annual **$2.835B imported-products basket is deliberately hypothetical** and is **NOT** a new measurement of Maui trade. The claimed $75.4M/$275.35M/$610M gross displacement low/central/high figures are strictly **scenario arithmetic**, **not cost savings, GDP gains, revenue, margin or guaranteed off-island substitution**. Maui County waste, water and 2022 agriculture measurements must not be relabeled Maui Island-specific. Existing ORCS water/power infrastructure may overlap proposed new facility costs.

## Distinct physical scenario layers (never add to imported dollars again)
- 53,000 reported County tons of green waste+biosolids: capture **25/50/75%** => **13,250/26,500/39,750 INPUT tons/year**, not salable compost tonnage. Actual categories may overlap.
- Historic Lahaina R-1 injection 3.16 MGD: reuse **10/30/55%** => **0.316/0.948/1.738 MGD**, which is recycled secondary usage, not newly created water. Useful real source displacement requires actual service and water-rights confirmation.
- Marine brine: value all salts and water, with full ZLD/marine protection as an environmental design objective even when product revenue falls short; do not assume salts all saleable. Deuterium is a trace **isotope in water molecules**, not conventional dissolved-salt revenue.

## Institutional priority sequence
**Tier 1:** permitted organics & clean soil processing; local food cold-chain and co-op handling; repair/reuse; realistic West Maui collection connected to existing facilities.  
**Tier 2:** real customer-backed R-1 reuse; certified C&D and printable materials; BioSolar/battery grid reliability under utility-approved controls.  
**Tier 3:** technically expensive desal/ZLD and chemical refinery only after full lifecycle marine and energy models, renewable/firm power, chemical product assays and contracted buyers; not assumed to finance itself.

**No-go safeguards:** cultural/Native Hawaiian water rights, resident affordability and employment priority, ocean/reef protection, invasive species safety, civil-hazard exposure, code-verified recycling products, critical battery handling, emissions and genuine R-1 reuse, actual data-governance rights. A project that transfers contamination from ocean to unprotected inland salt piles fails. Retain resilience reserves before exports under lawful agreements, and never double-count materials.
