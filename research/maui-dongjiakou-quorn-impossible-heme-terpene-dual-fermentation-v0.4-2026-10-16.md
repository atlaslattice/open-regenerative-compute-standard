# Maui × Dongjiakou — Quorn, Impossible Foods, Defined-Medium Mycoprotein, Heme and Terpene Recovery
**ORCS / Atlas Lattice | v0.4 | 2026-10-16 | PROPOSED · EVIDENCE-LINKED · ZERO REALIZED CREDIT**

## Architecture decision
The project will **not** grow edible fungus directly on heterogeneous municipal waste. Specialized resource-recovery systems first sort and convert wastes into separate resources; only certified, assayed, traceable **food-grade** glucose, nitrogen and mineral nutrients can enter a distinct hygienic fermentation system. Neither treatment nor sterilization automatically qualifies recovered waste-derived inputs for human food. Contaminated sewage, sludge, grease-trap mixtures, postconsumer unknowns and industrial-grade recovered materials are **not** presumed food-grade. Product and process validation, jurisdiction-specific regulatory review, microbial toxin/allergen screening, contamination controls and actual nutrient assays are required.

## Industrial protein reference: Quorn
Quorn reports growing **Fusarium venenatum** mycoprotein in large airlift fermenters on **high-grade carbohydrate-derived glucose** with ammonium, potassium, magnesium, phosphate and trace nutrients, under managed pH, temperature and oxygen. This is the preferred industrial benchmark for defined-medium filamentous protein, NOT a claim that Maui has the same infrastructure or feedstock. Quorn's documented continuous equipment is ~40m high, with five-week production runs. 
- https://www.quornnutrition.com/mycoprotein/what-is-mycoprotein
- https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tifs.2025.104981
- China NHC 2025 specifies F. venenatum **A3/5 and TB01** with process restrictions: https://www.nhc.gov.cn/sps/c100087/202511/b4a9a5f201644626b1c375d10fcadbe5.shtml
**Strain decision:** prioritize A3/5 as industrial benchmark; TB01 as comparison subject to independent US food authorization, nutrient composition, quality and sourcing. N. intermedia as separately regulated experimental comparator for food byproduct processes. No Maui-specific yields have been established.

## Precision fermentation comparator: Impossible Foods
Impossible Foods' soy leghemoglobin is **not synthetic human hemoglobin** and not the same as Quorn mycoprotein. It is an iron-containing **soybean leghemoglobin** protein manufactured by a modified yeast historically called **Pichia pastoris**, now commonly **Komagataella phaffii**. It supplies characteristic cooked-meat flavor/color and some iron, **not the bulk protein base**. FDA GRN **737** concluded no questions for **up to 0.8% soy leghemoglobin protein** in cooked ground-beef analogue products; a separate FDA 2019 rule authorized a specified **color additive** use. FDA GRN **1202**, closed March 7, 2025, covers specified soy leghemoglobin preparations for meat, poultry, fish analogues and **nutrition bars, protein/nutritional powders, cookies and brownies**, up to **2% soy leghemoglobin protein** in finished foods under that notification. **Neither GRN is blanket approval for a liposomal liquid gel, every producer, a new substrate or a new color use**. Use counsel and a food process authority for exact intended product and manufacturing conditions.
- https://www.hfpappexternal.fda.gov/scripts/fdcc/index.cfm?id=737&set=GRASNotices
- https://www.hfpappexternal.fda.gov/scripts/fdcc/index.cfm?id=1202&set=GRASNotices
- https://www.fda.gov/food/hfp-constituent-updates/fda-announces-effective-date-final-rule-adding-soy-leghemoglobin-list-color-additives-exempt

## Product strategy: keep flavor functions separate
1. **Unity Gel sweet variants:** top priority is qualified **Maui-grown citrus/lilikoi/pineapple flavor fractions**, including food-grade peel oils, terpenes such as d-limonene/linalool at appropriate flavor-use levels, and the full ester/acid fruit profile; pectin/fiber extraction for texture. Test real fruit lots and mass balances; actual terpene kg/year and cost UNKNOWN. **Soy leghemoglobin is not presumed useful in fruit-flavored gel**; it may create unwanted iron/metallic, color and oxidative effects.
2. **Savory mycoprotein food line:** Quorn-type mycoprotein plus qualified mushroom/umami and potentially precision-fermented soy leghemoglobin is a credible separate R&D concept for cooked meat analogues; do not extrapolate to uncooked liquid gel or make unauthorized claims.
3. **Liposomal actives:** specific nutrients only after physical particle characterization, stability, food safety and meaningful bioavailability evidence. Flavor-oil emulsion/microencapsulation is not automatically liposomal nutrition.

## R&D gates
A. Producer-authorized actual food byproduct mass and full wet/dry, sugar, nitrogen, contaminants and seasonality assays.
B. Compare food-grade recovered nutrients against purchased food-grade reference media, not raw waste; validate fungal biomass dry mass, actual protein, amino acids, RNA, allergen and toxin results.
C. Prioritize Maui **terpene/fruit-aroma extraction** (GC-MS) and **pectin** mass balance; test citrus-lilikoi, pineapple-lime and cacao options against blinded reference, separating pleasant fruit aroma from terpene performance claims.
D. For savory applications only, compare no-heme vs approved purchased leghemoglobin with strict use/labeling and sensory testing before considering a local precision-fermentation facility.
E. Publish measured kg/kg and kWh/kg and process receipts; calculate Maui import substitution only from proven island-origin inputs and signed sales; mark all other flows **UNKNOWN**.
