## 0. Evidence and deployment ladder

Track **event** and **design relevance** independently:

- **E0 PUBLIC IDEA/INTENT:** policy intention, planned product or research proposal; no delivered product.
- **E1 SOURCE-TRACEABLE ANNOUNCEMENT:** dated authenticated original company/agency announcement, without independent experimental substantiation.
- **E2 PROTOTYPE/REFERENCE DESIGN REPORTED:** company or scientific team publishes specific design or prototype data; label **vendor-reported** if not independently replicated.
- **E3 COMMERCIAL COMMITMENT:** a documented financing round, commercial access agreement, signed partnership or order; specify *exactly what was agreed*; no inference that units or houses are delivered.
- **E4 QUALIFIED PRODUCTION:** actual supplier production, source-country/lot proof and third-party qualification of the specific marketed SKU; do not award from PR wording alone.
- **E5 OPERATIONAL FIELD PERFORMANCE:** dated installed operation with measured outcomes, audited as needed, for actual application, conditions and scope.

**Evidence labels:** PUBLISHED_VENDOR, PUBLISHED_GOVERNMENT, PUBLISHED_RESEARCH, DERIVED, PROPOSED, UNVERIFIED, SOURCE_REPRODUCED, GATE_PASSED. E1/E3 can coexist: a signed commercial agreement can exist before any delivered homes. No asserted "council validated" or "AI consensus" is equivalent to independent engineering approval.

### Everywhere Factory / Housing
- Compare **on-site ICON**, **off-site Promise/D.R. Horton** and **hybrid county factory/onsite installation** at *house complete* scope. Variables: site/civil/foundation, walls, roof, MEP, assembly, certification, logistics, vacancy, equipment $/operating hour, yield and permits. The 3M units / $200k target remains an **owner planning ambition**, not supported by these announcements. No automatic assumption that prefabrication/houses are fully printed or fully automated.
- Derive a **robotic two-material subpart catalog** common to housing, cooling and transformer (only if materially and electrically approved): enclosures, noncritical clips, fixtures, panel hardware, ducts and sensor carriers. Track serial/lot and circular feedstock.

### Shreddernaut / Materials
- Segregate **municipal solid waste, construction/demolition, electronic waste, end-of-life solar PV, oilfield produced brine and desalination brine** as *different input identities, environmental permits and chemical processes*. Zero import and zero feedstock acquisition-price conditions are **not universal**; processing, transport, energy, water, purification and rejected fractions always positive budget terms unless offset by verified credits.
- E-waste/PV: screen Pb/Cd and electronics batteries, dismantle high-risk items before shredding, choose certified smelter/hydrometallurgical route, reserve/distribution by product purity. Oilfield lithium: assay real formation water, competing ion loads, sorbent capacity/aging, product recovery cost and disposal water balance. No quantified national lithium harvest without samples and flow.

### Cooling / water / data centers
- Benchmark **500 kW ORCS CDU** against **Delta 2.0/2.5MW** differentiated product benchmarks; include T_supply/return, delta-T, pressure, fluid mix, bypass filters, fault tolerance, maintenance and **cooling power draw**. Count full-loop water withdrawal/return and externalized power-water footprint separately from vendor "zero operational cooling water" claim.
- UK Covenant: add optional site-facing public-benefit, electricity capacity cost, PUE/WUE, renewable vs grid, water reuse and waste-heat reporting matrix; **not applicable US regulation**. Community power/water offset is a separately calculated, contractually funded ORCS target, not legal fact.
- DOMESTIC manufacture: verify actual component origin vs final assembly in Texas/Virginia before crediting whole system as 100% local-source material.

### AHD / Batteries
- First cell still **AHD-D1 transformer dielectric coupons**, with independent G3 gate. Battery second track **BATT-AHD-01** explores particle organization in transparent photopolymer carrier, but requires commercially meaningful electrode chemistry, electrolytes/separators, conductivity, cycling and resistance tests. Never infer conventional current collector fabrication via DISH. BattMan participation not claimed.

### Stars / Beyond Q
- New comparison study: **PPPL/CFS model-driven experimental pulse twin** vs independent ORCS reference-pipeline. Their listed M3D-C1/TRANSP/XGC codes relate to tokamak physics; **WarpX kinetic PIC** and **OpenMC neutron transport** address other validated portions. Integrate via a typed interface with separate nuclear reaction rates, boundary conditions, uncertainties, thermal flows and timing. Preserve reaction-specific conservation and existing independent DD/DT isotope closure. No published SPARC <20min benchmark means current result remains future *project target*.
- Do **not** change the owner 1.10 GW D-D or 1.13 GW D-T scenario merely because a SPARC news release uses "ARC"; different machine, regime, fuel cycle and validation.
