# Known gaps and next evidence

Status: UNKNOWN / PROPOSED; no field-verified implementation or realized savings claimed.

## Physical stream and geography duplication

Cannot sum permits, outfalls, influent, centrate, sludge and ash.

**Needed:** Unique plant/stream IDs, parent accounts and period-aligned mass balances.

## Existing reuse and energy recovery

Gross potential does not establish new supply.

**Needed:** Current product, land-application, heat, electricity and flare baseline for each plant.

## Flow and chemistry

Generic concentrations cannot rank verified yields.

**Needed:** Monthly flow, P fractions, NH4/TN, Mg/K/Ca/S, COD/VS, contaminants and uncertainty.

## Net electricity and heat

Installed MW is not usable or island-capable power.

**Needed:** Parasitics, availability, heat sink, temperature, black-start, protection and storage.

## Processing cost and mature vs first plant

National profit unestablished.

**Needed:** Engineering budget, labor, reagents, H2, catalysts, maintenance, financing and downtime.

## Residual treatment and PFAS/microplastics

Removal from water is not destruction or safe soil reuse.

**Needed:** Mass closure to solids/air/water; destruction/byproduct testing; permitted residual destination.

## Buyer and crop suitability

Nutrient content is not guaranteed fertilizer displacement.

**Needed:** Product grade, plant availability, customer demand, seasonal application and delivered cost.

## Policy credits

Credit dependence can change viability.

**Needed:** Eligibility, credit price scenarios and zero-credit case; keep separate from commodity sales.

## Regional import replacement

National ratios do not establish a route.

**Needed:** Destination purchases by product/origin, capacity, contracts, season and freight.

## All nonmunicipal streams

Total national all-wastewater inventory incomplete.

**Needed:** Separate industrial/mining/landfill/agricultural registers with boundary checks.

## Priority for the next version

1. Replace generic concentrations with period-aligned, flow-weighted national loads.
2. Subtract existing nutrient, land-application and energy recovery.
3. Obtain process-specific capital, reagent, energy, disposal and product-quality evidence.
4. Match named buyers and delivered products to purchases actually displaced.
5. Harmonize the different federal methane/electricity potential estimates.
6. Extend industrial, agricultural, mining and decentralized accounts without duplicating municipal inputs.
