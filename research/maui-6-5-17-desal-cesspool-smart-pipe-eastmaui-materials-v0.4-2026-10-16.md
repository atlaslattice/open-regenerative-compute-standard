## Decision record (PROPOSED)
- **West Maui desal: 6 MGD nameplate; North Shore desal: 5 MGD; Central Maui desal: 17 MGD = 28 MGD nameplate.**
- Assuming a modeling-only 85% net dependable output, these produce **5.10, 4.25, 14.45 = 23.80 MGD**, compared with *historic Maui County DWS* household meter volume **22.5 MGD**. Apparent headroom 1.30 MGD is NOT an island-specific coverage proof: historical county data include Molokaʻi, exclude private household systems, may contain second homes, and do not include essential public/commerce peak and firefighting service. Validate West and North individually; their output cannot automatically be assigned to Central without actual transmission routes.
- Initial water allocation rule: permanent households (safe affordable drinking/bathing/essential use), hospitals/schools/emergency/fire and legally protected users prioritized over discretionary high-volume resort contracts. Commercial food safety, essential services and lawful preexisting water rights must be honored. Resort infrastructure investors may negotiate incremental capacity and recycled-water delivery where law permits, but never bypass emergency priorities. This is proposed policy, not enacted law.
- Potable streams undergo legally approved RO, disinfection and post-treatment/remineralization as necessary for corrosion control, taste and health. 'PHd' = project shorthand for appropriate pH and treated potable quality, NOT clinically superior water.
- Recover *all safely and reasonably collectable* Maui Island public, private, commercial, residential and visitor wastewater, plus appropriate **cesspool conversion** and separate greywater use where approved. Supply compliant agricultural/industrial reclaimed water; isolate microplastics and contaminants; segregate brine and sludge; require no routine waste liquid discharge from new ORCS units; prioritize productively reused residuals and controlled secure final solids. Neither ZLD nor incineration equals zero waste.
- Co-locate at every campus an appropriately sized, water-frugal, independently secured AI inference node and conventional independent validated safety systems; integrate roof/canopy PV, biosolar/biogas and candidates for locally feasible geothermal.

## Mass balance: what is and is not new
Existing public-plant ALL-SOURCE historical wastewater: **Lahaina 3.41 + Kīhei 3.93 + Wailuku–Kahului 5.54 = 12.88 MGD**. At hypothetical 90% recovery -> 11.592 MGD treated reclaimed output (including existing 4.37 MGD reused). The notional additional recovery above existing recycled use is up to 7.222 MGD, before permits/demand/treatment/distribution constraints.
Older Hawaiʻi DOH estimates: ~12,200 Maui Island cesspools and 7.9 MGD discharge to onsite systems. This is separate from sewer-connected municipal plant influent in principle but requires parcel/source overlap check; it is not automatically collectable. **12.88+7.9=20.78 MGD** is *inventory scale*, NOT confirmed recoverable feedwater, and unverified private plants may add additional sources. DOH onsite systems GIS layer 24: https://geodata.hawaii.gov/arcgis/rest/services/Infrastructure/MapServer/24 . County sewer GIS: https://www.mauicounty.gov/2939/Wastewater-GIS-Program .
Model household circular target from county DWS baseline 22.5 MGD x 70% returned x 90% treatment yield = **14.175 MGD second-use**, contingent on expanded real catchment collection. This is already part of whatever municipal/onsite sewer streams supply it; DO NOT ADD household target to municipal/cesspool totals.
Agriculture and industry receive reclaimed water where end-use certified. Fraction recaptured in further cycles is crop/process-specific; loss to plant transpiration is beneficial consumptive use, not recoverable 'leakage'. Track first-, second-, third-pass water services but new water only once. No speculative closed-loop 100% recovery or products guaranteed.
All reclaimed supply should carry measured PFAS and trace chemistry as risk-based; salinity, sodium adsorption ratio, boron, pathogen standards, micro/nanoplastics sampling and retained contaminant handling matter for agricultural soils.

## Existing infrastructure: preserve sunk assets and OFFSET CURRENT SPENDS
For each supply/pressure zone/asset, record: current annual actual withdrawals (groundwater/stream/reservoir), produced MGD, delivered MGD, % physical loss vs nonrevenue accounting, historical treatment OPEX, pumping kWh, chemical and staff bills, repairs and renewal CAPEX, revenue and debt obligations, water-quality compliance, fire reserves, transfer costs and residual value.
Avoided annual cost = (genuinely *avoidable* old OPEX + genuinely deferred/avoided old renewal capital annualized + pumping and emergency-supply costs avoided) minus (new conveyance, energy, O&M, monitoring, residual legacy mothball/standby requirements). Historic sunk investment is NOT a recoverable cost saving. A well retired might retain standby costs. Record decommissioning costs and water-rights actions before claiming closure. Do NOT count avoided wholesale water purchase AND avoided pumping of the SAME gallon as separate benefits without reconciliation.
Existing Maui water rates and utilities financials are NOT equivalent to marginal avoided source OPEX. County budgets: https://www.mauicounty.gov/1896/Budget-Documents-Ordinances . Existing Maui water use rates: https://www.mauicounty.gov/216/Water-Charges .
Environmental ledger independent: stream diversion reduction by permit point; change in aquifer pumping at metered well; independently measured ecological water returned; monitor freshwater dependence of local communities. The principal benefit is measured extraction retired, NOT just new infrastructure.

## Perovskite and other high-value products: scientific evidence gate
A perovskite is **a crystal structure**, not an element that appears in sewage ready for extraction.
- 2026 lab study selectively recovered up to 98% calcium and 91% magnesium from desalination reject brine under its reactor conditions; these are **specific experiments, not Maui forecasts**. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2949823626001431
- 2026 paper on perovskite PV device **recycling** recovers high-purity Pb, Cs, iodine and bromine compounds from discarded cells and reuses them to fabricate new perovskite absorbers; **not bioreactor formation from ordinary sewage**: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2211285526002119
- 2026 paper on perovskite-like structure for rare earth pre-enrichment from *industrial molten-salt chlorination residue* is a different, high-temperature waste stream and not evidence of sewage/saline bioreactor economic yield: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0301479726019936
**Candidate branches:** (1) commercially tested seawater Mg/Ca salts/gypsum and recovered nutrient lines; (2) iron/phosphate struvite recovery from appropriate streams; (3) high-purity mineral-derived titanate or other functional perovskite ceramic *manufacturing* pilot, conditional on Ti/source economics, purification and emissions; (4) PV panel material end-of-life recycling. No perovskite revenue or efficiency credit in baseline. Keep Pb/Cs/halide processing segregated and under hazardous-material controls.

## East Maui non-extractive interbasin policy target
**Proposed result:** eliminate *avoidable interbasin extraction/export of East Maui stream water for Central and West Maui commercial/agricultural dependence* as replacement supply and efficient recycled irrigation become available, restoring scientifically and culturally appropriate instream flows.
**Explicit safeguard:** Do not impose a unilateral 'ban on ANY East Maui withdrawals' that would deprive Hāna/Keʻanae residents of safe drinking water, lawful Native Hawaiian kalo/loʻi and customary uses, DHHL reservations or domestic/fire emergency supply. Native Hawaiian representatives must define local priorities and participate in consent/governance. Local groundwater/watersheds are sensitive, not an assumed free replacement supply. Existing streams/aquifers can serve legally protected local uses at independently verified sustainable levels. Proposed Hāna local quality treatment and PH-balanced potable + microplastic capture and safe reuse applies only after source assessments and cultural/ecological review.
**Evidence:** August 2026 Hawaiʻi DLNR emphasizes native traditional rights, public-trust duties, municipal/domestic supply, instream standards and CWRM jurisdiction: https://governor.hawaii.gov/newsroom/dlnr-news-release-dlnr-takes-action-on-two-east-maui-water-issues-aug-14-2026/ ; October 8 2026 Native Hawaiian Legal Corporation case update: https://nativehawaiianlegalcorp.org/east-maui-water-court-ordered-contested-case-moves-forward/ .
Engineering proof obligation: calculate actual interbasin diversion by intakes, separately from permitted ceiling; create transfer/infrastructure replacements with agriculture recycled demand matching, municipal alternate-source security and monitoring; no claim of forced closure without lawful public-trust process.

1. Obtain Maui Island-only, West/Central/North household and commercial metered annual/peak flows, private purveyors and 2026 post-fire updated counts.
2. Sewer/as-built GIS extraction and defensible pipe route quantity takeoff (DO NOT imply complete blueprints already reproduced).

4. County/provider avoidable current OPEX and deferred asset CAPEX verified by asset, excluding sunk investments and retained standby expenses.
5. East Maui diversion-by-intake data, Native Hawaiian/local public-trust engagement, protected local water availability, farm target after independently tested sensor-led irrigation savings.
6. Campus electric+thermal energy and ZLD residual mass-balance for 6/5/17 MGD; independently model biosolar, compute and geothermal only where evidence supports.
7. District food/organic solid-waste tonnage traceable to scale receipts; perovskite material evidence checks, no unsupported revenue.
8. Government, engineering, utility, indigenous-rights-holder and environmental permitting and economic model review.

**Bottom line:** The revised 6+5+17 MGD portfolio is a viable *screening configuration* for a residents-first network; whether it fully serves Maui households depends on actual district billing/hydraulics. Capturing currently untreated cesspool flows is a real opportunity but cannot be counted before buildout. Aboveground sheathed piping with fiber and co-located inference is an **alternative to test on real surveyed routes**, not yet a reproduced set of Maui engineering blueprints.
