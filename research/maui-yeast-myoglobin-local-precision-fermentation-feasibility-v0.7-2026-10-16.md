## Decision
**YES, microbial production of food-use myoglobin on Maui is technically plausible**. There are two separate operational senses of “locally sourced yeast”:

1. **Island-propagated qualified yeast:** obtain a licensed/authorized genetically engineered precision-fermentation **Komagataella phaffii** producer strain and legally import it into Hawaiʻi; grow its future inoculum and fermentation biomass **on Maui** from approved qualified nutrient streams. This gives local cultivation/manufacture, not Maui-origin genetic stock or automatically Maui-origin feedstock.
2. **Maui-origin isolated yeast:** Mahalo Aleworks reports local wild-yeast capture/isolation and screening. An identified **Saccharomyces cerevisiae** candidate could in principle be engineered to produce myoglobin, but actual local isolate genotype, performance, food suitability, intellectual property, containment approval and GRAS/color-additive eligibility are **UNKNOWN**; it would be new strain R&D and not covered automatically by the earlier commercial K. phaffii processes.

Do **not** assume existing brewery yeast slurry, conventional ethanol yeast, wild yeast, expired brewer's yeast, or a kitchen yeast starter spontaneously produces myoglobin. Also do not assume any brewery tank may be shared with contained precision-fermentation yeast without hygiene, process and environmental approvals.

[Source-separated, owner-authorized food-processor byproducts] → independent specialized recovery of sugars / some qualified nutrients / aromatic oils / pectin / separate nonfood products → **certification of each relevant sugar, mineral and nitrogen fraction as food manufacturing inputs** (not a label granted by waste treatment alone) → dedicated contained recombinant-yeast fermentation on defined, monitored, validated food-grade media → harvested and purified myoglobin with verified heme occupancy, protein purity and process-residue QA → food-compliant ground-meat mycoprotein recipes with actual sensory and shelf life receipts.

## Product architecture
- Bulk protein: separate Quorn-type **Fusarium venenatum** process on specifically qualified nutrients, including nucleic-acid removal and protein QA.
- Savory aroma/color: separate **engineered K. phaffii** myoglobin process or a commercially sourced licensed ingredient for initial food tests, if acceptable under exact intended ground-meat use. A myoglobin ingredient is not the bulk-protein source.
- Venison variant: an **animal-free** venison analog with bovine myoglobin would be beef-heme savory imitation and **not authentic deer myoglobin**; expressing **axis deer** myoglobin is a distinct scientific and regulatory/IP undertaking with absolutely no validated Maui strain, pilot or permit. Actual ethically sourced locally harvested venison, if used, makes the resulting food **not animal-free**. No safety/novel food approvals inferred.
- Sweet flavors: Maui citrus, lilikoi, dragon fruit and cacao aroma/pectin research remain separate from the hemoprotein savory track. Heme's prooxidant/metallic off-notes could harm these profiles.

## Hawaiʻi import/legal gate
The Hawaiʻi Plant Industry Division states microorganism entry is conditioned on listed risk category, **advance import permits/authorizations and intended uses**; genetically engineered strains may require extra review. **An eligible FDA food ingredient does not automatically grant authorization to import live GM yeast into Hawaiʻi.** Contact the Hawaiʻi Department of Agriculture and Biosecurity Plant Quarantine Branch to check the exact organism/strain, containments and rules before obtaining a culture, and assess any federal GMO biomanufacturing obligations in consultation with specialists. https://dab.hawaii.gov/pi/pq/import-program/microorganisms-guidelines/ ; https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/hawaii/Haw-Code-R-SS-4-71A-29 .

Do not imply an existing license or market access to Motif's HEMAMI strain. Impossible Foods took over Motif's heme business through a 2024 settlement, so commercial strains/IP, permitted finished ingredient and upstream rights require actual contracting. **Availability UNKNOWN**.
