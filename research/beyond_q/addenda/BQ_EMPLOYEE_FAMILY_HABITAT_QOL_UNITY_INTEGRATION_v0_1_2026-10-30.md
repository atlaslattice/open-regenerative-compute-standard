# Atlas Lattice / Beyond Q — Employee & Family Habitat QOL Standards (Dongjiakou Unity Integration)
**v0.1 · 2026-10-30 · PROPOSED research policy, not implemented employment terms or a verified benefit budget.**
**Primary source continuity:** Dongjiakou *Unity Health QOL 8/8/8 Sound Mobility v0.9* and *Solbrain Unity Closeout v0.10*, 2026-10-10, both historical candidate designs held in author file Library. Preserve evidence boundary, five-day roster, shift recovery, qualified operator limits, individual rights, no double-counting, and actual measured resource/cash ledgers. Explicit supersession: prior **10 working days paid leave** becomes **20 working days (four weeks) annually** as Atlas proposed baseline, plus **5 paid designated holiday days** independent of the leave allowance; higher legal entitlements honored. Other statutory holidays/leave remain independent, with legal and local review. No existing employee entitlement is claimed.

## Employment and scheduling specification
- Five standard workdays/week; typical ~8 paid hours/day and protected 8/8/8 work / personal / sleep opportunity. Minimize chronic overtime by relief crews and cross-training, no suppression of lawful overtime pay. Protected breaks, no compulsory wellness attendance, right to disconnect.
- **20 annual working days paid vacation** (month in four five-day weeks); **5 designated paid holiday days**; plus applicable protected medical, parental, sick, bereavement and family-care leave. Clarify whether the five holidays supplement all legally applicable public holidays (assume they do in this proposed package). Offer contiguous 1–2 week vacation blocks and fair scheduling rotations; do not require all leave in December.
- Central **capacity-aware scheduling**: partner-specific rosters and certifications, staggered PTO and public holidays, minimum trained coverage, maximum simultaneous absence, swap rights and appeals. Holiday work compensated with **premium pay plus substitute paid leave**, exact premium and exempt/nonexempt status require policy/legal review. Avoid requiring workers to surrender protected holidays; publish fair rotation statistics.
- 24/7 shifts: one staffed position requires 8,760 coverage hours/year; at hypothetical 40h × (260 weekdays - 20 vacation - 5 designated holidays - 8 other absences - 5 training) = 1,776 available hours/person/year, **4.93 ideal full-time equivalents per continuously covered position before other holidays, breaks, handoffs, mandatory multi-person coverage, qualification scarcity and standby**. At least five whole persons is *arithmetic only*; robust staffing often requires more. No interchangeable qualifications assumed.
- 80% *worker* on-site residency target, voluntary; ~5–10 minute cycle trip or walk as an aspiration, measured by network routes. Family visitation, free ingress/egress, residential independence and right to leave. Reduced commute is household benefit; lower lateness or fatigue remain hypotheses until measured.
## Compensation, family, and housing
- Competitive cash wages, benefits and transparent total compensation; do not treat housing fair market value as automatic lawful wage payment or tax exemption. Prefer employer-funded, zero resident markup utilities and housing, no mandatory paycheck deductions. Operators recognize housing and benefits as employment costs and intercompany transfers eliminate on consolidation.
- Family-sized accessible 3D-printed and conventionally completed housing, secure tenure and neutral grievance route; housing loss not used to coerce workers.
- Family childcare, schools/education access, elder-care access, medical and mental-health care, pharmacy, dentistry, fitness and recreation are capacity-planned **essential community services** delivered by qualified third-party providers. Clinical information is never used for employment evaluation. Opt-in research is separate from entitlement to services.
- Community grooming: **voluntary** haircut/barber/hair care availability at 2–3-week intervals for those who want it, as a convenience/wellbeing service **not mandatory appearance standard** or presumed health intervention. Model actual service uptake and licensed professional staffing.
- Quality nutrition and food choice: good affordable everyday meals, fresh produce, culturally appropriate/dietary/allergy options, food safety QA, nutrient recovery only with lawful clean streams and validated separation, no imposed supplements.
## Jjimjilbang and wellbeing
- **Core proposed facility**: jjimjilbang-inspired communal recovery/social center with sauna, bathing, accessible quiet/rest areas, optional cooling, relaxation, private sanitation, resident programming and safe family arrangements; trained attendants, water treatment, chemical and infection controls, emergency support, fire safety and realistic operating costs. Independent utility and bathing water quality; never reuse untreated industrial streams for bathing. No guaranteed clinical effects.
- Also available: gyms, tai chi/other fitness, safe cycling, green spaces and gardens, music and cultural venues, films, social gathering, optional counseling and mental health providers with confidentiality, protected scheduling time for visits. Sounds/music voluntary, quiet housing and shift-worker daytime sleep protected; no unverified frequency-healing claims.
- Measure uptake, user satisfaction, service availability, queue times, cost, accessibility, sleep and fatigue only with lawful voluntary privacy-preserving protocols; no automatic health/productivity multipliers.
## Standardized digital equipment and privacy
- Provide **standardized capable phone for residents/workers, home laptop per eligible employee/household and appropriate dedicated work desktop/workstation**. Plan distinct cost, refresh cycle, loan/ownership, repair, battery, connectivity, support and family use; bulk procurement and interoperability benefits only if quantified after device-management/helpdesk/licensing costs.
- Privacy segregation: employer can manage **work-owned account and work workstation** for security, regulatory audit and job functions subject to documented notice and lawful limits. Personal phone and home laptop have private user profiles/data; **no blanket employer access** to off-duty communications, health, mental-health, family conversations, financial wallets or location. For BYOD/shared devices, enterprise containers and transparent, limited management controls; prohibit remote mic/camera collection except specific disclosed safety use with consent and legally justified process. Consent is revocable for optional research and monitoring; refusal doesn't affect housing, care or wage benefits. Worker-controlled personal AI memory, export/deletion and opt-out. Use anonymized/aggregated research only after actual reidentification-risk assessment; raw data is not placed on public blockchain.
- Internet access and basic digital-skills assistance without separate resident markups. Universal visitor/family access as applicable.
## 15-minute neighborhood and safety
- Walk/wheel accessible 5-minute greenspace, daily food basics, 10-minute childcare/clinic/pharmacy/gym, 15-minute grocery/recovery/cultural center/transport under true routes and service hours, buffered safely from industrial risk areas; safe bike/e-bike routes and parking, accessible shuttle for weather and disabilities. Freedom of movement retained; these are access tests not strict radius guarantees.
## Planning costs and evidence
For each of 5,000 hypothetical employees, model employer costs for leave replacement, holiday premiums, qualification/training time, cash compensation, payroll taxes, housing/upkeep, mobile/home/work devices and refresh cycles, connectivity, health coverage, mental-health access, grooming, fitness and jjimjilbang, childcare and family education. Keep employer-funded resident price at zero where specified **while retaining actual third-party service revenue and costs**. No free services, no phantom savings. Separately report resource electricity/water/thermal quality, wastewater and sanitation loads.
**KPIs**: leave actually taken; 24/7 qualified coverage; shifts >40h; holiday fairness; overtime costs; fatigue and self-reported satisfaction; recruitment/retention; employee household disposable income; transport time; service access; resident choice; privacy incidents; cost per resident and per employer; benefits disaggregated rather than summed.
