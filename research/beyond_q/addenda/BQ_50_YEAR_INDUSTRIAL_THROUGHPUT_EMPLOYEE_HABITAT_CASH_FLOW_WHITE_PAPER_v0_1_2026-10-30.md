# Atlas Lattice — Industrial Throughput, 50-Year Owner-Operator Economics and Resident Habitat
**2026-10-30 | White paper v0.1 | SCENARIO ONLY; NOT INVESTMENT GRADE**
**Author changes incorporated:** 80% of 5,000 campus employees residing on site by choice with families; employee housing, essential utilities, daycare/education and wellness at cost / employer-funded instead of profit centers; reduced commutes tested for retention and attendance; independent third-party JV operators; compute/fusion-isotopes, water, robotics, fabrication, food and services; USDC payment volume accounted **separately** from revenue and value added.

## Governing thesis
Atlas Lattice is the integrator and common protocol, not industrial owner/operator. A specialist owns and qualifies each subsystem. **20-year autonomy research objective; 50-year asset/community lifecycle.** No named party (Google, Microsoft, user-mentioned CLS, etc.) has agreed to participate. Existing conceptual Reactor B **1.13 GW D-T fusion power** and Reactor A **hypothetical 20 MW D-D primary fusion power** retain **zero commercial output credit** until the physics, conversion, breeding, product yields and economics survive independent tests. D-D/He-3 research remains central science, not demoted.

## Employee/family habitat as productive infrastructure
**Baseline:** 5,000 jobs, **80% workforce on-site resident by design target**, 4,000 resident workers, ~6,800 total residents including family under **hypothetical 1.7 residents per resident worker**, 1,000 commuter workers. Housing is a third-party owned and operated service **at cost with zero resident profit markup**; ideally direct employer-funded instead of payroll deductions. Separate housing cash losses or subsidy transfers explicitly in partner FCF and employee compensation/benefit ledger. Provision may be a taxable fringe benefit: IRS 2026 Publication 15-B https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15b outlines special exclusion only when business-premises, employer convenience and **condition of employment** tests apply; voluntary residency generally prevents blindly claiming the exclusion. **No promise of tax-free lodging.** Include local housing rights, nondiscrimination, family/school provision, independent lease terms, safety, privacy and ability to leave freely; 80% is a recruitment/occupancy design target, never compulsory confinement.
Consider family size, dual-worker households and unit types. Model childcare and educational access, grocery, health/wellness, food quality, recreation, sanitation and medical support based on genuine service capacity and recurring costs; private owner/operators may earn fairly compensated contract fees even when resident bill has no markup.
**Commute hypothesis:** default 35 minutes each way × 230 days × 4,000 resident workers = **1,073,333 hours/year** of household commuting time avoided relative to hypothetical off-campus commute, before accounting for local walking, alternative work patterns and travel elsewhere. This is **quality-of-life time, not automatically paid labor hours, company cash flow, or productivity growth**. Track attendance/punctuality, turnover, injury, fatigue, sick leave, morale, recruitment acceptance and worker choice with uncertainty and comparison cohorts. No preassigned dramatic decrease in tardiness; evaluate difference and confounders. Benefits cannot be booked without evidence.
Resale/value of voluntary housing cannot be conflated with employer benefit spending; a tenant should not lose essential housing solely because of a job dispute.
## 50-year and spatial scenarios
Module land footprint, coastal/inland siting, capital cost of printed homes vs traditional, local grid, water pipeline/transport, deuterium concentrate freight, reclaimed wastewater, climate hazards, cooling, reactor buffers and environmental safeguards remain open. **One-hour desal transport criterion is screening hypothesis, not engineering law**. At year 20 test achieved input autonomy; at years 20–50 explicitly replace plant, compute devices, pumps, 3D printer fleets, piping, housing HVAC, batteries, land reclamation and life-support assets. Price 50-year obligations in cash, not just annual average.
## Revenue, USDC and corporate partner economics
For each JV participant record physical sold quantity, USD contract price, internal/external buyer, external expenses, payroll, amortized/new capex, taxes and settlement currency. USDC is an optional programmable settlement rail. Annual **payment throughput can exceed external revenue multiple times** as payroll, retailers, transfers and repeated turnover of funds circulate. Circle or blockchain providers' gas/reserve-related returns are not campus output; no assumed stablecoin issuer partnership. Cost-plus and contingent royalties must identify eligible downstream economics; no duplicate counting from internal markup. Maintain privacy and correction-ready physical evidence receipts.
## Sensitivity priorities
Vary computed capacity, realized contracts, electricity $/kWh, PUE, human wage bills, home capex, rent/benefit policy, resident fraction 50–95%, local water rights, crop/food yield, fabrication scrap and qualification, maintenance intervals, power outages, discount rate and 50-year replacement factors. Generate stress cases with zero fusion success, expensive grid power, 50% unfilled housing, bad recruitment retention, failure of a desal supplier, and high childcare costs.
