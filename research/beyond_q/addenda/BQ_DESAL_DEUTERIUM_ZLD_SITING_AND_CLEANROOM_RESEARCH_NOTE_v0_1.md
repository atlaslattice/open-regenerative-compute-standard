# Beyond Q — Desalination, Deuterium, ZLD, Electrical Quality and Controlled Access
**2026-10-29 / v0.1 / proposed design and commercial hypothesis, NOT a validated process.**

## Concept
Co-locate the fusion/quantum campus near an existing desalination plant where a mutually agreed retrofit is feasible. Follow the Atlas Lattice Dongjiakou / Corpus Christi resource-capture approach: improved pretreatment, membrane recovery, modular high-value resource extraction, water and brine ledgers, and eventually site-appropriate minimal/zero liquid discharge (MLD/ZLD) technology.

**Proposed commercial structure:** Desalination operator gains a next-generation retrofit. Beyond Q receives contractual rights to qualified deuterium output, with financial sharing of other recovered products after transparent cost allocation. Rights must be specifically contracted; ordinary desalination has no inherently free deuterium coproduct. Compare make-or-buy deuterium economics before adding any revenue credit.

## Resource abundance and nuclear fuel comparison
UKAEA states about one D atom per 6,700 hydrogen atoms in seawater: https://www.ukaea.org/fusion-energy/fusion-energy-questions/ .
An estimated 1 million US gallons daily is 3785.4 m3/day water. Using ~111 kg H/m3 and D/H=1/6700 gives approximately 33 kg D/m3 or **~125 kg D/day**, ~45 tonnes/year *naturally present in incoming water*. **Not recoverable or qualified product yield**. (Correct units: this is **0.033 kg D/m3**, not 33 kg.) Molecular chemistry, isotope enrichment, process water recovery, downtime, solvent losses and costs must be modeled. Ordinary RO does not yield concentrated D as a material stream. D-T plasma burn demand may be only tens of kg D/year per GW-class thermal device at perfect single-pass burn; compare separation train scale and minimum economic module before any site claim.

## ZLD balance and environmental safeguards
- Treat desal water, salts, mineral products, solids, evaporation, concentrated brine and waste as distinct mass streams with impurity-specific qualification. Mineral recovery must not double-count chemically competing streams.
- Set ZLD as an **optional, costed** module until environmental, technical, permitting and financial gates pass; avoid claiming literal zero emissions/exhaust (gas venting, heat rejection, evaporation, solid residues and emergency releases remain).
- Dedicated ZLD CAPEX, OPEX, electrical MW and thermal MW at required temperature, waste-heat utilization credit (only where thermodynamically usable), contaminated waste, disposal and environmental impacts; no free thermal energy.
- Published models show substantial energy/cost variations: https://www.nature.com/articles/s44221-024-00327-1 ; https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0255270124001119 .
- Independent radiological containment: reactor tritium, activated material and possible toxic process wastes are NEVER assumed suitable for municipal potable water or commercial mineral recovery. Design physical segregation and monitored interfaces.
- Quantify marine ecology, coastal flooding, salt deposition, seismic and storm resilience in siting decisions. Water access is useful but not a hard coastal siting mandate.

## Electrical architecture — separate line item
- Compare conventional AC first, HVDC/MVDC intermediate and hybrid isolated power buses. Compute generator → transformer/rectifier → bus → DC/DC → low-noise rack/QPU rails with each conversion's heat and loss.
- **High-amp DC is not zero jitter**: specify load transient tolerance, current and voltage ripple/noise spectral density, EMI, magnetic fields, harmonics, power-quality reliability, protection and isolation. RF stability and optical/shot jitter are different physical metrics.
- ZLD, mineral recovery and isotope separation are additional process loads, not implicitly funded from promised D-D net generation.
- Safety-critical reactor loads remain independently supplied even if campus integration minimizes routine import.

## Containment and access interfaces
- Evaluate qualified personnel airlocks/vestibules with correct contamination-zone and ventilation/pressure design, access authorization, emergency egress, work permits, task-specific gloves, smocks/coveralls, contamination survey, dosimetry if required and protective equipment selected by qualified occupational/radiation protection teams.
- **Piezo-soaked/self-healing gel interlayer** is an experimental candidate for instrumented damage sensing, not a credited containment barrier. Test irradiation, tritium transport/retention, fires, cleaning chemistry, mechanical cycling, false negatives, longevity and common-cause failure. Retain primary and secondary independent qualified boundaries.

## Proposed financial terms and missing parameters
Evaluate four separable cash flow modules: base desal retrofit, D isotope separation, other brine resources, and ZLD. Distinguish gross avoided deuterium purchase, certified product volume, royalty/profit share, energy and supplies. No contingent module is presumed to generate free power or net profit. Add site's water/discharge permit baseline, measured inlet salinity/composition, throughput, water demand, electricity tariff, waste-heat temperature/time profiles, fuel off-take and commercial split as mandatory input fields.
