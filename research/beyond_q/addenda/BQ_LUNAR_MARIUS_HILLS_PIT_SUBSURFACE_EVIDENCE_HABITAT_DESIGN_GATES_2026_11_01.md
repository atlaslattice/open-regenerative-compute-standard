# Beyond Q / ORCS Moon–Mars Habitat — Marius Hills Pit: Measured Skylight vs Unconfirmed Lava-Tube Space
**Date 2026-11-01 | v0.1 | SITE EVIDENCE / DESIGN CONSTRAINTS; NOT A HABITABLE CAVERN SURVEY OR MISSION APPROVAL**

## Critical distinction

**Yes, subsurface scans exist**. SELENE/Kaguya 4–6 MHz lunar radar sounder, NASA GRAIL gravity data, LROC visible-stereo pit topography and published joint studies constrain **candidate voids** in the Marius Hills volcanic province. Orbital radar/gravity are inverse solutions with uncertain rock dielectric, void density and geometry; **no instrument has mapped a guaranteed safe accessible cavern floor, 3D room dimensions, roof bearing capacity or water ice at this particular pit**. NASA has selected **GIMLI** to investigate from the lunar surface; this is precisely why existing orbiter data do not suffice.

**Selection correction:** Official NASA release **September 30, 2026 (corrected Oct 2)**; GIMLI is **Geophysical Instruments for Marius Lunar pit Investigation**, not the previously relayed 'Geophysical Investigation of Marius Hills Lava Tube Interior'. GIMLI is a PRISM surface science payload planned to search for a tube by **ground-penetrating radar + active-source seismic + gravimeter + cameras**. It is *not yet a completed exploration or a verified cavern*. NASA emphasizes its current lunar south-pole Moon Base planning, **not a settled Marius Hills habitation plan**. Source: https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-adds-new-science-investigations-for-moon-base/ and PSI https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1146374

## 2. No automatic 50m→huge square footage implication

The pit is a **vertical skylight with an opening**, not a guaranteed below-ground hangar. To explain dimensions, a simple illustrative *surface aperture* elliptical geometry with **49–55m inner diameters** has approximately `π*(49/2)*(55/2) ≈ 2,117 m²` of horizontal **opening cross-section**, not verified usable floor. The **79–92m funnel** yields a larger *surface disturbance* ellipse `≈ 5,708 m²`, also **not habitable area**. The opening could lead to a collapsed chamber, large tunnel, no tube, or an inaccessible cavity blocked by debris. **Certified underground usable floor area remains 0 m² known** because roof clearance, surface morphology, void extent, boulders and load-bearing structure are not surveyed; this is a data-completeness classification, not proof that physical area is zero.

The 2024 **9km width × 60km length** inversion must NEVER be used for campus square footage, air volume, excavation or occupancy because it is not a direct measurement. Use an uncertainty lattice:
- **L0 OBSERVED SURFACE**: LROC coordinates, visible pit/overhang, stereo pit envelope.
- **L1 REMOTE-GEOPHYSICS INFERENCE**: candidate void presence/extent, unconstrained rock properties.
- **L2 IN-SITU SURVEY**: GIMLI ground radar/seismic/gravimetry, updated interpretations, structural roof integrity NOT yet certified.
- **L3 ROBOT ACCESS & HABITABILITY**: line-of-sight / entry ramp / void clearance, roof strength, rockfall risk, seals, radiation/thermal/water mapping, regolith handling and safe emergency egress.
- **L4 CREW HABITAT**: certified radiation, pressure-boundary/airlock, thermal loop, landing/exhaust plume, resupply, evacuation and occupational safety.

## 3. Program-site caution: ice at Marius Hills is not established

**Marius Hills is ~14°N, on the volcanic near side, not a permanently shadowed lunar south-polar cold trap.** The frequently proposed '**lava tube + ice deposit**' coupled business case conflates two types of potentially attractive lunar sites. Do not assume usable native ice is present in Marius Hills merely because some polar craters contain ice signatures. Treat local water extraction as **UNKNOWN**: require actual neutron spectroscopy/thermal/remote sensing and then in-situ composition, and maintain imported/recycled water scenario. **NASA's south-pole habitation goals and a speculative near-equatorial lava-tube settlement may be physically separate missions with transport costs**.

## 4. Minimal moon-habitat architecture integration

- **Route R0**: site robot traverses surface and pit rim only; no descent until demonstrated safe entry.
- **Route R1**: temporary, **self-contained pressurized modules** delivered into **already measured** shelter; tube rocks are shielding, not a pressure-sealed airtight vessel.
- **Route R2**: habitat scale to verified roof thickness, clearance and ground load capacity, not modeled 60km void.
- **Power**: compare surface solar + storage (shadowing and night period) vs approved advanced power sources by site; orbit/terrain lighting and dust must be measured. Do not state reactor net power without full mass/thermal/launch ledger.
- **Access**: 40–60m vertical elevation envelope drives elevator/crane/robotic winch design only as first CAD sensitivity; final placement and clearance follow GIMLI/direct mapping. Large 79–92m funnel may complicate landing separation and roof stability.
- **Water**: remote ice is a separate **transport/resupply** scenario; onsite closed-loop wastewater recovery remains necessary regardless of source.
- **Conservation**: Moon/Mars analog mass-energy/maintenance/comms log distinct from Earth Beyond Q costs. Synthetic geometry cannot be 'measured habitable sqm'.
