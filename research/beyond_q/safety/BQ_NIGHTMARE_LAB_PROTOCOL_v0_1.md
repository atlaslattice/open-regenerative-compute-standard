# Nightmare Lab v0.1 — Scenario Governance and Human Protection
**Atlas Lattice | proposed policy, not operational authorization | 2026-10-30**

## Mission
**If you can imagine how it breaks, we want to know before reality tries it.** Prevent severe industrial incidents through continuous adversarial, human-centered, evidence-graded scenario testing.

## Submissions
Anyone, including contractors/housekeeping and new staff, may submit anonymously or identified. Accept frontline observations without presumption of blame. Reporting interface should offer protected channels beyond direct managers, emergency-stop/escalation guidance, and an option for confidential worker advocates. Never use anonymous reports as evidence of individual misconduct without independent fair procedure.

## Scenario pipeline
Submission → de-identified scenario → physical-boundary/hazard definition → independent physics or organizational simulation → adversarial variants → safety response → expert critique → independent review → PASS/FAIL/UNRESOLVED → assigned corrective action → blind retest → signed versioned record. Preserve dissent and failures. No autonomous sabotage or real facility control from test agents.

## Human and organizational test families
Fatigue, unsafe scheduling, ambiguous responsibilities, shift handoffs, exclusion of junior technical objections, production pressure, management retaliation, understaffing, housekeeping access, emergency egress, ambiguous contamination zoning, PPE/doffing, alarm fatigue, conflicting sensor reports, cyber operator errors and compound environmental/technical incidents.

## Human digital twin privacy
Use workforce-level role/schedule/procedural scenarios, **not intimate personal profiles, individual risk scores, psychiatric inference, covert surveillance, or automated discipline**. Minimal data; purpose limitation; access control; finite retention; worker representation; documented appeal and independent oversight.

## Accept/reject
Safety has non-negotiable human/public/environment thresholds. Equipment uptime or finance cannot offset a safety failure. A result is *UNRESOLVED* if validation data are absent. Named humans and personnel disputes require separate due process.

## Audit fields
scenario_id; submitted_at; reporter_confidentiality; affected_safety_boundaries; assumptions; simulator_version; adversarial_generator_version; independent_reviewer; severity; safety_gate_status; corrective_action; retest_receipt; next_review; privacy_review. Real hazard response always follows qualified facility procedures and applicable regulations.
