# BQ Safety Design Constraints v0.1 — Design-stage checklist
- Design objectives only, not an approved safety case; IAEA/qualified nuclear, radiation-protection and occupational safety teams must evaluate.
- Independent confinement, monitored interspaces, licensed tritium inventory, waste tracking, emergency egress and abnormal release assessment.
- Personnel entry/exit: hazard-specific zone segregation, assessed vestibule/airlocks, worker accountability, emergency escape, suitable coveralls/smocks/gloves **only where risk assessment warrants**, dosimetry and contamination surveys. Cleanroom clothing is not radiation shielding or general tritium protection.
- Piezo sensor plus candidate self-healing gel interlayer is **unvalidated**; no safety credit until radiation/chemical/thermal/fire/aging/false-negative and common-cause test evidence.
- Protect worker stop-work rights, fatigue management, privacy of digital twins, anti-retaliation escalation, informed human decision rights.
- Full traceable safety barrier failure chain: detect → localize → isolate → contain → remediate → investigate → change → blind retest → independent review.
- No guarantee of absolute zero emissions; specify measured discharge criteria, detection limits, approved disposal and verified environmental monitoring.
