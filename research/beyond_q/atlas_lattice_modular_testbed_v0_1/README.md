# Atlas Lattice Modular Testbed v0.1 — JV Systems Integration
**2026-10-30 | PROPOSED, hypothetical architecture and computational test cases. No executed JV or operating facility.**
Atlas Lattice is the **neutral systems integrator, ledger steward, research coordinator and independent integration evaluator** of a multi-party circular-economy campus. Specialist owner-operators retain independent technical and safety responsibility, assets and commercial interests. A JV is a **candidate governance model**, not a current legal entity or contractual agreement.

## Hypothetical participants and roles
- **Fusion / isotopes and compute:** proposed third-party owner/operator consortium. Google, Microsoft and 'CLS' (as author phrased it; **identity unconfirmed; possible intended CFS**) are *illustrative counterparties only*, not endorsers, members, operators or confirmed partners. Reactor B **1.13 GW D-T fusion power** and Reactor A **hypothetical 20 MW primary D-D reference** are unvalidated modeling premises. D-D / He-3 and tritium research remains a **core scientific pillar**, not optional or subordinated to robotics.
- **Quantum/AI:** proposed third-party compute operator; architecture agnostic; classical AI training assumed viable research baseline. Quantum advantage for robotic training **not demonstrated**.
- **Water / desal / brine recovery:** specialist provider owns plant, water compliance and product quality; cost allocation to receiving partners based on traceable consumption and offtake. Model rights, feedwater, produced brine, contamination and losses explicitly.
- **Robotics:** third-party hardware vendor/owner/operator owns platforms, safety qualification and maintenance; Atlas Lattice defines common data schemas and integration tests.
- **Additive and conventional manufacturing:** third-party fabricator provides machining, printing, tools, spare parts, inspection/qualification with an independent supply chain.
- **Food / nutrients / agriculture:** third-party operators with independent food, biosafety and agricultural qualification and traceable nutrient/water/heat exchanges.
- **Nightmare Lab:** independent cross-partner adversarial simulation and review, human-controlled and forbidden from self-certifying safety.
- **Atlas Lattice:** JV interface specification; resource exchange clearinghouse; provenance; research/habitat analog accounting; independent disputes and integration audit, not a fantasy of one company designing all machines.

## Three nested modeling boundaries
1. **Module**: individually testable physics/input/output/risk assumptions.
2. **Campus JV**: partner-to-partner mass, energy, material, information, financial and rights transfers, no duplication of benefit.
3. **Earth/Moon/Mars comparison**: actual terrestrial economics vs explicitly scenario-modeled extraterrestrial delivered and risk-adjusted economics (not imaginary Earth revenues).

## Independent modules and coupling
All modules run on declared interfaces, including imported fallback cases; no module inherits physics validation from another. Execute coupled scenarios only after validating transfer equations and mass/energy conservation. An unproven fusion result must not become a hidden precondition for the robotics/water experiment. Conversely the water first testbed **does not demote the fusion/isotope agenda**.

## First reproducible computational reference scenario
See [reference module](water_robotics_reference_v0_1.py) and [test cases](test_water_robotics_reference_v0_1.py).
