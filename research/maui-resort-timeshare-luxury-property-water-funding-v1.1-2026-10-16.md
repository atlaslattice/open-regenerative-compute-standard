## Two DISTINCT interpretations of “10% timeshare tax”
**A. 10% additional ANNUAL property-value levy** on FY2027 timeshare-class net assessed value $4.224244B = **$422.424M/year gross** *in addition to the existing ~$62.94M tax*, a proposed effective total recurring property tax ~**11.49% assessed value/year** ($114.90/$1,000), ignoring response/exemptions. It is >6.7× existing annual timeshare property-tax receipts as an increment alone; practical incidence/foreclosure/declining assessment/constitutional and economic limits make gross static extrapolation highly unreliable. No characterization as a modest fee.
**B. 10% additional charge on taxable occupied-night timeshare fair rental value or gross bookings**. The **taxable base is NOT $4.224B asset value**. Annual charge=10%×actual annual timeshare fair-rental value, which must be obtained from State/County TAT category-specific aggregates, with privacy restrictions. In the illustration, timeshare occupied-night revenue assumed to be 10/15/20% of hypothetical tourist lodging spend, resulting in much smaller incremental receipts. Existing 3% County TAT still applies; if both new 7% lodging + new 10% timeshare overlay apply, timeshares pay BOTH increments on the same stays, requiring explicit combined rate display and legal review.
**C. A 10% TOTAL County transient lodging/timeshare rate rather than +10 percentage points:** current county rate already 3%, so the net *increment* is at most **+7 percentage points**, not +10, on eligible timeshare rental base. Document tax policy precisely before financial estimates.
