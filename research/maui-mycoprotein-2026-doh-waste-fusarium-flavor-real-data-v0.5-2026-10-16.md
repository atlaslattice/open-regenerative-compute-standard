## 1. Principal discovery: NEW Hawaiʻi DOH 2026 waste study
Published September 29, 2026, 134-page Tetra Tech report with hand-sorted **March–July 2026** representative samples at Central Maui Landfill (CML). Public page: https://health.hawaii.gov/shwb/solid-waste/planning/statewide-waste-characterization-study/ ; direct study: https://health.hawaii.gov/shwb/files/2026/10/Hawaii-Statewide-WCS-Final-Report_092926.pdf .

**Direct administrative metric, report Table 1-1 printed p.3:** Central Maui Landfill **295,646 US tons disposed FY2025**; separately Hana 1,099, Molokaʻi 3,600, Lānaʻi 5,387; do not add other island disposal to Maui Island volumes and don't assume it is all pristine, representative MSW.

**Direct 2026 hand-sorted composition, report Figure 5-18 printed p.53:** sampled overall Maui waste **9.12% FOOD**; **17.13% OTHER ORGANICS**, separate category; other sampled classes include paper 18.90%, plastics 13.05%, metal 7.19%, inerts/C&D 13.81%. Food + other organics = **26.25%** sampled composition, NOT 26.25% suitable for food-grade bioreactors.

**Derived arithmetic—not directly measured annual food receipts:** 295,646 short tons (FY2025) × 9.12% (2026 sample) = **26,963 short tons annual-equivalent FOOD category**. 295,646 ×17.13%= **50,644 short tons annual-equivalent OTHER ORGANICS**. Differing time bases, varying load profiles, presence/extent of exceptional construction or 2023 wildfire-debris-related loads in FY2025 **UNKNOWN**, sampling uncertainty, and actual 2026 waste volumes make these *cross-year screens*, NOT actual FY2026 weighed food-tonnage or recoverable food-ingredient quantities. Sampled 9.12% is a composition estimate, not a waste-to-edible-fungal-nutrient conversion factor. Site study classification and composite scope must be reconciled before facility design.

**Older comparison:** Maui Council Resolution 24-118 cited ~41,000 US tons food/32,000 green waste disposed in **2019**, itself a model/report estimate derived from older composition. Two values are not time-harmonized: neither shows a verified 34% decline. 2024 council minutes: https://www.mauicounty.gov/Archive.asp?ADID=32062 ; county's 2012 original study: https://health.hawaii.gov/shwb/files/2020/01/Maui-Waste-Composition-Final-Report-041112_1.pdf .

**Operating diversion:** West Maui Green Cycle reports **92,633.14 pounds** food scraps diverted from **five schools in the 2024–25 school year to the reporting point** (~46.32 short tons by arithmetic). This is valuable source-specific data and already compost-allocated material; do not presume it is available for a competing food fermenter. https://westmauigreencycle.com/school-programs . Hawaiʻi Organics Compost explicitly accepts **no food waste or biosolids** and reported **10,416 US tons cumulative GREEN waste diverted September 2025–May 2026**, also already serving soil restoration: https://mauinow.com/2026/05/06/hawai%CA%BBi-organics-compost-now-selling-locally-made-soil-products/ .

**Archive-only production precedent:** 2002 Hawaiʻi Natural Energy Institute biomass assessment described ~**15,000 wet US tons/year** Maui pineapple skins/dewatered byproducts at 50% moisture, **~7,500 dry US tons**, historically used as cattle feed. This is **24-year-old activity**, from a much larger pineapple era: **not current supply**, not spare material, and cannot be combined with modern Maui Gold output. Historical original https://energy.hawaii.gov/wp-content/uploads/2011/10/Biomass-and-Bioenergy-Resource-Assessment-for-the-State-of-Hawaii.pdf (December 2002; pineapple subsection and Abstract).

## 2. Dongjiakou/Quorn strain and defined-medium evidence update
**Industrial benchmark:** `Fusarium venenatum A3/5`, Quorn-style aerated fermentation using specified high-grade sugars and defined nitrogen/minerals. TB01 only after separate strain/permission/assay checks, `F. compactum` MM-135 separate Chinese notice comparator, `Neurospora intermedia` separate food byproduct experimental strain that cannot inherit Quorn permission. China NHC 2025 interpretation https://www.nhc.gov.cn/sps/c100087/202511/b4a9a5f201644626b1c375d10fcadbe5.shtml ; FDA GRN91 https://www.hfpappexternal.fda.gov/scripts/fdcc/index.cfm?id=091&set=GRASNotices ; Quorn https://www.quornnutrition.com/mycoprotein/what-is-mycoprotein . FDA specified uses and strain details apply to given notifications; no blanket new production authorization.

**Major primary 2026 measured result for A3/5:** Banks et al., *Carbon Capture Science & Technology* vol.20, 100666, published **2026**, FAO AGRIS index Sept 30, 2026. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2772656826000990 ; https://agris.fao.org/search/en/providers/122436/records/6abd1d31cc47f98724260e71 :
- **Glucose:** uptake rate **0.538 g/L/h**, apparent dry biomass yield **0.15 g biomass/g substrate**, max specific growth **0.13 h⁻¹**, ethanol byproduct **4.64±0.49 g/L** in its scale-down test.
- **Sucrose:** growth **0.16 h⁻¹**, ethanol **6.62±0.79 g/L**.
- **Fructose:** apparent yield **0.49 g/g** but slower uptake **0.186 g/L/h** and long progression, T90 **173.6 h**. This does NOT prove fructose is more cost-effective at commercial scale.
- **Galactose:** **0.18 g/g** yield. Lactose: only **34%** substrate consumed over **234.5 h** in its tested regime.
- **Expired functional drink:** maximum biomass **18.37±0.64 g/L**, versus compositionally matched synthetic control **12.49±1.21 g/L**; specific growth rates **0.33 h⁻¹** vs **0.10 h⁻¹**. Culture matrix and experimental design matter; this is **not** a food-grade Maui medium nor a gross protein claim.
- Dual sugar growth was nonadditive due to sequential/diauxic metabolism. **Do not replace detailed fractions with a single generic Maui fruit-waste substrate yield.**

A peer-reviewed **2025–2026 techno-economic review** highlights substantial process costs for benchmark F. venenatum and variability among carbon sources: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11947-025-04202-z . External per-kg protein model costs cannot be interpreted as Maui quotes; reactor O2 transfer, sterilization, downstream RNA reduction, protein extraction/drying, transport, utilities and local labor are major missing terms.

**Crucial process separation per user instruction:**
(1) Municipal/industrial waste -> independent specialized recovery streams with nonfood destinations, separate contaminated residuals;
(2) *eligible, source-traceable food production side streams only* -> real assays and qualified sugar extraction/purification;
(3) each recovered carbohydrate, food-grade ammonium/nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium, magnesium and micronutrient fraction gets **separate human-food process qualification** (chemical residues, microbes, pesticides/mycotoxins, trace metals and toxics);
(4) dedicated compliant food-grade F. venenatum A3/5 fermentation on **defined medium**; source must still comply with exact legal production/intended-use requirements;
(5) independent biomass cell recovery, RNA reduction, allergen/mycotoxin/speciation, protein amino-acid and stable ingredient assays;
(6) food-grade flavor and lipid/pectin/texture cascade, packaging and preserved-shelf-life testing.
The existence of nonfood chemical recovery from sewage/brine does **not** imply recovered nitrogen/minerals qualify for food. No animal/feed/soil streams commandeered without diversion rights and lifecycle benefit.

## 3. Terpenes and palatability FIRST
**Actual Maui crops** per Mahi Pono 2026 operator include lemons, limes, oranges, tangerines, **lilikoi**, banana, coconut and others on >14k acres planted, with >3 million trees: https://www.mahipono.com/facts-and-figures . This establishes *species*, not citrus processing volume or peel waste. **Maui Gold** 2025 report averaged ~50 tons **whole pineapple per week**; peel/core juice-plant allocation unknown. https://mauinow.com/2025/10/22/maui-gold-pineapple-wants-to-ramp-up-to-full-production-but-deer-and-drought-stand-in-the-way/ . Maui Kuʻia has 6–7k cacao trees/20 acres but acknowledges much of its flavor chocolate cacao originates in **Ecuador**, so do not label cacao powder Maui-sourced without lot traceability: https://mauichocolate.com/pages/cacao-origins .

**Real primary extraction results, NOT local yields:**
- Vasco-Correa & Zapata Zapata, 2017 *LWT*: **17–26 g galacturonic-acid-equivalent pectin/100 g DRY passionfruit peel** using enzymatic extraction, with successful **7-L bench scale**, reported 85% galacturonic acid and 68% degree of esterification. This is **not** kg pectin per kg *wet Maui lilikoi*. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lwt.2017.02.024 .
- *Foods* 2022 extraction comparison gives **5.3%, 6.5%, 9.9%, 10.7%** pectin yields in different process conditions, with varying emulsion stability and thermal sensitivity. These different analytical bases/processes cannot be blended as one mass yield: https://doi.org/10.3390/foods11243995 .

**Terpene fractionation** must quantify limonene, linalool, citral, geraniol as appropriate **food aromas**, including actual identity/GC-MS and supplier certificates. Passionfruit/pineapple *fruit character* also needs ester, acid and sweetness balancing. These compounds do NOT supply a supported physical/cognitive enhancement dose. Do not use fragrance-grade essential oils in food. Terpenes are not a universal fix for chalky fungal fiber, bitter essential amino acids, metallic mineral premix or fat instability.

**Human-food product development first:** UH Maui College **Maui Food Innovation Center** has a state-funded ~4,000 ft² Pilina facility, equipment reported 35/65-gallon mixing kettles, bottling/labels and freeze dehydration; no primary-source evidence of approved live fungal fermentation equipment. Source https://maui.hawaii.edu/foodinnovation/about and https://www.hawaii.edu/news/2023/11/07/food-innovation-center-maui/ . Use for actual formulation/sensory QA pending permission; partner microbiological or sterile fermentation capability is **UNKNOWN**.

**Optional Impossible Foods comparator:** Soy leghemoglobin via specified genetically engineered yeast process is an *optional savory* taste/iron/color R&D tool, not a bulk protein supplement, not synthetic human blood/hemoglobin, and not clearly sensible for sweet citrus Unity Gel. FDA GRNs and 2019 color rule cover exact uses only; do not infer ordinary liposomal gel clearance. Compare no-heme, mushroom umami, and authorized sourced heme in a distinct savory line, not in baseline fruity gel. https://www.hfpappexternal.fda.gov/scripts/fdcc/index.cfm?id=1202&set=GRASNotices .
