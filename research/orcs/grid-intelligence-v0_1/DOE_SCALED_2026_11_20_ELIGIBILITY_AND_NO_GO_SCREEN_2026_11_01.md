1. Identify domestic transformer OEM/qualified ceramic insulator/electrical steel/CTC winding maker or actual grid refurbishment shop with **TRL6+ component or TRL8+ refurbishment** and capacity.

Optional DOE **Teaming Partner List** via eXCHANGE (NOFO Part 1 §I.C) requires public investigator/org details, contact information, capabilities; DOE expressly disclaims endorsement. **Only list with user approval and accurate registered entity details**, do not automatically post personal contact data.

**Route B — Preserve AHD-D1 as precommercial material-science research:** study transparent photoreactive resin/ceramic positioning, life tests and published errors independently; seek differently scoped early-stage R&D programs when opened (SBIR/STTR or university lab collaboration subject to program-specific rules), not this TRL6+ industrial demonstration solicitation. No guarantee of financing or registration.

## 4. No hidden barriers, no misleading assumptions

The **50%** match is the principal economic constraint, but it is based on *eligible whole-project costs*, and approved third-party/in-kind contributions may sometimes qualify; it is not necessarily 50% personal cash up front. A professional proposal also requires legal financial/safety infrastructure, accepted supplier contracts, actual customer need, a labor and facility plan, domestic-sourcing calculations, formal test plan and likely audit obligations. This validates the experience that such federal invitations are administratively difficult, while still distinguishing **individual credential** requirements from **organization and manufacturing readiness** requirements.

**Truthfulness gate for public communication:** "DOE announced an opportunity relevant to domestic grid components" is verified. "ORCS qualifies," "DOE endorsed AHD-D1," "a two-page proposal is enough," "Pulsar's isotope plant powers fusion," or "an independent individual can claim an award" are unsupported.
