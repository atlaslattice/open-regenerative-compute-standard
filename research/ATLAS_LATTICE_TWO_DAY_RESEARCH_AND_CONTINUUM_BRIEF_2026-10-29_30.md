## Twenty-year Beyond Q campus redesign: proposed testable objective
Dual-purpose terrestrial demonstration and lunar/Martian habitat analog; track separate *commercial margin* and *research value* (no double-counting). Aim aspirational 100% local supply across power, water, food/crops/flowers, air/gases, fertilizer, soap, maintenance, additive manufacturing and selected chemicals, while transparently counting imported isotope, microelectronics, trace minerals, feedstocks and repairs. Recycle and recover waste, reduce imports, retain safety relief, then distribute only **verified surplus after reserves**.
A generic warm-water stream does not automatically heat a fermenter: cultures generate metabolic heat and often need cooling. Recovered fluorine from PG chemistry may lead to qualified LiF via several gates, not directly to reactor-grade FLiBe; isotopics, BeF2 and hazards remain.
Nightmare Lab must adversarially test both **physics** and organizational assumptions, overpressure, pollution, model drift, contamination, loss of cooling, failures under isolation and degraded sensor inputs.
