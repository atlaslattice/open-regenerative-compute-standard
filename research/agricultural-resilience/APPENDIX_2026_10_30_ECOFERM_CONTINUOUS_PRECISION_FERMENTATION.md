# Nutrition resilience — ECOFERM continuous precision fermentation
**Consortium announcement, not industrial productivity proof.**
NIZO announced ECOFERM Sept 30 2026, a Dutch public-private effort with DAB.bio, Vivici, dsm-firmenich and university partners, researching **continuous fermentation with in-situ biomass retention and controlled cell agglomeration** for precision-fermented proteins (e.g. beta-lactoglobulin). Source: https://www.nizo.com/news/ecoferm-fermentation-alternative-proteins/ .
**Research questions:** Compare batch/fed-batch/continuous biomass retention; feedstock carbon, nitrogen and minerals, nutrient conversion yield, product titer, volumetric productivity, contamination and genetic stability, extraction, CIP/SIP downtime, energy/water, cleaning waste, capex and unit economics. This is a protein production pathway, **not by itself nutritionally complete food** or an end to food insecurity.
