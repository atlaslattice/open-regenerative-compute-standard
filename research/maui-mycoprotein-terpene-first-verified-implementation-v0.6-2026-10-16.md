## Executive conclusion
The current data justify a **data acquisition and laboratory feasibility phase**, not an industrial edible bioreactor purchase. The leading process benchmark is **Quorn-type Fusarium venenatum A3/5 on carefully controlled food-grade defined media**, using only resource-recovery fractions that receive independent food safety, chemical and regulatory qualification. For Unity Gel, **actual Maui citrus/lilikoi/pineapple terpene-rich flavor fractions and pectin texture** are the first sensory focus; an Impossible-style yeast-produced soy leghemoglobin ingredient belongs only in an optional separately reviewed savory branch.

**Never directly feed mixed postconsumer waste, sewage, biosolids or industrial mineral concentrate to an edible fungal cultivation process.** The priority is **waste-to-qualified-resource -> defined fungal nutrient media**, with independent quality assurance at every stage. Contaminated residuals and nonfood streams must have safe disposal or alternate recoveries.

## Facility sequence based on evidence
**A. Recover already available material without stealing food from people.** Maui Gold's on-island cutting/juice line is a more plausible place to weigh actual trim and wash residues than Mahi Pono's whole-fruit lime export stream. Mahi Pono reject limes may be available, **quantity UNKNOWN**. A value-added company such as **Pau Hana Co.** reports Maui-grown citrus in mixers; verify **where juice is actually squeezed and packaged** before attributing peel to Maui. Existing cattle-feed, donation and compost uses have first-class accounting and need voluntary consent.

**B. Aroma FIRST.** Request food-qualified orange/lemon/lime oils; analyze GC-MS d-limonene, linalool, citral, trace aldehydes/esters; evaluate passionfruit pectin and flavor aroma with actual qualified residues and no invented yields. Compare against purchased legal food-flavor controls. Citrus oil alone does not recreate the fruity character; esters and acid/sweetness matter. Texture and mineral bitterness will be measured at the full finished-gel composition.

**C. Purified carbohydrate and nutrients.** Profile Brix, glucose/fructose/sucrose, starch, microbes, pesticide and heavy-metal residues. Extract and separately qualify each *food-grade* sugar/salt; no claim that any industrial-brine N/P/K is compliant. No mixed waste broth enters food fermenters.

**D. Protein**: F. venenatum A3/5 is the industrial Quorn benchmark. A 2026 primary external study found 0.15 g/g glucose and 0.49 g/g fructose apparent biomass yield in very different kinetics (slow fructose utilization), and 18.37±0.64 g/L biomass on expired beverage vs 12.49±1.21 g/L matched control. These are external **experiment-specific** observations and cannot be transferred to Maui peel kg or future food-grade operator costs. Paper: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2772656826000990 . Chinese TB01 and other strains must receive separately scoped US food review; don't equate regulation by species alone.

**E. Finished nutrition + certification**: after lawful food-safe batches exist, evaluate actual protein fraction, essential amino acids, residual RNA and tolerability, flavor liking, nutrient retention, liposome survival if used, shelf life and packaging. Complete local-origin percent of Unity Gel remains UNKNOWN. Keep two-pouch meal distinct from optional supplement or full-day emergency ration.
