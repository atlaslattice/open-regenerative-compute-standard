# Hawaiʻi Mobile Resource Recovery — Supplier and Operator RFI

October 23, 2026 | Public draft; not sent. No order, funding commitment or county sponsorship.

Route questions 1–3 to equipment suppliers, question 4 to H-POWER/its operator, and question 5 to rental or operating-service providers. No recipient must answer outside its remit.

**Subject: Request for information — mobile recovery equipment and excavated-waste acceptance, Hawaiʻi**

We are assessing commercially available mobile equipment for a Hawaiʻi material-recovery campaign. The initial scope is characterized aged municipal waste, with clean construction material assessed separately. Contained wildfire debris is excluded. We seek written technical and commercial information to compare purchase, rental and contracted operation. No award or funding is committed.

1. **Model and sustained performance.** Please identify the exact offered model and reconcile the 30–150, 200 and over-600 t/h claims associated with DS260/DS15SE and Dongguan references. For each, state metric tonnes versus U.S. tons or cubic metres, feed type, moisture, bulk density, screen aperture, input size, sustained versus peak rate, operating duration and included processing stages. Supply measured power/fuel demand and minimum practical feed rate. Distinguish screen throughput from complete-line throughput and accepted-product yield.
2. **Reference and acceptance trial.** Please provide the named Dongguan client, campaign dates, operating hours, weighed input/output mass balance, product purity, residual fraction, availability and maintenance records, plus a reference contact with permission. Propose a representative-feed witnessed trial and contractual acceptance criteria, including a remedy if the delivered system fails them.
3. **Factory-integrated Hawaiʻi package and price.** Please quote the complete configuration and exclusions, including a compatible 60 Hz supply package (voltage, phase, motor ratings, starting demand, drives/VFDs and auxiliary loads), anti-clogging options, guards, dust controls, corrosion protection and controls documentation. Identify included magnets, nonferrous separation and conveyors. State packed weights/dimensions, freight terms to the selected island, all separately quoted charges, commissioning, training, lead time, spare-parts package, warranty, support and expected wear-part costs. Explain the start/end conditions, crew, utilities and lifting requirements of the 48-hour deployment claim. Distinguish factory-validated options from buyer-installed modifications.
4. **Excavated combustible-material acceptance.** Would H-POWER consider a characterized trial batch of excavated landfill combustibles? Please supply written limits and test methods for calorific value, moisture, chlorine/halogens, ash/inerts, metals, particle size and prohibited materials; preprocessing, sampling and delivery requirements; trial quantity and approval pathway; available acceptance capacity; and whether the commercial arrangement would involve a gate fee, zero-price acceptance or payment. We assume no RDF sale revenue pending agreement.
5. **Comparable rental or operating-service offer.** Please quote the same processing scope for a short trial and a 12-month campaign, separating equipment rent, operators, mobilization/demobilization, island freight, insurance, fuel/power, service, wear, allowed hours, additional shift rates, supporting machinery and residual handling. State availability, purchase option, off-hire terms and total exclusions. Give priced throughput scenarios rather than applying the earlier 20-tonne/day target as a production constraint.

Please distinguish guarantees, reference measurements and estimates, and include quote validity and supporting documents. Written responses will inform a transparent comparison of delivered cost, accepted recovery, residual disposal and net airspace recovered.


Please identify the standard base machine and every factory-integrated custom option. Application-specific configuration is welcome; provide validation, support responsibility, price and schedule for the exact offered configuration.
