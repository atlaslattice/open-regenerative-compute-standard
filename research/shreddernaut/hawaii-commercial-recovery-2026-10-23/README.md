# Shreddernaut Hawaiʻi: Commercial Mobile Recovery and Land Preservation

**The proposed next step is to deploy and characterize existing commercial equipment, rather than manufacture a custom 20-tonne/day prototype.** This research package seeks technical review, supplier information, potential host-site collaboration and lawful downstream material outlets. It does not announce an operating project, funded purchase, county partnership or verified commercial return.

## Why evaluate this now?

Additional landfill capacity consumes land and requires acquisition, engineering, construction, closure and long-term care. A resource-recovery campaign deserves comparison against those full costs, including land preservation, accepted material recovery, residual disposal and verified net airspace recovered. It need not rely solely on metal-sale revenue to have public value. Whether recovery avoids a particular landfill expansion or acquisition remains to be demonstrated.

On Oʻahu, Honolulu's [published landfill status](https://www.honolulu.gov/env/ref/res-landfill-status/) distinguishes physical capacity from the March 2, 2028 closure order. Recovering airspace does not automatically authorize continued disposal beyond that deadline. [Honolulu's siting material](https://www.honolulu.gov/env/ref/new-landfill-siting/) describes roughly 90,000 tons/year of MSW and special waste plus 165,000 tons/year of H-POWER ash/residue; these annual flows are not a measured legacy stock inventory.

For Maui, the county reports [completion of Olowalu debris hauling and restoration in December 2025](https://www.mauicounty.gov/m/newsflash/home/detail/18111). Contained wildfire debris is excluded from this proposed municipal-waste campaign. Clean construction material, incoming waste and aged municipal waste require separate characterization and operating cases.

## Commercial equipment is the preferred direction

A Desen mobile screening package reported by DeepSeek at approximately **US$300,000** is a leading purchase candidate. This is an **attributed, unverified price lead**, not an authenticated Hawaiʻi-delivered quotation. Public Desen material advertises [DS15SE relocation/deployment within 48 hours](https://www.zzdshj.com/xingyezixun/101428.html); this does not establish international shipping or complete site-startup time. That page also claims over 600 t/h, inconsistent with other 30–150 and 200 t/h figures supplied in the research discussion. None is adopted as demonstrated Hawaiʻi whole-line throughput.

The user relays DeepSeek's statement that each Desen deployment is customized to its environment rather than sold as a single stock configuration. **This configuration-policy claim is unverified.** Named base models and factory-integrated site packages can coexist. We welcome a supplier-defined package integrating electrical compatibility, screening options, controls, containment and service, with written pricing and performance responsibilities.

**Our working procurement preference is purchase if validated**, with existing-island contractors and rental offers retained as comparable alternatives. The project does not need to invent a trommel or operate every stage inside a freight container. Shippable skids, trailers, mobile machinery and containerized controls are all acceptable. The stronger economic/product claim—cheaper and more effective than alternatives—requires equivalent quotations and actual performance evidence.

## Initial process boundary

Weigh and characterize batches; inspect and recover whole items; isolate hazards; screen controlled feed; recover ferrous material; add nonferrous separation where justified; grade and ship accepted products; lawfully manage residuals. Bulk shredding, plastic washing/drying/pelletizing and onsite refining are excluded from the initial base scope. Add selective size reduction only where required by a demonstrated output specification.

No agricultural use of recovered fines or positive RDF sales price is assumed. H-POWER is a potential technical discussion, not a committed paying outlet for excavated fuel. A screen alone is not a complete metal-recovery or decontamination line.

## Capacity scenarios, not equipment promises

Assume 300 days/year and 80% availability, applied once. Rates are metric tonnes/hour during available operating time.

| Assumed sustained rate | One 8-hour shift: tonnes/year | Two 8-hour shifts: tonnes/year |
|---|---:|---:|
| 30 t/h | 57,600 | 115,200 |
| 75 t/h | 144,000 | 288,000 |
| 150 t/h | 288,000 | 576,000 |
| 200 t/h | 384,000 | 768,000 |

An 11-million-metric-tonne legacy inventory is retained **only as a sensitivity case**. Four hypothetical 200-t/h units on two shifts would screen 3.072 million tonnes/year, processing that assumed quantity in about 3.6 years before ramp-up, excavation and downstream constraints. Screening all feed is not complete environmental remediation. Do not mix U.S. short tons and metric tonnes.

At $300,000 per base machine, four machines imply $1.2 million in base-equipment cost, not the full fleet program cost. A [Warren CAT listing](https://www.warrencat.com/rental/material-rock-screener/) at $16,697/month implies about 18 rental months equal a $300,000 purchase price before scope differences. This is equipment-expense equivalence, not operating payback or a Hawaiʻi rental offer. Riverside County's [screen-and-stacker procurement](https://media.rivcocob.org/proceeds/2025/p2025_10_07_files/15.02001.pdf) is an additional public-sector comparator.

## What the campaign must establish

1. Exact offered model and reconciled sustained throughput on representative feed, with complete-line rather than screen-only scope.
2. Delivered and commissioned cost, factory-integrated electrical package, wear parts, service access and contractual acceptance criteria.
3. Reconciled input/output mass balance, product purity, accepted shipments and lawful residual routes.
4. Actual labor, power/fuel, excavation, transport, testing and residual costs.
5. Cost per tonne processed, net airspace recovered and full public lifecycle comparison with disposal alternatives.

Report measured trial costs separately from modeled production costs. A 20-tonne/day characterization trial need not become a permanent production limit. Test actual minimum feed, uptime and separation quality before assuming scalable utilization.

## Research integrity and current corrections

The unsupported **$28–54 million annual material-revenue headline has been withdrawn from the financial case**. It relied on unverified positive-price RDF and retail-compost comparisons. DeepSeek explicitly acknowledged errors or overstatement concerning nonferrous arithmetic, historical EPA equipment-price inference, Denton evidence, unequal rental/purchase cost scopes and describing a landfill as pure cost. Landfills can collect tipping fees; account for them and residual-disposal needs. Preserve public land benefits without assuming all mining is profitable.

The [2017 Denton thesis](https://mavmatrix.uta.edu/civilengineering_theses/388/) is a modeled academic precedent, not evidence of realized Hawaiʻi returns. Provenance preserves original AI claims, subsequent corrections and source links. **Unverified does not mean disproven; a published supplier claim does not mean independently demonstrated.**
