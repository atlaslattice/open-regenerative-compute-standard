## 1. Problem and program boundary
ORCS is a proposed 30-year, $1.5T national productive infrastructure financing architecture targeting **3 million net-new active paid jobs and 3 million finished, habitable $200k-all-in homes by 31 December 2030**, subject to physical, financial and legal verification. County participation is a local benefit guarantee, not an arbitrary one-factory mandate. Component streams include modular manufacturing, robotics, grid work, recovered domestic materials, wastewater modernization, agriculture/nutrient recovery, housing and community-positive AI/infrastructure nodes.

Keep commercial cash flow, public capital, job-year occupancy, net-new direct paid employment, housing delivery, product qualification, environmental flows and noncash community benefits in separate ledgers. Source status vocabulary: PUBLISHED / DERIVED / ASSUMED / TARGET / OPEN.

## 4. Mathematical definitions
- Annual materials direct FTE = inbound short tons × measured productive labor-hours/ton ÷ productive hours/year.
- Waste modules = CEILING(annual actual tons / (nameplate tons/module × utilization)).
- Water/grid field construction *annual job-year occupancy* = capital projects actually executed that year (USD millions) × **scenario assumed** direct job-years per $1M; add incremental permanent O&M separately.
- Potential net-new jobs = a separately supported share of gross activity; site transfers, existing roles, displacement and supply-chain substitution must be subtracted.
- Waste gross revenues = qualified tons sold × realized market price + contracted eligible tipping tons × fee.
- Waste annual surplus proxy = gross sales and eligible fees - labor - nonlabor operating costs - annualized capital charge. This is **not** equity return or fully taxed profit.
- 145M tons is inbound **processing capacity**, not automatically 145M incremental landfill diversion. Source streams must reconcile: new diversion + upgraded existing recovery + legacy landfill mining.
- Where field construction projects are temporary but sequential, distinguish annual job-year occupancy from net-new *concurrent* long-run jobs. Do not add the same worker twice.
- Community economic benefits cannot be used as debt-service cash absent a bankable contract/appropriation.

## 5. Factory staffing: important earlier ORCS correction
Four crews of 60 workers on 40 paid hours/week supply 4×60×40 = 9,600 work hours/week, short of the 60×168 = 10,080 hours required to staff 60 always-present posts. The bare floor is **252 FTE/site**; a leave/relief scenario of 5.25 FTE per continuously staffed post implies **315 FTE/site, 990,360 jobs nationally for 3,144 sites**. Both remain *staffing scenarios*, not demonstrated factory equipment requirements. Previous 240/site and 754,560 national jobs **do not cover** the stated 24/7 staffing condition.

## 6. Finance and timetable
Illustrative $1.5T 30-year debt at 5% nominal fixed amortization is ~$97.6B/year; 3M workers × $70k loaded/year is ~$210B/year in labor cost alone, not automatically paid from the debt. Existing proposals involving $180B liquidity/risk capacity plus $20B enabling budget need reconciled sources/uses. The previously discussed $100B/year refinery authority cannot continue 30 years inside $1.5–2T; it requires limited duration, different funding or reduced amounts. The 2027–2031 five-year waste ramp cannot silently be treated as completed by 2030.

## 7. Open work and failure conditions
No county-by-county QCEW/CBP records, individual plant audits, signed processing contracts, utility project awards, actual factory labor coefficients, qualified product off-take, land costs or training enrollments have yet been ingested. Pilot gates: verified physical throughput, independent safety/product qualifications, feasible unit economics and debt coverage, audited net-new payroll, observable community benefits, and causal AI productivity comparisons. Stop or redesign if qualification fails, the business case requires fabricated revenue, baseline jobs are relabeled, or county impacts cannot be audited.
