# New Deal 2.0 / ORCS — $200B Liquidity + Standards, $1.5T Civilian Productive Infrastructure (Thought Experiment v0.2)

**Date:** 2026-10-17  
**Status:** PROPOSED / NON-CANON / SIMULATED / NOT APPROPRIATED / ZERO REALIZED CREDIT  
**Owner:** Civilian-led national ORCS proposal; potential defense-community interoperability is an ancillary public benefit, not authority for military control.

## Executive hypothesis
Authorize **$200 billion** in proposed federal budget authority within or alongside an FY2027 defense-package negotiation, and establish **separate authority for up to $1.5 trillion of financed civilian infrastructure**. The $200B includes $180B of modeled liquidity/risk capacity and $20B for a transformational regulation, standards, permitting, inspection, data-quality, and training program. The **$200B is not itself $1.5T of loans**, nor is the $1.5T proof of private-sector credit availability. Congress would have to authorize purpose, governance, financing, and budgetary treatment.

If the underlying defense request stays $1.5T, adding $200B implies a **$1.7T** proposed package; an unchanged $1.5T headline requires reallocating $200B, not double-counting. The $200B figure is **13.33%** of the original $1.5T headline, rather than less than 10%.

**First-year mandate:** demonstrable mobilization in all 50 states, with county-level access and benefit reporting: apprenticeships, commissioned modular facilities where feasible, contracted offtake, construction starts, baseline publication, and initial service improvements. **Construction acceptance deadline:** 36 months for standardized modular projects, 60 months for complex infrastructure, with independently documented exceptions. Authorization in Year 1 does not imply $1.5T of physical installations completed that year.

## $1.5T capital financing assumptions
Illustrative loan-equivalent $1.5T financed at fixed **5% for 30 years**, fully drawn at outset and level-payment: **$97.58B annual debt service**; **$2.927 trillion** of aggregate 30-year nominal payments (i.e. $2.927T). If loans are progressively drawn, effective annual payments, timing, interest accrual and reserve exposure change. Model nominal dollars and explicit inflation separately.

The project's **approval target** is a minimum **1.50x benefit/cost in 5%-discounted present value**, net of operating and lifecycle costs, with bankable cash flows for debt repayment. Public value, monetary operating receipts, domestic gross output, GDP value added, imported materials displaced, and avoided costs must be separate accounts. *No guarantee* that the $1.5T stock of qualifying projects exists. Realized returns below 50% alone do not trigger automatic forfeiture when honest disclosure and competent operations are proven; deliberate misstatement or controllable failure does.

**Stress case:** In a previous full-upfront-draw, 5% financing illustrative deployment scenario with operating activation factors 10%,25%,50%,80%,100% and a 1.50x discounted financial objective, startup liquidity requirement approached **$159B** including bridge interest. The proposed $180B liquidity allocation leaves about **$21B nominal headroom in that specific scenario**, not a reliable margin under cost inflation, delayed demand, defaults or broader shocks. Stage drawdowns, milestones, capitalized interest where lawful, insurance, and verified contract receipts before commitment.

### Project approval gate — three independent verifications
1. **Resource + buyers:** source-specific annual flows, existing uses, deliverable end-product demand and legally enforceable offtake or verified savings.
2. **Engineering + regulation:** real vendor CAPEX/OPEX and commissioning plan; tested technology; product health/environment criteria, rights and permits.
3. **Finance + public benefit:** full-cycle discounted benefit cost, cashflow/debt-service coverage, contingencies, credit exposure, displacement adjustment, auditable community outcomes, independent downside analysis.

All stages record MEASURED / SIMULATED / PROPOSED / QUARANTINED / ZERO-REALIZED-CREDIT status, public aggregated receipts, versioned audits, redress channels.

### Milestone enforcement / recovery
For missed **controllable** milestones, employ notices, cure windows, independent attribution review, withheld performance payments, liquidated damages where enforceable, letters of credit, bonded performance, pledged collateral, rights to replace the operator or step into an eligible project, insurer/surety claims, and referral for fraud. Recovery must follow contracts, governing law, creditor priorities, court process, and due process. **Do not promise automatic confiscation of all contractor assets or assume contractor balance-sheet assets always equal project size; require risk-proportionate bonding/collateral and parent/surety guarantees**, with access for competent small suppliers. Document force majeure, agency-caused delay, changes in law and other no-fault relief. Bankruptcy and existing secured creditors may limit repossession.

## ROI, deficit and fiscal ledgers
Economic efficiency savings, private earnings, physical trade replacement, and **federal/local deficit reduction** are tracked separately. Avoided imports do not automatically improve the trade deficit 1:1; U.S. exports and import content of replacements matter. GDP gross value added != gross intermediate throughput. **Reduced deficits are not automatically project revenues** and must not be added to the same underlying cost saving twice. Legal taxpayer benefit needs cash receipts or reduced appropriations/expenses; public social dividends and education carry noncommercial metrics.

Defense comparison: a $1.5T annual defense request serves public security purposes, includes workforce and capital, and is **not** a $1.5T commercial investment with zero value. This paper models an option to fund civilian supply security alongside defense, not verified equivalence, agency endorsement, passed appropriations or superiority of actual net present benefits.

## Term limits / policy separation
A **third elected presidential term is prohibited by the Twenty-Second Amendment** unless the Constitution is amended. A New Deal 2.0 proposal does not change that legal rule. Infrastructure can be designed for administrations of either party and be judged on community benefits independently of candidate preference.

## Five-year / 30-year delivery milestones
**2027:** full 50-state mobilization, county project registries, apprenticeship cohorts, open standards, early retrofits and accepted local pilots; national data baseline. **2028–2029:** modular plant completion / startup, verified operations receipts, third-party audits. **2030–2031:** complex infrastructure completion, final commissioning, national impact review, corrective operators where warranted. **Years 6–30:** independently verified operations, amortization, maintenance, repayments, contingency testing, and capped reinvestment from actual excess cash.
