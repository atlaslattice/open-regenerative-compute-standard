## Conditional nuclear blanket precursor route
Phosphate ore fluorine co-products -> purification/speciation (e.g. anhydrous HF if feasible) -> qualified LiF manufacture -> beryllium fluoride acquisition/manufacture -> controlled blend/cleanup -> candidate FLiBe batch -> analytical qualification -> blanket/neutronics/tritium validation.
**Not a direct equivalence:** LiPF6 electrolyte output is not LiF supply; LiF is a separate precursor/product and LiPF6 chain can use LiF, but LiPF6 tonnage does not imply recovered usable LiF production capacity. Beryllium procurement, high-purity handling, corrosion control, isotope composition (Li6/Li7 depending on design), tritium breeding, activation products, safeguards and radiological/chemical industrial hygiene are separate gates. No claim project already makes hundreds of tonnes of fusion-grade salt.
