# ORCS — Guizhou phosphorus–titanium–iron–lithium integrated circular project
**Primary-source company announcement dated Oct 28 2026; project plan, not commissioned results.**
Guizhou Phosphate Chemical Group reports site preparations for a proposed **RMB 33.1bn** project: **400kt/y titanium dioxide** with **1.4Mt/y ferrous sulfate heptahydrate**, **600kt/y lithium iron phosphate**, and **20kt/y lithium hexafluorophosphate**; proposed PG decomposition supplies sulfuric acid to phosphoric acid manufacture, and titanium byproduct ferrous sulfate supplies LFP line. Source: https://www.guizhoulinhua.com/en/895.html .
**Important:** Ferrous sulfate heptahydrate wet-mass cannot be equated to contained iron mass; neither annual nameplate product totals nor internal reuse is actual yield. Need energy, sulfur and iron stoichiometry, wet/dry assay, phosphorus/fluorine balances, raw feed procurement, waste treatment, capex/opex, environmental permits, end market and internal transfer prices. Avoid claiming positive margin or closed loop from announcement alone.
