## New evidence

[ Barr & Moir, Test Results on Plasma Direct Converters (1983), DOI 10.13182/FST83-A20820](https://doi.org/10.13182/FST83-A20820) supplies a separate TMX experimental benchmark. Its abstract reports incident/collected/suppressor powers of 138/79/12 W. The body gives 0.14 A at 565 V, suppressor drain 0.025 A at -500 V, and 0.15 A of incident ions averaging 930 eV. These descriptions give respectively 67 W and 66.6 W net; both are consistent with the reported 48±2% at stated precision. Preserve each record rather than silently reconcile rounded inputs.

Figure 15, printed page109/PDF page12, contains 19 ion-collector current markers recovered here. The TMX module's 0.05-mm wires, 1.5-mm pitch and 130-mm aperture are benchmark geometry, not STARS specifications. Its geometry must not be confused with the paper's separate 100-keV test stand. No measured efficiency or dimension is transferred to the p–11B apparatus.

## Recovery and uncertainty

DOWNLOAD.json pins the author-hosted PDF SHA256. Original paper and scanned figures are not redistributed. FIGURE15_MARKERS.json records source page, native image size, crop, affine axis anchors and all marker coordinates. The three anchors handle scan skew. Source-image closed-hole centroids are re-extracted with connected components and compared to 18 archived marker coordinates; the remaining open-ring marker is explicitly manual. The marker selection was checked visually against the scanned figure; no current outliers are deleted. Repeated-voltage points remain separate, not averaged into an artificial exact curve.

The ±3-pixel tolerance is an analyst digitization sensitivity, not a reported experimental error or confidence interval. All extreme combinations of axis-anchor errors are combined with marker-coordinate corners, preserving the global nature of calibration errors. The resulting CSV bounds do not account for point-to-point experimental scatter, campaign systematics or unknown covariance. Slightly negative digitized coordinates close to the origin are retained with their bounds rather than clipped to a physically preferred value.

The optional curve check reconstructs a plateau/exponential tail using the annotations, then fits lower-voltage groups and withholds entire high-voltage groups. It is not an independent campaign. The displayed-annotation reconstruction has 11.73 mA RMS residual against all markers. The fit trained on 15 points gives a 16.40 mA held-out RMS residual on four points. We do not supply a fabricated measurement-noise model or promote a numerical optimizer's success to physical calibration acceptance. Fitted parameters and all observations remain in the results. The fit is our approximation to net terminal current, not a universal incoming-alpha energy distribution.

## Reusable terminal accounting

terminal_ledger.py implements signed terminal V*I, defining current as net positive-charge flow into an electrode. Positive output and external draw are tracked separately. An electron current into a positive collector reduces output; a negatively biased suppressor with positive-current drain consumes power. All sign combinations are supported. Missing incident power gives an unknown ratio; zero incident power gives an undefined ratio rather than NaN or a fabricated efficiency. Negative net results are preserved. The arithmetic remainder is not automatically recovered heat or a resolved loss channel. Facility wall-plug power remains null.

Eight sign/missingness tests pass. Each source recovery run passes nine numerical/provenance checks; these do not establish a goodness-of-fit acceptance threshold. Two fresh executions reproduce the CSV, result JSON and generated visualization exactly in the pinned environment. The accounting benchmark establishes implementation behavior; it does not identify secondary-electron yields, space-charge limits or complete STARS operating performance.

## Charge-state search

CHARGE_STATE_GAPS.json preserves two leads: Armstrong's carbon-foil helium measurements and Funsten's carbon-foil negative-helium result. Neither provides a recovered, target-matched B11/BN numerical package. No fraction is assigned to STARS. No claim is made that matching data do not exist. The low-energy negative-helium lead also means a future general charge-state interface should not silently assume nonnegative charges are always exhaustive. That does not establish a significant negative-ion population in our apparatus.

## Reproduction

Python/NumPy/SciPy/Pillow/PyMuPDF/Matplotlib are required for figure recovery; the terminal ledger itself uses only the standard library. Software versions and source/code hashes are recorded. Download the source through fetch_source.py: it refuses to replace an existing mismatched file. No external solver runs are needed.

```
python collector_evidence_v0_1/fetch_source.py --out collector_evidence_v0_1/local_source.pdf
python collector_evidence_v0_1/recover.py --pdf collector_evidence_v0_1/local_source.pdf --out collector_evidence_v0_1/fresh_run
python -m unittest discover -s collector_evidence_v0_1 -v
python collector_evidence_v0_1/audit_replay.py
```

Each output directory must be new. The checked-in CSV/results/plot are sufficient to inspect the recovery without downloading the PDF. The generated plot is an original replot of recovered measurements and our reconstruction, not a copied scanned figure. Published geometry and currents remain benchmark-only inputs. Future integration needs actual target exit states, a physically specified collector/circuit and applicable loss/charge-state data; no universal efficiency multiplier is introduced here.
