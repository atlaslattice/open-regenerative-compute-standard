The fresh source-to-facility control is executable. The original physical target/pocket and collector specification remains unresolved. Keep the definitive apparatus contract unfrozen and do not relabel this control as full original C or the A/B hybrid.

The modeled nuclear release is about5.983ppm of the source-beam energy. This identifies reaction probability/energy retention as critical, but it is not a plant gain measurement. The initial99.9994% source-efficiency threshold applies only when source-conversion losses receive no electrical credit, downstream recovery is perfect and auxiliary net draw/storage change are zero. It is not a universal facility bound. The current ledger explicitly adds source-conversion loss, auxiliary-own-input recovery and source idle/preparation costs. Perfect all-input recovery gives a modeled-Q energy-only upper envelope; temperatures, exergy, sinks, parasitic draw and actual converter behavior remain unresolved.
