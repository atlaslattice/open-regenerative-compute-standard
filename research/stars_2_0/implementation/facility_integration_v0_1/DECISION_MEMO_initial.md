# Integration decision memo — candidate v0.1, 2026-11-01

The software can now execute the reference source-to-facility chain in one command. This does not resolve the original physical target or collector drawings. Keep the full apparatus contract unfrozen.

The implemented reference is an externally driven cold-target single-pass control. Its expected primary-channel nuclear energy is about 5.983 parts per million of source proton kinetic energy. Even an enclosing facility returning every modeled energy channel as electricity, with zero auxiliary cost and no storage change, needs wall-to-proton efficiency greater than 0.9999940168 for strictly positive net electricity. Direct alpha collection alone is insufficient for this control even with an ideal source. These are necessary bounds conditional on the modeled reaction count, not measured plant efficiencies or rejection of the entire p–11B program.

The priority is now to change a physically specified source/target/transport mechanism and test whether its reaction probability and energy retention improve. Optimizing a scalar collector percentage cannot repair this control's low reaction probability. Proposed repeated encounters, hot-plasma burn, acoustic-dependent source overlap and the A/B hybrid require different state evolution and new reaction counts; the current bound must not be transferred to them as a result.

1. Keep this new control ID separate from unchanged C and from A/B. Preserve all 26 original C module entries and the hollow-sphere/PRISM/ENN additions; availability is not proof all alternatives are installed or all responses are modeled.
2. Recover or explicitly define a new version of the physical target/support/collector geometry. The archived 4–6 micrometre film candidate, later P1/P2/P3 suggestions and original ceramic pocket remain distinct. Do not silently substitute any of them.
3. Qualify an absolutely normalized source energy/angle/position/time distribution and material state. A literature component or source-stability benchmark is not automatically this apparatus input.
4. Test target and transport alternatives under equal source and wall budgets. Add source recirculation or burn only with cumulative stopping/scattering, losses, field work, residence time and particle inventories. No independent gain-factor multiplication.
5. Populate facility costs from observations or explicitly derived, cited component models. Count preparation/timeout/abort costs, firing-dependent costs and source drive once. Retain missing values and negative outcomes. Cyclic-storage power requires zero inventory change or a qualified complete noncyclic flow adapter.

The paired acoustic work remains relevant: better readiness/firing reliability changes unconditional throughput and cost amortization. Improvements in the per-fired source distribution or target state must enter the physical model; they are not created by adjusting diagnostic qualification. Do not assert that an unmodeled acoustic response has been calibrated to zero.

No source, material, collector, startup, sustained-burn or grid-assisted operating contract is frozen by this release. The separate Beyond Q isotope-refinery study remains a different apparatus/accounting branch.
