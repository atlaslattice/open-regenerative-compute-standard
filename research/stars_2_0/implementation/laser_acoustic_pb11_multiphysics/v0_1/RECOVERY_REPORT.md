## Unresolved pocket inputs

Ceramic composition; isotope-bearing phase; loose/bonded/sintered/film form;
grain sizes and distribution; packing and porosity; dimensions and wall thickness;
material density; isotope fraction and B11 inventory; apertures; acoustic mounting,
damping, forcing and thermal environment; laser exposure; complete multilayer
stopping stack; proton entry and alpha-exit paths; detector acceptance.

The template contains no invented dimensions or default chemistry. It is not
fabrication CAD. Separate material density/porosity/contact/thermal/optical property
records must be supplied with uncertainty and applicability before solver input
is promoted. The completeness checker is deliberately a prerequisite check, not
comprehensive constitutive or CAD validation.

## Supersession reconciliation

The October29 architecture addendum used earlier A/B/C meanings. PR43 and the
project originator's later clarification supersede those labels prospectively:
A=new hollow-sphere+PRISM+ENN hybrid; B=A+original STARS; C=original STARS.
Historical files/labels remain unchanged. The older acoustic contract's statement
that original bundles were not verified public is historical: PR42 subsequently
preserved/replayed the recovered originals. Neither update creates an apparatus
calibration. Mechanical pocket work can proceed as an explicit candidate without
silently incorporating it into every apparatus identity.
