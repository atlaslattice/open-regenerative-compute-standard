## Compare mechanisms and interfaces

Do not assume a spherical torus, a tandem mirror and a laser pitcher-catcher are one interchangeable confinement region. They may be alternatives, or distinct sequential stages if an explicit physical interface is specified. Plasma state, magnetic topology, density, timescale, target survival and transport must be compatible. An ENN proton-energy range or a PRISM concept is not itself an integrated source/confinement performance model.

Record both total source distribution and channel-specific relative collision energies. A proton cutoff is not the whole spectrum. Nuclear source validity must cover the selected energy regime; a single-resonance generator must not silently cover low-energy and tens-of-MeV regimes. Track incoming kinetic energy, nuclear Q, material losses and external field work separately for alpha birth and exit. Preserve all sourced rate and spectral units, including alpha counts versus reaction events and quantities perMeV.

For acoustics keep readiness, firing success, pointing/timing stability and conditional pulse output distinct. Retain all attempted preparations and missing outcomes. Qualifying more diagnostic records alone cannot create physical energy; more productive physical pulses may change aggregate energy. Startup or concurrent preparation differs from serial preparation before each pulse.

## Comparisons and promotion

1. Recover and replay the reported hybrid exactly, preserving impressive, null and adverse outputs.
2. Compare evidence-constrained and exploratory-potential results under disclosed source, time, energy, material and cost boundaries. Different apparatus budgets need explicit normalization; an architecture-level comparison does not identify a single component's causal effect.
3. Ablate source changes, geometry/field changes, retention/ash handling, acoustics and conversion separately where physically meaningful. Keep alternate configurations selectable rather than multiplying incompatible published gains.
4. Promote individually supported changes into a new Stars design candidate with a named delta, citation/derivation, applicability, interface and uncertainty. Preserve the original reference and hybrid as independent configurations.
