# STARS 2.0 G1/G2 coupled-field benchmark v0.6

**Date:** 2026-10-28. **Executed, exploratory. NOT a calibrated reactor, plasma, BN foil or fusion-energy simulation.** Append-only new local version referencing v0.1–v0.5.

## Architecture and control contract
A = unisolated/no electromagnetic (EM) guidance; B = −80 dB isolated/no EM; C = isolated + intentional 10 kHz acoustic drive/no EM; D = C + static uniform 3 T magnetic guidance. All mechanics uses the same uncalibrated 10-kHz single-mode oscillator from v0.5. Mechanical isolation suppresses only *ambient input*, not the intentionally applied *internal* actuator; attenuation is idealized and specified only at 10 kHz. Phase between background and drive is not characterized, hence report separate amplitudes and possible phase-coherent total bounds.

## Numerical inputs
External harmonic pressure: 1 Pa; after −80 dB: 0.0001 Pa. Internal drive: 0.05 Pa. Mass per area 0.01 kg/m²; beta=500 s⁻¹. Proton kinetic energy =675 keV LAB; alpha illustration 2893.3 keV (8.68 MeV/3), **not a sampled correlated 3-alpha reaction**. Uniform static B=3 T, E=0, fictitious cylindrical sidewall radius R=0.3 m. Particle source at axis launched perpendicular to B. Numerical Boris integrator, 7000 steps over 350 ns. Neglect axial movement, fields generated by particles, collisions, stopping, straggling, density, target, collector, and power. Field is *trajectory guidance only*, not realistic confinement or recirculation.

Passing toy orbits are not evidence of plasma confinement, stability, nuclear reaction rate, or electrical recovery. A static B field cannot do kinetic work on the particles.

## Decision / negative controls
No per-event reaction probability or alpha-production rate is calculated, so all arms have *unavailable* nuclear output rather than fictitious gains. No reliable electrical energy ledger is available: the magnets, actuators, laser, cryocooling, pump and output collector have not been specified with measured loads. Replacing absent values with zeros would be scientifically improper.
