## Empirical
The EXL-50U publication describes H-mode operation in boron-containing plasma. This establishes a confinement-regime observation; it does not establish net p-11B electrical output, or validate acoustic pretreatment. Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13343428/
DeepSeek also reports >10^8 p-11B reactions/s and beam-target yield enhancement 1.48x/1.75x, but those particular studies were **not independently verified in our source audit**. Keep separate from EXL-50U H-mode.
## Theory
2026 Physical Review C muon-catalysis work calculates increased low-energy p-11B reaction cross sections: https://journals.aps.org/prc/accepted/10.1103/2p5j-2m53 . An increased cross section is **not** proof of favorable muon-production, fuel, capture, plasma heating or whole-plant energy balance.
A 2026 centrifugal-shear non-equilibrium proposal has been published as a technical disclosure: https://www.tdcommons.org/dpubs_series/11795/ . Review assumptions, Rider comparison, collisions, magnet/field conditions and stability before attaching it to PRISM. A theoretical paper is not a validated operating machine.
## Existing experimental gate protection
Preserve sham-controlled acoustic/laser success probability, shot timing, jitter and target stability independently from fusion energy yield. Do not infer an acoustic effect from H-mode or muon theory.
