## 3. Corrections that must survive into implementation

### D01 — Energy labels and the excitation function

v0.3–v0.6 repeat the earlier 675-keV-CM premise; v0.7 puts it on hold; v0.8/v0.9 identify the literature feature as incident LAB. The mathematical conversion was not the main error: assigning the source feature to the wrong frame was. Preserve original frame labels and derived coordinates separately. The later 41-point dataset's largest sampled point at 650 keV LAB does not independently locate the underlying resonance center. Do not replace a source-resolved excitation function with a guessed universal peak.

### D02 — Alpha production is not automatically an event cross section

Early birth integrals use event probability followed by three alpha births. Later EXFOR recovery supplies the reported Spraker alpha-production observable, whose energy-window/analysis semantics require an explicit transformation before event use. A normalized CSV and exact numerical reproduction do not authorize division by three, another multiplication by three, or substitution into the event probability integral. Retain source statistical errors and common 3.6% target uncertainty. The convenient EXFORTABLES zero-error fields do not erase ERR-S in the master.

### D03 — No single catcher optimum has survived as a calibrated result

v0.6: 5 µm escaped energy, 6 µm escaped count in original surrogate. v0.7: revised reported rear 5 µm and combined 6 µm, plus separate adversarial scenario brackets. v0.8: sampled report rear 4.5 µm and combined 5.5 µm. v0.9: unreproduced revised report rear 5.5 µm and combined 6 µm. Later v0.4 engine audit reports objective-dependent collector shifts, including the 6-versus-7 µm discrepancy. These are different model/source/objective generations, not repeated measurements of one optimum and not a continuous dataset. Record objective, face, source spectrum, support, threshold, code version and grid with every maximum. Keep broad near-optimal regions and uncertainty before choosing hardware.

### D04 — Conservation needs independent physical masses and connected transport

Q alone is not the entire LAB product kinetic budget: for a stationary target, the full three-alpha kinetic sum includes incident proton kinetic energy. The later Gemini audit finds effective boron mass chosen for toy closure and a thickness routine disconnected from its event generator, using fixed weighted spectral bins instead. Passing the event test therefore does not verify the thickness spectra. Independent mass/Q/four-vector checks and event-to-transport wiring are separate requirements. Unsupported fixed branching remains unsupported after a conservation pass.

### D05 — Materials and supports cannot share an undocumented stopping law

BN, elemental B, silicon, carbon, oxides, adhesives, windows and detector dead layers require distinct qualified inputs. Film thickness alone omits its support column. Describe upstream/downstream and substrate-facing/vacuum-facing separately; reverse orientation changes incoming beam transport too. Matched B-11 atoms/cm² aids comparison but does not equalize stopping, competing reactions or detection. Range inversion is conditional on the table and CSDA assumptions; it is not exact physical transport.

### D06 — Causal and operational questions need separate denominators

v0.7 corrects overconditioning on the proton-spectrum mediator; v0.8 adds no-actuator REFERENCE alongside OFF/SHAM/ON; v0.9 adds qualified-shot throughput. Stage-4 review subsequently requires all randomized shots and treatment-dependent qualification to be reported separately. Missing detector output is not zero output. An apparent improvement among selected usable shots can arise from selection alone. Equal mean with lower variance may improve research throughput, but does not establish more nuclear energy or net electricity.

### D07 — Boundaries determine recoverable energy

The same alpha joule cannot be credited to electricity, wave heating and recoverable heat. Transmitted proton kinetic energy outside the thermal boundary cannot be included as target recoverable heat. Material energy loss is not automatically recoverable calorimetric heat. Include escaping particles/radiation, field or material storage, unresolved sinks, service costs and failed shots. Compare collectors on the same incoming phase-space distribution, not a separate 100-mJ benchmark against a sub-microjoule modeled plume.

### D08 — Source status changes with receipts, not enthusiasm

v0.8/v0.9 missing-data/source statements are historical checkpoints. Later EXFOR master recovery closes specific numeric acquisition gaps. Later Tidal Lantern audit reports actual Gemini engine v0.4 receipt and execution, two bundled test passes and five generated CSVs. It also records a source-hash mismatch and says source/CSV repository ingestion was incomplete. GPTWork has not locally reproduced that later engine. Do not state that no one received/executed it; do not promote the two tests to the entire preflight suite or physical calibration.

### D09 — Harmonic mathematics is a separate hypothesis

v0.9's mathematical announcement is not an experimental acoustic result. Later numerical phase rankings depend on peak, threshold occupancy and curvature metrics; measured displacement differs from inferred local stress/fatigue. The October 28 scope separates SHUGS/TCSS from Stars physical prediction. Conventional measured modes, Fourier/PDE/FEM methods and physical acoustic preparation remain legitimate. No exotic operator supplies reaction or efficiency multipliers.

- [PR #13](https://github.com/atlaslattice/open-regenerative-compute-standard/pull/13): five-parameter thin-target synthetic design, four verification groups and a minimal experimental candidate. Areal-density and efficiency calibration resolve exact degeneracies only under its favorable assumptions; uncertain fluence, birth distributions, backgrounds and correlated errors add new ones. A model discrepancy is exactly absorbed as reaction normalization, so full rank is insufficient.

**Attribution correction:** PR #13's proposed self-supporting/minimally supported boron target is not a newly discovered design. DeepSeek's pre-existing A/B/C target protocol already includes it. The narrower elemental-B-11-first suggestion is a simplification candidate relative to the BN emphasis of earlier papers, not an approved replacement for the BN experiment. Qualification must include fabrication, handling, composition, beam damage and independent material data. Enrichment itself is not presumed economically necessary. Keep BN as an explicit comparison branch rather than silently deleting it.

**Priority reconciliation:** earlier papers often emphasize converter optimization and acoustic trials; v0.9 and Stage-4 review strengthen clean-beam calibration and identifiability. The current user-directed focus selects that conventional physical lane first. It does not erase acoustic research or imply that any physical enhancement has been disproved.

## 6. Proposed build order after this review

1. Resolve source quantity/frame/uncertainty semantics and material-specific stopping coverage; preserve unqualified data in quarantine.
2. Reconcile and locally reproduce the actual later Gemini engine before assigning its test results to GPTWork. Retain a separate reference implementation and independent conservation oracle.
3. Qualify real layer stacks and detector/beam calibrations, including B-11 inventory and fluence; select feasible elemental-B/BN comparison rather than freezing the toy geometry.
4. Connect physical mass-correct correlated events to layer-resolved transport and detector forward folding. Use independent analytic and source fixtures, convergence and mutation tests.
5. Extend synthetic design to binned spectra, realistic noise, common calibration nuisances and discrepancy alternatives; choose measurements by information gained per cost only after stating priors and feasible measurement precision.
6. Freeze grouped holdouts, likelihood/background checks and failure criteria before viewing held-out experimental outcomes; obtain independent calibration data. Scientific freeze and blinded predictions require their separate approval gates.
7. Only then promote target/collector/acoustic optimization under a complete shot and energy ledger. Publish failures and near-optimal regions.

This register does not freeze a definitive contract. Subsequent corrections belong in STARS DELTAS v0.2 or later, with sources and an explicit delta from this version.

## 7. Reproducible text accounting

`compare_papers.py` verifies all eight paper Git blob hashes, orders them v0.2–v0.9, and emits seven adjacent unified diffs plus line counts. Fetch source files from the commit and paths in SOURCE_INDEX into a local directory using their original basenames, then run:

```bash
python compare_papers.py --source-dir /path/to/papers --out /path/to/new_receipt_directory
```

The output directory must not exist. Python standard library only; no RNG. The published `TEXT_DELTA_SUMMARY_v0_1.json` retains identity/line-count results; full diffs regenerate from pinned originals rather than duplicating the entire research corpus. Text removals in this accounting are differences between separate documents, **not repository deletions or scientific retractions**. In particular v0.5 supplements v0.4 rather than replacing its omitted detail. Matching is line-based with autojunk disabled; a reorganized paragraph can count as removed/added without a change in scientific meaning. Semantic interpretation is in §§2–6 above.
