## B. DeepSeek's five final safeguards — full audit and adopted disposition

### 1. Low-count Bayesian signal versus background: **ADOPT as preregistered parallel analysis, not exclusive mandate**
In G0-B, estimate counts using a Poisson likelihood for valid low-count bins, with detector efficiency, exposure, and background control measurement; use hierarchical/nuisance modeling for shot-to-shot variation, overdispersion, dead time, pileup and correlated instrument artifacts as appropriate. Prespecify meaningful priors and sensitivity analysis, both signal+background and background-only hypotheses, and compute Bayes factor BF10 alongside intervals/effect sizes, posterior predictive checks, and a robust predeclared frequentist cross-check. A fixed **BF10>10** can be a prospective decision threshold **if** calibrated in simulation for false positives and expected operating regime; it is not inherently universally superior or immune to p-hacking, and "5 sigma post-hoc" is not a suitable comparator to a properly preregistered frequentist test. Blind experimental acceptance threshold remains **not frozen** until background rates, sample sizes, test objective and external approval are established.

### 2. Shot-time state versus cold state: **ADOPT conditional time-resolved material state, do not assert numeric expansion**
A measured film's density, composition, areal B-11 distribution and geometry at proton arrival may differ from room-temperature metrology under prepulse heating/ablation. Define n_B11(x,t), phase/state, surface displacement, transmission and proton time-of-flight consistently; measure early-time dynamics through validated shadowgraphy/interferometry where applicable. A **radiation-hydrodynamics 1D model** may be a transparent sensitivity branch **for a laser-irradiated relevant region only**, with an explicit implementation/validation gate; it is not automatically appropriate for a separately positioned, proton-driven catcher. The quoted **4 μm→10 μm** is a possibility/example, not an observation. Cold areal density can be conserved during expansion absent mass loss and geometry changes; projected expansion length alone does not determine how many B nuclei are in the beam. Set a static validated baseline and compare measured-state sensitivity.

### 3. EMP and detector readout: **ADOPT instrumentation requirements, not compulsory fiber architecture**
Include EMP-only reference/dummy channels, shielded terminated pickup channels and particle-blocked controls; independently benchmark cable pickup, saturation, timing, optical interference and transducer interference. Fiber-optic links and remote shielded digitization are a strong **design option**, requiring real tests of near-detector preamplifiers, electro-optical conversion, local power and dynamic range. A Faraday cage on a detector blocks electric field pickup but does not necessarily preserve alpha acceptance or rule out all artifact classes; use matched geometry and particle-blocking tests. SHAM specifically tests acoustic-drive electrical/thermal confounding; add detector-only EMP controls as a **distinct control axis**, rather than assuming SHAM alone validates particle species.

### 4. Bayesian/ML optimizer cannot cheat: **ADOPT as mandatory release gate**
No optimization-driven promotion ahead of source-fit and G0-B blind validation. Constrain beam, acoustic frequency, amplitude, phase, delay, target state and thermal limits to independently measured feasible domains. Version-pin cross sections, stopping, code and detector model. Use held-out validation across shots/material batches/source conditions, adversarial edge/boundary tests, interpolation versus extrapolation flags, and compare to fixed baselines. An optimizer-selected setting requires new blind experimental confirmation; no test-set reuse as training.

### 5. Target lifetime and degradation: **ADOPT repeated-shot diagnostic / cost ledger, not an assumed depletion mechanism**
Measure changes in thickness, composition, dose, boron inventory, beam transmission, cracking, blistering, redeposition, contamination, optics and alpha output across actual shot series. Ion implantation and thermal/mechanical damage are plausible; **meaningful nuclear B-11 depletion is NOT established** under the current tiny modeled reaction yields. Nuclear product accumulation and light-element impurities should be measured rather than called inevitable "poisoning." A >20% yield-decline criterion can be a candidate preregistered lifetime endpoint, but must be chosen using calibrated baseline variance, target specification and cost model; not assigned as a universal physics threshold. Include replacements and downtime in qualified-shot facility cost.

## C. Three non-negotiables, adjudicated
1. **Fail closed on missing primary nuclear/stopping data** for any run labeled calibrated; emit units, source version, energy domain, uncertainty and error state. A deliberately separated **surrogate-only** mode may run with explicit warnings and no calibration/validation claims. Do not silently extrapolate.
2. **SHAM is essential but not magical:** an observed SHAM increase prompts investigation of heating, geometry, electrical pickup, coupling or contamination. It may represent a real *noncoherent* intervention, not automatically bad grounding. Never infer coherent acoustic resonance merely from ON>OFF.
3. **Preserve null/negative tests**, including substrate attenuation. A single null can reject a particular geometry/operating condition without proving that all supported films cannot work.
