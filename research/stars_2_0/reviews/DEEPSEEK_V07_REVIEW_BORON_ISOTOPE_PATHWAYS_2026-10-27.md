## 3. Boron-10 to boron-11: chemistry versus nuclear transmutation
User proposed breaking apart/recombining matter, possibly by neutron or electrons derived from hydrogen. Critical physical constraint: **chemical dissociation, plasma separation, electrons stripped from hydrogen and recombination cannot change a nucleus's isotope (mass number)**. Isotopic separation works through small isotopic effects on chemical/physical processes, concentrating pre-existing ¹¹B rather than transmuting ¹⁰B.

**Measured nuclear alternative:** ¹⁰B(p,γ)¹¹C → (β⁺ or electron capture; t½ ~20.4 minutes) → stable ¹¹B. The ¹⁰B(p,γ)¹¹C channel is measured in Phys. Rev. C 89, 014601 (2014), DOI https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevC.89.014601 over 2–6 MeV proton bombardment. ¹¹C decay properties: https://mirdsoft.org/products/MIRDspecs/MIRDspecs_HTMLs/C-11.htm; it decays to ¹¹B. Predominant decay is β⁺, minority electron capture. This pathway is **nuclear activation/transmutation, not a viable demonstrated commercial enrichment method**. It requires proton beam input, has competing ¹⁰B(p,α)⁷Be and other channels, produces short-lived radioactivity and positron/gamma handling needs, and isotope yield/target recovery are unquantified here.

**Neutron capture caveat:** ¹⁰B absorbs neutrons with high probability but generally proceeds through ¹⁰B(n,α)⁷Li, rather than retaining the neutron as stable ¹¹B. See IAEA/BNCT summary https://www-pub.iaea.org/MTCD/Publications/PDF/CRCP-BOR-002_web.pdf . Avoid interpreting the short-lived intermediate excited ¹¹B* as a durable ¹¹B product. No large-scale transmutation route is demonstrated here.

**Engineering priorities:** Compare:
- Natural B (~80.1% ¹¹B) vs enriched B catcher: alpha yield per incident proton, neutron/activation side products, contamination, wasted boron-10, target mass and price, and reactor wall-plug/lifecycle costs.
- Existing chemical exchange/distillation vs solvent extraction processes: product purity, yield, stages, chemical hazards, energy and financial costs. 20-stage >81.73% is progress but distant from >99%; do not extrapolate linearly.
- Proton-driven transmutation scenario only as a separate *feasibility bound* after evaluated reaction branch ratios and beam-wall energy per ¹¹B nucleus recovered are established. Cross-section fraction, losses, activation and handling must be explicit.
- Optional boron-10 **separate product/byproduct value** rather than requiring it to be transmuted; preserve materials circularity principles.
