# Stars 2.0 — Physics-Only Research Scope and SHUGS Operator Separation v0.1

**Date:** 2026-10-28  
**Status:** PROJECT-LEAD DIRECTION / APPEND-ONLY SCOPE CLARIFICATION; NOT AN EXPERIMENTAL RESULT, CANON CHANGE, OR FREEZE OF G0-B.

## Decision
Stars 2.0 is a physical proton–boron-11 research program. Use independently validated equations, measured or evaluated nuclear/material data, standard solvers, quantified uncertainties and conservation checks. **Exploratory mathematical/ontological operators are not reactor components and must not influence a Stars yield, confinement, or net-power estimate without an independently derived, calibrated physical correspondence.**

### Within Stars
- p + 11B -> 3 alpha: lab/CM conventions, measured cross sections, reaction-channel/branch data, event-level four-momentum conservation.
- Proton source and beam diagnostics; stopping/straggling; actual target stacks and measured B-11 areal atom density; material heat/elasticity.
- Acoustic preparation, pretreatment, resonant mechanical response and laser phase (as characterized physical inputs); laser–plasma processes (including SBS only where appropriate).
- Magnetic/electric particle transport with real field boundaries, axial losses, space charge and collisions.
- Detector forward model, correlated alpha escape, collector acceptance, shot qualification, and complete wall-plug electrical ledger.
- REFERENCE/OFF/SHAM/ON and duration/phase A/B controls, source grades and blind preregistration gates.

### Outside Stars — route to SHUGS / Element-145
- SHUGS/HSUF von Mangoldt/zeta kernels, GUE-KS target statistics and N=145 architectural optimization.
- TCSS / Path B 12x12x12 chiral or xp operator; missing X_z, L_z, H_xp definitions and theoretical spectral claims.
- Hilbert–Pólya, theta/Mellin, Riemann-zero / Riemann-inspired S-curve and Rainbow Yin–Yang *operator* assertions.
- Frankenstein/1,728-node routing, topology, governance and cognitive/ontology operator experiments.
- Operator spectral clustering as claimed physical membrane/target coupling without calibrated M,C,K, boundary conditions and measured response.

### Narrow interface
Stars may **export** traceable measurements and derived physical spectra to SHUGS for search/visualization. SHUGS outputs have no upstream computational authority over Stars unless a *separately approved* hypothesis clears mathematical definitions, dimensional units, source-independent baseline comparison, and blinded physical validation. Conventional spherical harmonics, Bessel eigenfunctions, Fourier/PDE solvers and experimentally measured spectroscopy remain legitimate Stars mathematical tools and should not be removed merely because they use the word 'spectral'.
