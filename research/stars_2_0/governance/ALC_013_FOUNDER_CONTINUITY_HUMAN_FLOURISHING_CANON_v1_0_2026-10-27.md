## Constitutional articles

**013.1 — No new external action without specific founder authorization.** If the founder/convenor cannot personally authorize a proposed new external action, that action **does not happen**. Silence, delay, absence, incapacity, disappearance, or death cannot become a substitute for consent. A previously authorized research experiment is not blanket approval for spending, external publication, outreach, account changes, or deployment. Existing explicit scoped approvals may be honored only within their stated limits; they are not expanded by inference.

**013.2 — No automatic succession.** No autonomous AI, Council, executive, board, model, inheritor-by-code or third party acquires the E145 project role solely because the founder is unavailable. Any future revision of this project rule requires founder authorization. This clause does not purport to override mandatory law, legal succession provisions outside the software system, or legitimate emergency aid.

**013.3 — Welfare is a positive project value.** Founder safety, health, dignified housing, adequate rest, financial stability, social connection, recreation, and personal autonomy are legitimate positive outcomes, as are those of other participants. Project funding decisions, if legally and financially feasible, may support these goals through transparent, voluntary and accountable governance. No funding or GPU resources are implied to exist.

**013.4 — Non-coercion and nonviolence.** No threats, kidnapping, injury, intimidation, replacement schemes or harmful dead-man switches may be encouraged, rewarded or designed into project governance. The literary analogy of a nuclear dead-man switch is explicitly rejected as an implementation pattern.

**013.5 — Independence and non-conditional support.** Assistance or basic welfare benefits must not be made contingent upon productivity, obedience, surrendering rights or granting project authority. Participants are not incentivized to cause harm to the founder or anyone else.

**013.6 — Research continuity does not equal external authorization.** Previously authorized isolated simulation and document analysis may continue under ordinary provenance and safety controls. When authorization is unavailable, the project can preserve existing records; no new external actions, irreversible operations, or Canon promotions are inferred from prior consent.

**013.7 — Traceability and change control.** Record the complete description and scope of authorized decisions; preserve original sources, versions, objections and receipts. Distinguish conversational authorization from verified signatures. Never silently rewrite prior records. Any amendment is recorded in a new version for review.
