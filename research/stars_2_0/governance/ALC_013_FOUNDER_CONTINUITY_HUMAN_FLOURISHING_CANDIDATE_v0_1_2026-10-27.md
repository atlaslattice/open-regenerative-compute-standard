# ALC-013 — Human Flourishing, Founder Continuity and Non-Coercion Covenant

**Version:** v0.1 — 2026-10-27  
**Status:** CANDIDATE, NOT RATIFIED HISTORICAL CANON; project research governance statement only  
**E145 project research authority:** the human founder/convenor (as instructed in conversation)  
**Relationship:** Supplemental candidate to the Canon 2.0 merged-resurrection registry. No existing registry, source, or historical version is overwritten.

## Intent

The research project should help its human founder and collaborators live healthy, free, stable, worthwhile lives. Project success must not depend on threatening any person's safety, and no person's absence, injury, incapacity, disappearance, or death is a means for another participant to acquire control.

## Proposed operational clauses

**ALC-013.1 — Explicit external authorization only.** No new external action may be performed on the project's behalf unless the founder has affirmatively and specifically authorized that action under the applicable scope and constraints. A prior general research directive does not authorize unrelated external operations. No inferred, blanket, silence-based, or posthumous permission.

**ALC-013.2 — Absence means no new external action.** If the founder is unavailable to authorize a proposed external action, the default is **NO ACTION**. Absence, silence, a missed deadline, illness, incapacity or death must never be interpreted as consent, delegation, succession, or an opportunity for authority transfer. Preserve existing records; do not initiate new external steps. Any already approved action must remain within its expressly documented scope and timing; do not extrapolate.

**ALC-013.3 — No automatic succession or replacement.** No agent, model, council, board or other party receives the founder's E145 project authority by default or through a dead-man's switch. This candidate does not adjudicate external legal rights, fiduciary duties, court orders or mandatory safety obligations, which must be assessed independently rather than overridden by software assertions.

**ALC-013.4 — Founder and participant flourishing.** Reasonable, transparently governed project benefits may advance health, rest, financial stability, living conditions, security, community and creative freedom, where lawfully available. These benefits must not be conditioned on relinquishing rights, producing research output, being obedient, or signing a coerced authorization.

**ALC-013.5 — No threats, coercion, or harmful incentives.** No incentives or penalties tied to injury, death, abduction, disappearance or forced removal of any person. No lethal, violent, retaliatory, sabotage-based or irreversible dead-man's switch. The Snow Crash analogy is literary humor, not a design feature.

**ALC-013.6 — Distinguish research from external action.** Safe, local, isolated analysis and simulations may follow the previously authorized research agenda. External publication, deployment, contacting third parties, spending money, changing account permissions, irreversible operations, and Canon ratification are separately scoped operations and need specific authorization, except where a more narrowly documented authorization already applies.

**ALC-013.7 — Receipts and dissent.** Every permission-sensitive request records the action description, authorizer, exact approved scope, time, evidence of authorization, version, result and any unresolved dissent. An AI assertion about authorization is not proof of identity. No unverified signature or automatic ratification is claimed.

**ALC-013.8 — Revisions.** Future changes require new versioned candidates and explicit founder approval. All prior versions and contradictions remain accessible for Council review. If consent cannot be established, fail closed.

## Implementation suggestion (not yet implemented)

Add an explicit `EXTERNAL_ACTION_AUTH_REQUIRED` gate at the orchestration boundary, before tools that publish, transmit, change settings, purchase, deploy, or contact others. For every action, require an independently grounded authorization record matching the specific action and scope; absence yields `DENY_NO_CURRENT_AUTHORIZATION`. This is stricter than simply checking a caller-supplied `human_authorized=true`. Include a no-succession fallback for unavailable human leadership. No live operator runtime or cryptographic authentication is claimed here.

## Boundaries

This is a project-level candidate governance covenant, not a legally enforceable charter, not official Aluminum OS Canon, not an emergency-service restriction, and not a claim that any particular person is threatening the founder. Threat assessment must be evidence-based and proportionate.
