# Beyond Q — Gemini convergence audit: FLiBe chemistry and fusion engineering
**2026-10-30 | Evidence note, no parameter changes**
## Primary breakthrough
Das et al., *Quantum Computations on Fusion Blanket Molten Salts*, preprint posted 2026-06-29, https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30402 , used hybrid quantum-classical calculations and IBM hardware to study tritium-containing FLiBe clusters. Quantum fragment energies agreed with high-accuracy benchmarks at mean absolute deviation ~0.3 kcal/mol across nine configurations. **Full-system reconstruction exposed ~110 kcal/mol average tritium-binding-energy discrepancy.** These are NOT accurate validated bulk extraction predictions: fragment construction/long-range electrostatics remain serious limitations. Oak Ridge press report https://www.ornl.gov/news/oak-ridge-national-lab-cleveland-clinic-and-ibm-achieve-first-known-computations-fusion/
### G3 research interpretation
- Treat as empirical computational demonstration of a **method**, not experimental tritium recovery, measured diffusion coefficient, speciation map, residence time, permeation or extraction efficiency.
- Add potential future chemistry input layer: composition, temperature, ionic configurations, free energies, equilibrium constants and uncertainty (not computed here).
- Require thermodynamic and transport validation before coupling to FLiBe extraction ODEs, time-dependent inventory, permeation barriers, throughput or tritium breeding ratio.
- Preserve existing baseline Reactor B **1.13 GW D-T fusion** and Reactor A **hypothetical 20 MW primary D-D**. No automatic recalibration.
