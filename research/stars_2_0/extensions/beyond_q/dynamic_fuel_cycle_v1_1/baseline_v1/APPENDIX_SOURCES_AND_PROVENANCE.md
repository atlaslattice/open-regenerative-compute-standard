## C. Provenance / origin of research work
- **Project originator (Atlas Lattice):** Requested two-reactor Beyond Q, coastal desal/deuterium self-supply, separate isotope and electricity ledgers, real-source-first with extrapolation explicitly labeled, Nightmare Lab falsification and transparent failure histories.
- **GPT-6 / ChatGPT (OpenAI):** Flagged role confusion gross burn vs campus net, scoped separate A0/A1/A2, identified 1.13GW basis, DD primary vs secondary burn, double-count heat, corrected factor-of-two neutron count and 3.65 vs 3.85 arithmetic, drafted independently rerunnable ledger and white paper. Prior assistant remarks were hypotheses and require independent checks, not assertions of published new physics.
- **DeepSeek:** Provided Chinese-language independent reviews, candidate breeding/recovery efficiency bands, Harbor Island and isotope supply leads, acknowledged corrections, proposed 1.3–2.6kg A blanket test envelope and extraction scenarios. Its internal contradictory tables were retained and corrected.
- **Gemini Argon (Google):** Proposed lithium enrichment optimization challenge, material-alloy alternatives, A and B coupled system fuel-cycle counterargument, cited MIT and 2026 experimental leads. Exact simulated numbers like natural-Li TBR=1.01–1.04, +5–8cm shields, 105–135MWth, universal 20–30% optimum were **not independently verified** and should not be promoted without reproducing exact conditions.
- **No autonomous peer review, no named AI laboratory contribution, no external validation claim.** The contributions are discourse/model analysis, and primary papers retain their own scholarly authorship.
