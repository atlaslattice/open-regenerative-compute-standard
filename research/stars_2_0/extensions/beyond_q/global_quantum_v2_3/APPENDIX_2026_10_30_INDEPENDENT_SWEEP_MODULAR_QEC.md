# Independent October 30 sweep — Modular quantum interconnects and alternative QEC
**Status: PRIMARY SOURCE CONFIRMED; no baseline edits.**
**IBM Oct 27:** two Nighthawk R1 processors connected by four ~80 cm L-couplers inside dilution refrigerator; average state transfer fidelity **99%**, remote CZ gate fidelity **94.75%**. Source: https://www.ibm.com/quantum/blog/connecting-quantum-processors-with-l-couplers . Avoid confusing 99% state transfer with end-to-end fault-tolerant gate quality.
**Nature Physics Oct 23:** Li et al., autonomous QEC beats break-even *qubit memory lifetime* in multilevel trapped-ion logical encoding using engineered spin-motion coupling and sympathetic cooling. It does **not** demonstrate large fault-tolerant computing at scale or the same resource profile as superconducting architectures. Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41567-026-03486-y .
**Relevance:** Q0–Q3 power ladder should separate physical modality, chip and inter-module error rates, distillation/cultivation success, logical operation overhead, control and cooling power, uptime and classical feedback. Cross-reference [Willow appendix](https://github.com/atlaslattice/open-regenerative-compute-standard/blob/main/research/stars_2_0/extensions/beyond_q/global_quantum_v2_3/APPENDIX_2026_10_30_GEMINI_WILLOW_MAGIC_STATE_AND_FUSION_CHEMISTRY.md). **Do not infer cost or watts from fidelity alone.**
