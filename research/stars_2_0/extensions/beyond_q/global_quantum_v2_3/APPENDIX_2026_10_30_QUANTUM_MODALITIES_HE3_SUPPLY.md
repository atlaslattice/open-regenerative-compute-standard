**Status:** Incoming DeepSeek market watch, not hardware benchmarking. Audit: [convergence register](https://github.com/atlaslattice/open-regenerative-compute-standard/blob/main/docs/fiction/iron_empire/DEEPSEEK_RESEARCH_CONVERGENCE_EVIDENCE_AUDIT_v0_1_2026-10-30.md).
DeepSeek reports Chinese superconducting, neutral-atom and photonic platforms and concern over dilution refrigerator He-3 supply; individual vendor specs, capacities and financing figures remain **pending primary verification**.
**Do not assume every quantum platform requires He-3**: superconducting qubits often employ milli-Kelvin dilution refrigeration; neutral atom and photonic systems use different operating conditions. Distinguish cooled control electronics and other components from computational qubit requirements.
Possible data fields: vendor, platform type, verified qubit count/meaning, required operating temperature, typical dilution unit charge inventory, annual makeup loss/recycling, cryogenic power, uptime, price, confidence, source date.
