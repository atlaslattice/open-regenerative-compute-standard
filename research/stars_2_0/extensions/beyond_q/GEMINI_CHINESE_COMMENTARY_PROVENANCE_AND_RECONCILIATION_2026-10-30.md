## Translated central thesis
The external commentary argues that an abundance economy is not an inert resource inventory, but a continuously maintained, evolving industrial system across **manufacturing supply chains, quantum efficiency, materials science, and system intelligence**. Industrial supply is not resolved merely by demonstrating plasma physics, and a civilization cannot create a self-maintaining ecosystem by conquest alone. This is an interpretive thesis, not an empirically established universal law.
