## Direct G3 lead — quantum calculations on FLiBe

The [new G3 appendix](https://github.com/atlaslattice/open-regenerative-compute-standard/blob/22bd49ceecdd5fbaafc0a42a3fa842e954708a9b/research/stars_2_0/extensions/beyond_q/tritium_self_sufficiency_audit_v1/APPENDIX_2026_10_30_GEMINI_FLIBE_QUANTUM_CHEMISTRY_FUSION_SUPPLY_WATCH.md)
points to Das et al., *Quantum Computations on Fusion Blanket Molten Salts*,
[arXiv:2606.30402v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30402v1), submitted June 29,
2026. The primary arXiv abstract was retrieved directly after the search-service
lookup returned an error. It reports nine clusters, fragment energy mean
absolute discrepancy ~0.3 kcal/mol, but whole-system conformational and
binding-energy discrepancies ~12 and ~110 kcal/mol respectively. The fragment
embedding/reconstruction is a major limitation. This is a computational method
demonstration, not measured bulk FLiBe extraction, diffusion or permeation.

**Disposition:** SOURCE-TRACED primary abstract; promising chemistry research,
but no kinetic coefficient, extraction fraction, source TBR or operating
temperature is changed in v1.2. A future adapter must require species,
temperature, composition, chemical potential/free energies, rate-law form,
uncertainty and experimentally or independently benchmarked transport data.
Fragment binding energies alone must not become an extraction ODE rate.
