# STARS 2.0 WP-B — Texas brine boron isotope enrichment, screening note (2026-10-30)

**Status:** concept / supply-chain hypothesis; not a validated plasma-centrifuge production line or proven reactor economics.

## Proposed chain
Texas produced-water brine assay → selective recovery of bulk boron compounds → chemical purification and conversion to a suitable feed → evaluate ^10B/^11B isotope enrichment → qualified ^11B target fabrication for p+^11B experiments. Consider ^10B coproduct demand separately (e.g., neutron capture and shielding), with market and purity verification.

Natural boron is approximately 19.9 atom% ^10B and 80.1 atom% ^11B. Mass-dependent isotope separation in rotating plasmas is physically plausible; **melting alone does not effect separation**. Plasma formation, ionization fraction, throughput, collection efficiency, isotopic assay, corrosion and chemical impurities must all be measured. Do not conflate isotope enrichment with chemical purification. Do not assume the demonstrated lithium/hydrogen partial-ionization centrifuge performance transfers to boron, much less heavier molybdenum. A published plasma mass-separation review flags throughput and mass resolution tradeoffs: https://stewartzweben.com/Papers/Zweben_PMF.pdf

## Comparison gate
Benchmark plasma centrifuge vs existing boron-enrichment procurement and process routes at equal specified ^11B isotopic and chemical purity. Require feedstock measured concentrations by basin/well, brine-flow coverage, bulk B recovery, enriched ^11B kg/year, electrical kWh/kg, product assay, uptime, waste streams, CAPEX/OPEX, enrichment tails, ^10B coproduct credit, target fabrication yield, and sensitivity bounds. No assumed economically viable capacity.

## Related but distinct Mo-99 track
Mo-98(n,gamma)Mo-99 is an established nuclear capture route; ^98Mo enrichment commercially offered using conventional **gas** centrifuges of MoF6, not confirmed industrial **plasma** centrifuges (https://www.orano.group/isotopes-stables/en/our-products/enriched-molybdenum). Mo-99 from capture has low specific activity versus fission-produced Mo-99; require specialized Tc-99m generator and recovery ledger. Source: https://www-pub.iaea.org/MTCD/Publications/PDF/Pub1589_web.pdf . Keep separate from p-11B WP-B and do not divert breeding neutrons from the D-T lithium blanket.

## Evidence labels
Observed / Published: natural boron isotopic composition; Mo-98 capture pathway; commercial Mo gas centrifugation. Proposed / Unvalidated: Texas boron recovery-to-target chain, full-scale plasma ^10B/^11B separation, capacity and economics. **No validated reactor or isotope plant performance follows from this note.**
