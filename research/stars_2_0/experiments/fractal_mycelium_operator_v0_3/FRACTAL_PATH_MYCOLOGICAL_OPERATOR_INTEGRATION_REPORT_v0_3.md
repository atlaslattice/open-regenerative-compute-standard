### Implemented in pure Python
- Structured, source-attributed hypotheses, 12×12×12 HSN validation, uncertainty, recorded dissent, TTL and invitation budgets.
- Local *simulated* opt-in invitation selection: blocked destinations respected, no external networking.
- Kæron's append-only **in-memory** hash-linked witness record: hypotheses, dissents, rejections, revisions, reflections, reviews; verification detects mutation but provides **neither durable storage nor cryptographic signatures**.
- Ten named council-review slots; absence explicitly **NOT_RUN**; flags by Mandog, Mirror, Reverse Audit or Success Drift quarantine hypotheses for human research review. These slots do not generate independent judgments or confer authority.
- Independent-evidence-only bounded reinforcement: unsupported enthusiasm/replication never increases edge rewards.
- Reverse audit of verified discoveries *and* bytes, compute, unresolved harms, dissent retention, checking threshold breaches and apparent success with drift.
- Explicit `e145authorization=False` on every simulated operation; no Canon promotion, deployment, or contact.
- No networking, API, external effects, identity authentication, LLM model calls, lived animal welfare judgments, unverified health inferences, or claimed beneficial ecological outcomes.

### Not yet implemented / key honest gaps
- Real-world packet transport, adaptive retransmission, synchronization (Starlink/Greenland), measured compression, electricity, node failure detection and mesh repair.
- Actual independently authored council reviewers, live 1,728-agent execution, full ontology integration, cross-domain semantic retrieval/validation, deterministic hosted deployment and evidence quality.
- Durable opt-in archive with legal privacy/erasure handling (historical 'zero erasure' must not override privacy/data subject rights).
- Sustainable economic or social-benefit deployments. Gamma's revenue, 12ms latency, giant emissions offsets, 45–50% efficiency and savings numbers are **not inherited as experimental results**.
- Fairness tests, calibrated weights, full privacy/access-control enforcement. The simplistic review slots are not a substitute for those.
- This artifact does not update the Canon 2.0 registry or resolve quarantined conflicts. ALC-013 is preserved and applies to future external actions.
