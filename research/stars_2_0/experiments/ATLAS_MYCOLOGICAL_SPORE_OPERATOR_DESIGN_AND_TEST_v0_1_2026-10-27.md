## Engineering hypothesis

Biological *inspiration*, not biological equivalence:
1. **Hypha**: typed, attributed neighbor-to-neighbor channel across H.S.N addresses.
2. **Spore**: compact candidate hypothesis packet: `source_refs`, `operator_id`, `claim`, `uncertainty`, `counterexamples`, `ttl`, `budget`, `no_action`. Spores are not unreviewed truth.
3. **Chemotaxis / nutrients**: bounded local relevance and independently validated utility may adjust future invitation scores; false-positive signals decay instead of self-reinforcing.
4. **Septal isolation**: faulty, untrusted or spammy branches isolated; no further propagation without trust and rate gates.
5. **Anastomosis**: edges between distant knowledge disciplines proposed for explicit, versioned graph review; not blindly accepted.
6. **Dormancy / germination**: low-priority speculative hypothesis archived, awakened only when a query demonstrates related evidence; preserves dissent and avoids endless circulation.
7. **Fruiting**: independently validated, attributable result separate from source evidence and speculative proposal. Canon promotion remains human-ratified.
