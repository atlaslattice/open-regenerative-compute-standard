## Executive summary

An independent, open-methodology ORCS research adaptation of the **State Key Laboratory of Water Pollution Control and Green Resource Recycling** (水污染控制与资源绿色循环全国重点实验室) in the Yangtze River Delta's **Water Town Living Room**, relying on public announcements by Shanghai Municipal Science and Technology Commission, Qingpu Government and Tongji University. This **mirrors public research objectives and measurable study design**; it is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, replicating unpublished algorithms of, or handling confidential material from** Tongji/Nanjing University or the Chinese laboratory. Respect each sovereign jurisdiction, local regulatory requirements, Native Hawaiian knowledge governance and data rights.

**Verified Chinese milestone:** The laboratory's demonstration-zone site broke ground **September 28, 2026**; Shanghai published official material September 30 and October 14, and Tongji reported a target completion **end-2028**. State-backed collaboration combines Tongji and Nanjing universities. The advertised "一超脑六中心" means **one digital-intelligent water-environment superbrain plus six research centers**.

**ORCS objective:** Convert that research taxonomy into a publicly reproducible *federated observational system* for Maui and Corpus Christi/Harbor Island, testing water quality, forest and vegetation health, 3D printed infrastructure, ZLD/brine solids, ecological risk, energy and resident resilience. No central AI is permitted to bypass drinking-water safety PLCs, cultural data controls, grid relays or human approvals.

## 2. Test and engineering protocol: evidence before claims

**A. Register baselines.** Each watershed/hydraulic sector has signed, versioned GIS footprints, flow and chemistry baselines, local legal water allocation, metered energy, operator and ecology context. Sensitive Native Hawaiian cultural site boundaries may be confidential. No fictional baseline.

**B. Mirror three research comparisons:** (1) centralized vs distributed desal/reuse/ZLD with hydraulic routing and 8,760h firm energy; (2) conventional PV vs native-plant-understory BioSolar with paired site controls, measured evaporation/runoff/cooling and 0/1/2% energy uplift hypotheses; (3) ecological forestry management with/without bounded local inference-assisted detection, measuring stewardship labor time, plant survival, wildfire fuel and native species richness. Include seasonality, untreated controls where ethical and safe, confidence intervals and blind validation.

**C. Separate implementation gates:** lab assay -> container-scale proof of concept -> permitted onsite pilot -> full-life-cycle comparative study -> procurement & engineering -> operational measurements -> independently reviewed replication. Stop if ecology, freshwater priority, affordability or actual authority fail.

**D. Transparent output:** open model parameters, reproducible code, machine-readable test specifications, versioned publications, provenance and negative results. Culturally restricted ecological knowledge remains under its rights-holders' control and is not obligatorily open sourced. Publish measured baseline, simulated forecasts, contracted construction and actual commissioning separately.

**E. AI role:** on-premise **inference-first** classifiers, anomaly detectors, time-series forecasts and operator recommendation engines, not heavy model training. Any broader ecological computer science research uses explicit consent, public/permissioned training data, safety boundedness and informed governance. No speculative claims about AI subjective experience.

## 4. ORCS Texas comparison experiment — full-stack water-energy-resource ledger

Compare for each specific location: (A) current authorized brackish/groundwater + reuse + demand conservation; (B) 100MGD Harbor Island marine-discharge concept, per its own permit basis; (C) optional **selective brine mining** of key constituents with marine discharge under a separate lawful permitting strategy; (D) ambitious ZLD / solids logistics as a **research comparator**, not an assumed suitable retrofit or legal standard; (E) three smaller distributed RO/reuse campuses near verified demand and industrial parks.

Each case needs common accounting for:
1. Final potable product MGD and seasonal availability; intake chemistry, RO recovery, concentrate volume/composition including toxic pre-treatment residues; publicly protected resident allocation.
2. Real project engineering CAPEX including long-distance transmission, pump stations, route/environmental fees, electricity delivery, firm reserve, offsite infrastructure, corrosion and coastal storm exposure.
3. Complete kWh/m³ separately for RO, ZLD and brine mineral separation; plant thermal needs, firm dispatch, BioSolar as **supplemental**, not a mandatory 100%-solar claim.
4. Elements Na/Mg/Ca/K/S/Cl/Br/B/Li and heavy-water isotopic D inventory in water; mass/charge closure, reagent and residue assumptions, purity, legitimate market and transport. No entire salt intake treated as revenue.
5. Hydro-ecology: water quality, plume/entrainment, runoff, sediment, PFAS/microplastics as sampled, fisheries and estuary salinity risk. Avoid assuming all seawater withdrawal or concentrate discharge is harmless OR that ZLD always beats regulated discharge in life-cycle emissions.
6. Human governance/financial: capital/debt vs benefit, construction timetable, contracted off-take, grant/tax sources actually committed, public trust and procedural requirements. U.S. state/county approvals apply; a foreign research agenda does not confer Texas permitting privileges.

The Harbor Island **191.2MGD discharge design** makes a strategically useful high-flow *theoretical brine-inventory benchmark*. At **100MGD finished** and **40% recovery**, raw water intake is **250 MGD** and RO concentrate on a simple water split is **150 MGD**, different from **191.2 MGD maximum permitted treatment-waste discharge**: **do not incorrectly equate the numbers**. The difference may reflect ancillary waste, hydraulic design or permit allowances, but the public description alone does not prove the exact reasons. Get detailed engineering balances before modeling products. At 35g/L seawater salt, 250MGD feed implies **~33,126 tonnes/day contained feed dissolved salts** as theoretical mass, **not recoverable saleable solids**. Use local seawater assays and plant definitions before relying on it. This research scale is orders of magnitude above a 1MGD Maui study and cannot be extrapolated at fixed $/MGD for ZLD.
