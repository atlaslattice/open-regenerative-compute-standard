# Help build a useful Maui resilience model

This is public research, with no implied county, utility, landowner or community partnership. Contributions can be made through this repository's issues and pull requests. Please do not publish personal records, confidential utility information or proprietary contracts.

## Most useful contributions

1. **Maui and Native Hawaiian community review:** define the priorities, cultural review, consent pathways and resident outcomes the model must respect. Independent local stewardship is essential.
2. **Measured water and wastewater baselines:** dated Maui-island/catchment flows, approved R-1 reuse, demand, treatment energy, seasonal storage and quality constraints. Keep county and island boundaries distinct.
3. **GIS siting:** public parcels, hazards, real pipe/road distances, rights-of-way, existing assets and protected habitat buffers. Separate verified facts from proposed interfaces.
4. **Energy engineering:** public load/production data, 8760-hour dispatch, interconnect limits, critical loads, battery reserves and outage recovery. Do not upload restricted infrastructure details.
5. **Circular-material accounting:** sampled wet/dry feedstocks, ash, digestate, HTL residues, contaminant fate, product qualification and realistic offtake.
6. **Transparent economics:** actual published bids, permitted reuse demand, complete technology costs, insurance/finance and household distribution. Retain unfavorable findings.
7. **Ecology and ocean health:** independent wetland/reef/watershed baselines, hydrology, water quality and native habitat metrics.
8. **Software and data:** deterministic regression cases, replayable provider fixtures, documented feed licenses, provenance, accessible mobile design and browser checks.

For data, include the source URL, collection/publication dates, geography, units, method, license, uncertainty and whether it is observed, reported, forecast, simulated or proposed. For corrections, identify the file/assumption, evidence and effect on results. No modeled positive may substitute for a required ecological, cultural or health review.

Start with `simulator-v1.2/VERIFICATION_v1_2.md` and run the tests. Please submit small, independently reviewable changes. No public rollout or project financing is currently represented as approved.
