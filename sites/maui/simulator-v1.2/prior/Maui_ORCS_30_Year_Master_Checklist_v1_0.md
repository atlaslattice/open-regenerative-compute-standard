## Water / ocean / wastewater
- [ ] Reconcile 28 MGD conceptual seawater desal capacity (West 6, North 5, Central 17) against actual potable demand, drought reserves, existing water, aquifer draw and reclaimed substitution; optimize, do not assume build.
- [ ] Inventory West Lahaina, Wailuku–Kahului, Kihei/South and proposed Waikapu 4 MGD treatment capacity, effluent grades, sludge solids, energy, upgrades and existing R-1 distribution.
- [ ] Pilot vibrating membrane bioreactor versus conventional MBR; track total energy, fouling energy, membrane life, chemicals, N/P and reliability.
- [ ] Pilot forward osmosis / anaerobic digestion / RO integration only where water chemistry and economics support it; separate brackish from seawater recovery claims.
- [ ] Test Polygone-type microplastic removal with independent verified removal rates, cost and residual handling.
- [ ] Model brine mineral recovery (magnesium, gypsum, salts, chemicals), minimum-liquid-discharge and ZLD only where lifecycle economics and marine protections pass.
- [ ] Evaluate heat recovery, pressure-energy recovery, solar scheduling, batteries, emergency water storage and Central-to-West emergency reroute.
- [ ] Compare above-ground sheathed piping, buried and hybrid routes; fiber, leak sensors, piezo monitoring, seismic/wildfire survivability, aesthetics and right-of-way.
- [ ] Map cesspools, agricultural irrigation, industrial reclaimed demand, soil salinity and aquifer effects; potable priority for residents.
- [ ] Evaluate county-owned mineralized bottled water and recycled packaging only with verified health, packaging, demand and financial feasibility; resident discount/tourist pricing proposal.

## Organics, sludge and fuels
- [ ] Build distinct generator manifests: grocery stores, farms, restaurants, hotels, food processors, household scraps, landscape/green waste, animal manure, sewage sludge, fats/oils/grease and used cooking oil.
- [ ] Apply hierarchy: waste prevention, edible rescue, safe animal feed, soil retention, then conversion; avoid counting already diverted streams twice.
- [ ] Reconcile historical 41k short tons/year landfilled food and 32k green waste against current Maui island figures, seasonality, moisture and capture rates.
- [ ] Compare AD/co-digestion, compost, fungal processing pilots, hydrothermal liquefaction (HTL), oil/grease fuel pathways, and woody-residue thermal pathways by matched feedstock.
- [ ] Quantify wet/dry solids, volatile solids, methane leakage, digester heat, biogas/CHP, HTL biocrude yields, upgrading, hydrogen, process-water cleanup, PFAS/metals and ash/residues.
- [ ] Evaluate PNNL 2026 continuous HTL engineering-scale results; do not mistake dry-ash-free yields for wet-feedstock yields or research biocrude for certified fuel.
- [ ] Verify buyers and certification for renewable diesel, marine fuels, aviation intermediates, and compatible utility biofuel; measure actual imports displaced.
- [ ] Test struvite, ammonium, compost, biochar/soil amendments, gypsum and nutrient recovery with agricultural safety trials and runoff controls.

## Recycling / micro-haul / materials
- [ ] Design source-separated neighborhood micro-haul for food/plastic/other with dedicated green-waste and hazardous-material routes; validate weekly pickup where sanitary, more frequent food pickup as required.
- [ ] Map depots, compact EV/low-emission fleets, payloads, routes, empty miles, truck hours, charging, backhauls, odor and contamination.
- [ ] Include abandoned vehicles, scrap metals, aluminum, glass, plastics, EPS surfbrick, construction/demolition materials, repair/reuse and potential 3D-printed rebuild products.
- [ ] Require product qualification, safe chemistry, structural code compliance, buyer commitments, capex and full transport economics.
- [ ] Model avoided landfill costs and recovered value separately; $10/t processing and $55/t product value remain hypotheses, not site-specific validated facts.

## Biosolar, electricity, data center
- [ ] Audit existing island petroleum generation, wind, utility PV, customer PV, planned Waena 40 MW/160 MWh battery, Kuihelani Phase 2, Ukiu biofuel and relevant grid-retirement schedules.
- [ ] Place PV on structurally suitable roofs, parking canopies, depots, industrial land and appropriate agrivoltaic sites; integrate native vegetation, bioswales and shade without claiming vegetation generates electricity.
- [ ] Run hourly electricity + thermal + chemical-fuel ledgers, including storage losses, flexible desal/wastewater/HTL loads, grid curtailment and islandable emergency microgrids.
- [ ] Size modular inference/data center to demonstrable water, routing, energy, disaster and ecology optimization benefits; meter IT load, PUE, cooling water, embodied impacts and recoverable heat.
- [ ] Match data-center and generator heat temperatures with actual digesters, drying, process preheat and thermal storage; do not count heat without a useful off-taker.
- [ ] Evaluate geothermal only after actual Maui geology and permits; avoid assumed resource availability; treat microhydro as pressure recovery, not free generation.
