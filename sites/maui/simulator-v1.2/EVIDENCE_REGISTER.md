## Source-of-truth rules

1. Label **MEASURED** only when observed by instrument, registered record or source-backed field data in the correct date and geography. 'Official announced capacity' does not equal realized operating output.
2. **FORECAST** explicitly identifies external predictions, issue time and lead time; don't present as observations.
3. **SIMULATED** identifies scenario, assumptions revision, time coverage, units and non-certification; show provisional finance separately from public value.
4. **PROPOSED** marks preliminary engineering, siting, funding, costs or implementation. **QUARANTINED** means unresolved or unsafe claim excluded from official scoring.
5. **RECEIPTS** means authentic document or raw log with stable URL/ID, checksum, collection timestamp, license and any quality flags.
6. Do not convert a countywide flow or regional percentage to Maui-island-only, assume 4 MGD future Waikapū inflow is all incremental, value uncertified fuels as sales, assume retired stations provide perpetual heat, or assume a protected wetland can accept industrial effluent.
7. All monetary inputs in `data/assumptions.json` are model parameters, not signed quotes. Climate/GHG net-positive certification must await full LCA and independent field evidence.
