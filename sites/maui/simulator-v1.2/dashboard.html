Weather-to-simulation interface

The latest available 7-day public weather forecast can drive the 168-hour PV signal, with forecast shortwave irradiance values. It does not automatically calibrate a 30-year climate distribution.

Required before operational use: site metering, Maui feeder telemetry, grid interconnection permission, sanctuary ecological baseline, verified feedstock contracts, coastal and flood maps, critical-load islanding tests, independent reviews, and historical climate ensembles.
