## 3. Physical flows to reconcile

### 3.1 Water, brine and ocean

Seawater → screening/pretreatment → advanced RO / tested FO-AD-RO → remineralized potable supply priority residents → separately metered commercial use; brine → environmentally permitted return or selectively recovered salts/Mg/gypsum → MLD/ZLD only after lifecycle capex, power and marine assessment. Municipal wastewater → safe conventional/vibrating MBR + validated microplastic/PFAS control → approved R-1 reuse and possible advanced potable pathways **only if legally and operationally certified** → farms/landscaping/industry and aquifer extraction avoided; recovered sludge routed separately to AD or HTL. Aboveground armored pipe/fiber/piezo/leak and hybrid buried alternatives, redundant emergency storage and central-to-west rerouting as **scenario subject to hydraulics and rights**.

Original proposed 6 West + 5 North + 17 Central **28 MGD desal** must be optimized downward or reserved based on actual need. The **v1.1 runnable alpha uses 2 MGD new desal as hypothetical demonstration**, not approval. Distinguish gross recycled gallons from *additional potable freshwater withdrawal reduction* and environmental water benefits.

### 3.2 Food, biosolids and fuel

Prevent edible food waste → groceries and restaurants rescue edible food → hygienic animal feed where appropriate → separate food/grocery/restaurant/hotel/farm organics → covered AD with process heat and biogas → CHP/vehicle gas where approved; or wet-feed HTL (food and **screened sludge**) → **biocrude** → external hydrogen/refining/qualification → potential renewable diesel/SAF/marine/generation fuel (no bookable revenue until offtake + certification). Digestate, AD liquid and HTL aqueous phase: **separate tested fate and no uncontrolled field application**. Compost green/woody residues, soil stabilization, fungal bioconversion as a research lane with non-sewage food-safe substrates. Anaerobic gas and HTL biocrude are alternative allocations, not two gross outputs from the same mass.

Baseline inventory distinct generator records: households, hotels, resorts, restaurants, grocery stores and markets, wholesalers, farming and ranching, tree trimmings, sewage sludge dry/wet, FOG/UCO and construction/demolition. 2019 reported **41k short tons food + 32k short tons green at landfill** are historical disposed streams. The runnable model never adds hypothetical grocery tonnage again to that 41k. Farm residues in scenarios are *additional synthetic feed*, not a claimed island fact. Recover useful solids, minerals and nutrients only where contaminants, price and offtake pass tests.

### 3.3 Materials and micro-haul

Presort food/plastic/other with separately managed green and hazardous waste. Micro-haul small EV/low-emission vehicles, local enclosed depots, frequency appropriate to odors/food hygiene, remote weigh scales/quality images and rejection receipts, track actual route load, turnaround, cold chain, charge, depot power, empty legs and coastal/sanctuary traffic; regional backhauls. Full recovered categories: EPS surfbrick only after fire/safety approvals, sorted polymers, aluminum, glass, electronics and battery safe streams, metals/scrap/abandoned cars, construction/demolition, repair/refill, 3D-printed code-compliant rebuilding materials. Include landfill methane collection before assuming all energy requires a new digester.

### 3.4 Energy, biosolar, data-center and heat

Biosolar = conventional PV with native planting, ecological stormwater management, canopies and suitable agrivoltaics. Storage is **not generation**. Island electrical dispatch: PV/wind/biogas/qualified liquid biofuel where feasible → process pumps/MBR/RO/desal/refrigeration/HTL electric draws/depots/compute → batteries → grid imported/exported with power ratings, curtailment, roundtrip losses, interconnect and resilience reserves. Parallel heat ledger: boiler/CHP/high-temperature waste heat/data center low-grade heat/digester heat, thermal storage and usable sink by temperature and time; HTL 350°C chemistry cannot be completely powered by low-grade compute heat without upgrading. Chemical fuel ledger: methane and finished certified product separate from biocrude potential. County renewables **41.6% in 2025**, **not Maui island ratio**.

Modular compute (e.g. 2 MW IT exploratory case) must meter PUE, cooling-water footprint, grid reliability, heat offtake, cyber/physical segregation, useful public computational outcomes, finite equipment replacement and optional renewable-aligned batch jobs. Compute is a **net load**, not a generator. Fail safe local analog/manual operating controls; there is **no command path** between dashboard and safety-critical utilities.

### 3.5 Agriculture/ecology/social

Compost, soil amendments, verified struvite/N/P, gypsum where pure, contaminated residues excluded, water/soil tests and nutrient loss vs controls; agricultural/farm productivity, food import displacement and runoff; East Maui water diversion alternatives; native reforestation, erosion and marine reef health; Kahoʻolawe / nearby ecological jurisdiction only with precise geographic scope. Axis deer and inspected island protein remain a **separate conditional agricultural/ecology module**—not waste feedstock credited by default. Lahaina community revitalization, tourist vs resident water pricing proposal, local staff/qualified trades, multi-year skilled worker contracts with lawful residency and **verified unoccupied** lodging only after resident needs prioritized, disaster refuge/pumps/cooling, native cultural and county/landowner consultation.

Kanahā program draft **$6M illustrative capital, plus separately funded O&M**; ecological results reported by independent observers. No sanitary/industrial shortcuts or mitigation offset accounting that could waive protection.

## 5. A real scenario engine, not one glorified spreadsheet

The alpha engine is **runnable** using Python standard library. It produces 30 annual results + 168 representative synthetic/forecast-fed hours and exposes a read-only local browser dashboard. It is intentionally only an early demonstrator: no validated complex transport optimization, 8760 full-year production dispatch, observed electric load, calibrated plant CFD or geology, hydraulic simulations, livestock ecology, grid stability, field operational data or 30-year stochastic climate ensembles. Those are the **next Work deliverables**.

**Scenario matrix** (`data/scenarios.json`): status quo; retrofit-first; distributed compost/AD; HTL + AD; HTL + modular compute; storm 72-hour grid outage; drought; fossil fuel supply/price shock; wildfire; commodity price collapse; tech yield/energy shortfall. Work should extend to Lahaina-level August 2023 combined wind/fire/evacuation, Kona-low extreme rain and wastewater loading, multiweek shipping disconnection, 100-year coastal flooding, slow sea-level trends, and simultaneous wildfire/grid/port outages; include 30-year meteorological ensemble distributions, soil moisture and drought feedback, hurricane wind damage, and worst-case island water/food/power reserves.

**Input parameters** are intentionally provisional and explicitly labeled in `data/assumptions.json`. Current starting points include historic 41k/32k short-ton landfill organics, **assumed** 11.7 MGD three-plant island wastewater reference, hypothetical baseline R-1 reuse 3.5 MGD, synthetic future biosolids 6k wet tons/year for hybrid, hypothetical PV 25 MW/data center IT 2 MW/desal 2 MGD in the illustrative hybrid. Never report these hypothetical quantities as 2026 county measurements. E.g. raw renewable diesel product cannot be derived directly from PNNL's dry-ash-free oil yield without full moisture, ash, product upgrading and offtake. 30-year NPV is incremental to a synthetic no-build case and excludes future unsubscribed export value and uncertified fuel sales.

## 7. Non-negotiable QA gates and failures

- No feedstock reuse double counted, no recovery credited without market/quality receipts, no wastewater R-1 treated as potable, and no 28 MGD build authorized by simulation alone.
- No 'year 1 pays for everything' absent signed costs. User's prior $10/ton processing and $55/ton value numbers are **hypotheses**, not adopted into v1.1 as universal real costs.
- No projected 100% or zero-liquid ocean discharge claim without brine environmental studies and energy/cost comparisons.
- No sanctuary industrial outfall; no existing East Maui diversion or native access harmed to optimize a utility ROI.
- No simulated net energy generation passed off as hourly metered export or battery generation; no HTL steam from low-grade compute exhaust without a heat pump and net power penalty.
- No claims that a developer built a certified fuel plant because PNNL achieved laboratory/pilot biocrude yields.
- No claiming a live forecast ingestion succeeded unless `live/latest.json` contains a fresh `LIVE_RETRIEVED` result with source URL and timestamp and raw response archived.
- Public engine and sources are human advisory only. Emergency alerts require authority verification and no automated action.
