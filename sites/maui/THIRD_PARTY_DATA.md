# Third-party public data and research references

This release includes dated public responses from Open-Meteo, the US National Weather Service, NOAA CO-OPS, USGS earthquakes and NOAA SWPC for reproducibility. Raw receipts record source URLs, retrieval timestamps, HTTP responses and checksums. Provider content is not relicensed by this repository. Consult each provider's current attribution, data license, permitted use and request limits before reuse or persistent service.

Open-Meteo: https://open-meteo.com/en/terms and https://open-meteo.com/en/licence . Weather forecast data are from Open-Meteo and its contributing weather-model providers. This is not an endorsement.

NWS: https://www.weather.gov/documentation/services-web-api . NOAA CO-OPS: https://api.tidesandcurrents.noaa.gov/api/prod/ . USGS: https://earthquake.usgs.gov/fdsnws/event/1/ . NOAA SWPC: https://www.swpc.noaa.gov/ . Agency responses are reproduced as retrieved; their classifications, timeliness and warnings remain the providers'.
