# ORCS v1.6 — Named U.S. desalination activation candidates against gypsum import replacement and 20% strategic reserve

**14 October 2026 | ADDITIVE · ACTUAL NAMED FACILITIES ONLY · ACTIVATION IS PROPOSED · NO PHYSICAL RECOVERY VERIFIED**

## USGS gypsum mass-balance and planning target
2025 USGS MCS2026 figures: domestic crude gypsum 20.0 Mt, synthetic gypsum 17.0 Mt, crude imports 6.8 Mt, exports 0.038 Mt, apparent gypsum consumption 44 Mt; U.S. net import reliance ~15%. **44 minus crude production (24 Mt) is NOT a verified shortage** because synthetic domestic gypsum exists. Proposed scenario: replace ~6.8 Mt/year gross imported gypsum and accumulate an additional **8.8 Mt = 20% annual consumption** inventory within a single year, assuming no existing suitable reserve stock. Therefore ~**15.6 Mt/year incremental qualified domestic gypsum in first year**, annual imports only ~6.8 Mt afterward if no replenishment needs. These are demand/planning targets, NOT established U.S. reserve law; gypsum grade and region must be matched. USGS PDF: https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026.pdf

## Transparent **capacity-normalized chemistry counterfactual**, NOT actual plant recovery
Prior Harbor Island 100 MGD finished-water proposed plan gross theoretical ~733,000 t/y gypsum equivalent. Mathematical multiplier = **7,330 t/year per equivalent MGD**, requiring **identical chemistry, plant recovery, annual utilization, and precipitation chemistry** not proven for real other sites. Thus:
- 3 named operating seawater sites 78 MGD: **571,740 t/y** unrealistic 100% gross chemistry screen; **0 tonnes of qualified recovered gypsum verified**.
- Plus four named proposals 114.8 MGD = 192.8 MGD: **1,413,224 t/y gross chemistry ceiling** under same unverified equivalence, **not any saleable yield**.
- Versus **15.6 Mt** first-year target: even hypothetical **100%** normalized capture on all 7 named sites covers ~9.1% and leaves **14.187 Mt** gap. Assuming 50% hypothetical recovery: ~0.707 Mt and ~14.893 Mt gap. These calculations should not guide construction approval or assume all 7 projects build. Local agricultural suitability and actual mineral chemistry need specific assays.
- 15.6 Mt / 0.733 Mt = 21.28 Harbor Island equivalents at 100% → **22**. At 50% → **43**. **The current named fleet does not reach the necessary scale**, and no honest list of only these existing desal sites closes the proposed national requirement.

## Practical activation steps, in order
A. Survey/intercept real concentrate upstream of dilution at Carlsbad, Tampa and Santa Barbara, with owner permission. Document quality, throughput (raw and reject), Ca, SO4, NaCl, Fe, trace metals, radionuclides if relevant, legal disposal constraints. Do not disturb water provision.
B. Embed flexible brine mineral recovery and off-take options in proposed Harbor Island and then separately Doheny/Monterey/Laguna Madre project permitting and engineering only with owner consent.
C. Screen the **actual 2024 TWDB database** plus other state databases for existing qualified saline/gypsum recovery (brackish assays must be measured), and industrial gypsum residues/FGD streams. Prioritize upgrading existing production and reclaiming already-available qualifying byproducts: U.S. 2025 synthetic gypsum **17 Mt already** in baseline, cannot count it twice.
D. Grow national replacement plus reserve portfolio only after qualified assays show viability; do not propose constructing 22–86 huge seawater desal plants *solely* to recover gypsum.
E. Competitive, product-specific allocation: direct soil correction when NRCS/field conditions support, existing industrial/wallboard/cement uses, verified sulfuric-acid hubs as separate energy-dependent process, national geographically reachable reserves. No sales, permits, dispatch or export restrictions attributed to ORCS.

## Interpretation of 'activation'
**No facility listed is currently known to mine gypsum from its brine.** Listing as candidate does not mean an owner agreed, permitted a mineral recovery retrofit, or has contracted a buyer. Site assays, owner participation and complete lifecycle/process economics determine any real activation readiness. Policy priority is domestic supply → reserve → affordability → legal export candidacy. No synthetic empirical facility or fictional material.
