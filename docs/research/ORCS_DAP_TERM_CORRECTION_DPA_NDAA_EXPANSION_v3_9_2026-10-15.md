The two established terms are:
- **DPA** — Defense Production Act (50 U.S.C. §4501 et seq), under which priority orders, allocation and incentives may be available with statutory/administrative rules.
- **NDAA** — National Defense Authorization Act, annual authorization legislation covering defense policy and funding authorizations, distinct from actual appropriations.
The phrase **"Defense Authorization Program (DAP)"** could not be verified as the official name of a separate contemporary federal program. Avoid inventing a separate agency initiative.
