## National resilience modules to build, sequentially
1. 50-state geography / physically compatible commodity grades / baseline refinery output and PADD import/export flows; existing PADD3→PADD1 $4.5424B hypothetical substitution leads.
2. Owner-approved spare transport capacity, vessel eligibility, tariffs, travel time, contractual and safety constraints, Jones Act scenarios legally verified by jurisdiction.
3. Reachable reserve and shock-avoidance planning (oil, materials, fertilizer, water and food) accounting for supply and geographic access.
4. Hazard ingest and *human reviewed* exposure linking for hurricane, flood, wildfire, earthquake, drought, grid, geomagnetic and aviation disruptions; disaster agency official alerts take precedence over experimental analytics.
5. Separate customs trade changes, domestic gross cash circulation/turnover, BEA input-output value added (net of substitution/leakage), consumer prices, jobs and operator fiscal constraints.
6. Scenario comparison across voluntary U.S.-first procurement, lawful Jones Act waivers, subsidies/efficiency, tariffs, selective import controls, reserve-first exports and consumer affordability triggers. Policy proposals must be reviewed for authority, retaliation, shortages, trade law, consumer impacts and emergency exceptions.
7. Human approval and grievance/rollback/veto; no arbitrary private asset control, no automatic import ban or export authorization.

## $3/gallon gasoline scenario
User suggests conditional `domestic served AND usable reserves at target AND typical retail gasoline <= $3/gallon` before *candidate* exports. It is **not an existing policy**. A single national gasoline threshold is insufficient for diesel, jet, LPG, crude and geographically varied grades; monitor regional real prices, refiners' costs and risks of artificial shortage or unintended harm. Separate policy levers, actual enforcement authority, refinery production elasticity, trade restrictions and international obligations. A passing computation **never grants export authority**.
