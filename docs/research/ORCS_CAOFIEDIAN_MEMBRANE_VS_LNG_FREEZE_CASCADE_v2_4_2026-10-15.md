## Identified Chinese process
Hebei Daily reporting in May 2026 explicitly identifies Tangshan Sanyou Blue Ocean Technology's Caofeidian brine concentration equipment as ultrafiltration, nanofiltration (selective Ca/Mg hardness removal) and four-stage, two-polarity reverse osmosis. The reported 3 tonnes incoming reject brine yields 2 tonnes additional fresh water plus 1 tonne concentrated liquid brine at approximately 120 g/L salinity, for soda ash/industrial salt chemical processing.
