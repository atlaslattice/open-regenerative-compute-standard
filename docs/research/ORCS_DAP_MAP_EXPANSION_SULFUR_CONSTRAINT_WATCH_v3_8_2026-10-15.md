## CHS + OCP North America proposed Waggaman, Louisiana fertilizer plant
On Aug 26 2026 CHS and OCP North America announced a proposed >1.0 million metric tonnes/year phosphate-based fertilizer finishing plant in Cornerstone Energy Park at Waggaman, Louisiana. Oct5 trade update estimates up to $450M capital expenditure, early 2027 construction initiation and late 2028 or 2029 operational potential, conditional on approvals/finance. Project is first new U.S. phosphate plant of this type proposed since 1984. OCP plans to supply phosphoric acid, described as Moroccan-sourced in some coverage, so US DAP/MAP granulation expands but full upstream American phosphate/phosphoric acid self-sufficiency is NOT automatic. The precise DAP fraction of >1Mt/y product slate and exact imported acid tonnes are NOT disclosed. Do not assume 1Mt/y DAP 100% unless confirmed. ## Existing U.S. Mosaic capacity and sulfur availability
Mosaic 2025 SEC 10-K confirms Florida and Louisiana U.S. processed-phosphate/DAP/MAP/MicroEssentials/feed-phosphate annual *operational capacity* 9.9 Mt and 2025 output 6.3 Mt, with actual site-specific utilization and downstream mix differences. SEC https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1285785/000128578526000017/mos-20251231.htm . Mosaic current corporate bulletin reports temporary idling Louisiana Uncle Sam and Faustina phosphate production plus reduced Florida output and temporary Brazil curtailments due to sulfur scarcity/high costs. The 9.9Mt is product-family combined, not pure DAP and not immediately recoverable idle production. https://mosaicco.com/Article . Q2 2026 Mosaic already partially curtailed in May, quarterly phosphate products 1.4Mt and lower fixed cost absorption. SEC https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1285785/000128578526000109/pressreleaseq22026-ex991.htm .
U.S. upstream sulfur/sulfuric acid and phosphate inputs are a capacity utilization gate. Reactivating permitted facilities with secure domestic sulfur may yield faster additional tonnes than greenfield plant construction, but constrained by sulfur purity, transport, cost, permitted emissions, phosphate rock, ammonia and actual operating agreements. Need site-by-site process mass balances and supply contracts.

## ORCS implications
U.S. reserve-first DAP project should distinguish feedstock autarky from domestic finishing. Mass balance: phosphate rock -> sulfuric acid -> phosphoric acid -> ammonia neutralization/granulation -> DAP; track sulfuric acid, imported/exported merchant-grade acid, ammonia, gypsum/phosphogypsum byproduct, permitted waste storage, and weatherization/resilience power. Do not count phosphogypsum as marketable synthetic gypsum without characterization and permits. Compare restart of Mosaic idled existing capacity versus +1Mt/year greenfield finishing plant and its reliance on imported Moroccan acid. Use fertilizer grade equivalence P2O5 rather than summing unlike nutrient tonnage, and quantify domestic import displacement net of input imports. Status is announced facility versus production constrained; no new October federal DPA fertilizer build verified.
