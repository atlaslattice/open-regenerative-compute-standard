## Analysis discipline
Potential lower Brazilian yields are a **credible transmission risk**, not an observed quantified loss from these sources. Countrywide 12% fertilizer purchases contraction ≠ 12% reduction in soybean/corn yields; farmers may change crop, application, stock draw and sourcing. Model regional soil/crop nutrient demand and purchased-vs-applied nutrient availability, planting window, prices and elasticities. Sulfuric acid is critical to phosphate fertilizer production, yet sulfur ≠ sulfuric acid ≠ elemental sulfur content ≠ gypsum equivalence. China acid exports and separate phosphate fertilizers export limits have distinct mechanisms.

## ORCS hazard/fertilizer ledger fields
Observed sulfur CFR price, acid grade and contract delivery price, Brazil and U.S. sulfur/acid imports by customs code, exporter restrictions effective dates and exemptions, supplier concentration, fertilizer plant status, phosphate fertilizer production, fertilizer delivered and applied by type, crop/soil/season, reachable fertilizer and sulfur reserves, logistics prices, environmental effects.
