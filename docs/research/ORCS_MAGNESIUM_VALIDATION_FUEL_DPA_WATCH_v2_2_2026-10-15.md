## Magnesium-from-brine experimental validation — 2026
Duong et al., *Journal of Water Process Engineering* 92 (2026), article 110857, DOI **10.1016/j.jwpe.2026.110857**, “Integrated recovery of magnesium sulfate from hydrate desalination brine and its conversion into MgO nanoparticles for phosphate removal”. Publisher: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2214714426014157

This is **laboratory-scale process evidence**, particularly for selective precipitation and reuse of recovered MgO in wastewater treatment. **Reported 98.4% Mg recovery** at pH 10.5 from five-cycle enriched brine (**6734 ±36 mg/L Mg2+**); **98.1% precursor-to-MgO conversion** and **98.8% purity**; reported phosphate removal **98.98% ±0.10%** in real livestock wastewater with **94.8±0.3 mg/L PO4**. Distinct measurements; do not multiply or transfer 98.4% to ordinary seawater/brackish RO plants, or assume nanoparticle product sales at industrial MgO price. Need measured total process energy, reagents, Mg/PO4 material balances, pilot throughput, calcium selectivity, residual chemistry and qualification. Mark as **PEER_REVIEWED_RESEARCH_DEMONSTRATION**, not COMMERCIAL_MINE or national fleet yield. Use to justify screening recovery sensitivity and possible phosphate-reuse loop; no portfolio baseline change.
