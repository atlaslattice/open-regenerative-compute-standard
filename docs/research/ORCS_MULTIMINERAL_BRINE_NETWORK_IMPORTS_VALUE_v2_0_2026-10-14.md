# ORCS v2.0 — Brine Mining Portfolio: US Network, Minerals, Import Displacement and Market Value

**14 Oct 2026 · Additive evidence-gated model, zero realized credit.** Unlike a hypothetical 100× Harbor Island multiplication, this module explicitly excludes missing brackish feed chemistry, unknown runtime, and proposed facility output from verified production.

## Inventory and source provenance
- 91 named facilities/projects are presently in the v1.8 and v1.9 register, but **not 91 observed operating brine mines**. Some were explicitly historic, idle, or proposed. Only **three current named operating seawater plant product-capacity values** are included in the numeric national *screen*: Carlsbad CA 50 MGD, Tampa FL 25 MGD, Charles E. Meyer Santa Barbara CA 3 MGD. Their **78 MGD** is nameplate freshwater produced, not independently observed brine reject volume or plant uptime. The proposed 100 MGD Harbor Island reference is **not operating**, and belongs in a separate capacity sensitivity, as do Doheny 5, Monterey 4.8, Laguna Madre 5.
- The U.S. Geological Survey reports typical seawater dissolved chemistry in mg/L: Mg 1,350; K 390; Br 67; Ca 410; sulfate 2,700; Na 10,500; chloride 19,000: https://pubs.usgs.gov/circ/2003/circ1262/ . Concentrations refer to **feed seawater**; ideal intake-side annual ion inventory can be computed at assumed recovery 50%, operating fraction 90%, 365d, converting MGD to intake via product/(0.5); brine outflow via intake − product, and ideally all rejected salts go to concentrate (some pretreatment solids removal and product carryover not accounted). **These are scenario parameters, not actual measured plant operating values.**
- In addition to MgO equivalence (Mg×40.304/24.305), K2O equivalence (K×94.196/(2×39.0983)), and gypsum equivalent as min(Ca×172.17/40.078, sulfate×172.17/96.06), compute NaCl bound min(Na×58.44/22.99, Cl×58.44/35.45). Gypsum demands sulfate and calcium, and Ca may also be assigned to other compounds: prevent double use.
- Deuterium cannot be converted into a marketable output with an RO salt-removal step. A screen may use isotope D/H about 0.000156 in water and hydrogen mass fraction 2/18.015, but D2O recovery would require independent isotope separation and energy. Treat D as **separate not monetized exploratory inventory**, not a commercial product. No deuterium import substitution credited. Likewise lithium and uranium require plant-specific trace assays and recoverability.
- Chloride/sodium/salt mass and purified market sodium chloride are different. Bromide mass (Br−) and elemental bromine product are different. MgO equivalence is not metal magnesium. K2O equivalence is not KCl fertilizer. Gypsum equivalent is not dried screened soil-amendment product.

## Import/reliance and valuation reference (USGS 2026 report describing 2025 estimates)
Official summary: https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026.pdf .
- Gypsum: 2025 U.S. crude domestic ~20Mt, synthetic already used ~17Mt, gross crude imports ~6.8Mt, total apparent consumption ~44Mt. User's proposed first-year target 6.8Mt incremental qualified import displacement PLUS 8.8Mt stock build (20% annual total gypsum use), 15.6Mt. Imported gypsum/product grade must match.
- Magnesium compounds: ~**640,000 t MgO-equivalent imports**, 60,000 exports, domestic ~400,000 production, consumption ~980,000, net import reliance **59%**. This is not Mg metal; 20%-consumption reserve = 196,000t MgO-equivalent (not yet an official reserve).
- Bromine: 2025 import unit value around **$3/kg Br-content**, gross import quantities and exports should be independently extracted from authoritative chapter. U.S. also produces bromine from domestic brines with confidential domestic quantities; there is **no inference that imports equal a real national shortage**.
- Potash: U.S. net import dependence is material; need exact 2025 K2O-equivalent imports/exports before a percent-offset claim. U.S. output value **$550M** (2025), not a per-ton quote.
- Salt: USGS salt price and specific domestic use grades must be mapped; salt inventories can exceed accessible market demand, and **captured content cannot all be priced as merchant salt**.
- Deuterium: research isotope, not a suitable undifferentiated spot commodity or import-shortage proxy. A premium laboratory price per gram should *never* be multiplied by bulk seawater D inventory.

### Pricing discipline
Market reference used without inventing unsupported 'current' spot prices: USGS 2025 **bromine imports average $3,000/t Br-content**, a real observable import unit value, not a future locally recovered ex-works price. For gypsum and salt, publicly available product/grade USGS historical unit values should be loaded, not a one-size merchant commodity quote. MgO chemicals, potash K2O, salts and isotope grades all need specific basis. This stage uses an expressly illustrative analyst editable vector ($/tonne): gypsum **$35–65**, MgO-equivalent **$250–500**, potash K2O-equivalent **$350–700**, NaCl industrial **$35–75**, bromine content **$3,000 observed import unit benchmark**, but all except bromine are **scenario assumptions, NOT current verified market prices**. These are NOT the cost of mining, not guaranteed merchant offtakes; there is no consolidated dollars of actual market output. Sensitivity pricing is to reveal which products dominate *gross potential*. Sulfuric acid derived from gypsum MUST NOT be counted as extra production at the same time as gypsum sale: alternative scenario only.

## National and 20% reserve gate
No export eligibility until verified product-specific actual U.S. deliverable production + accessible inventories >= domestic consumption + approved geography/product-grade reserve. The reserve is a **stock target** equal to 20% annual demand; not an automatic requirement to produce 20% more each subsequent year. The net import gap is calculated after domestic currently used supply. If U.S. demand already adequately served, decide whether substitution is desirable by cost, risk, emissions and affordability; avoid oversupply/dumping.

Economic columns separate: 
1. ideal ionic inventory (physical ceiling)
2. process product recovery under hypothetical 25/50/75% (cannot automatically be realized)
3. suitably qualified volume min(recovery, effective domestic import replacement + unmet reserve stock build + legitimate other demand)
4. *gross reference price × theoretical output* (screen, **not** sales, GDP or national savings)
5. net cash flow after equipment CAPEX amortization, electrical power, reagents, discharge/solids handling, logistics, product testing and contracted sales; **not currently estimable**.

## Bottleneck and next evidence tranche
Crucially, **total U.S. desal reject/brine volume is UNKNOWN** because the vast majority of named facilities lack measured annual feed/reject volumes and brackish assays. Do not call the 78 MGD operating seawater subgroup a total national brine output. Validate USGS 2025 commodity imports and unit prices at grade-specific basis, then ingest real TWDB and SFWMD plant flow and assay data. Example names and operators alone are not measured productivity.
