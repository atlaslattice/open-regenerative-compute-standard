## LNG baseline per tonne and on-site AI improvement

At measured-total-process 1%,2%,5% hypothetical optimization, gross process electricity reduction 70.25 / 140.5 / 351.25 GWh/year. ## Bio-solar definition, validated research and important distinction
Term is **vegetation-integrated conventional photovoltaic green roof**, NOT cyanobacteria/algae 'living bio-photovoltaic cell'. University of Technology Sydney Bradfield City field study Aug10 2026: PV systems atop living native green roof generated **11.1% more than conventional roof PV on average during Australian summer**, up to **23.25% during extreme heat episodes**, with 28C lower roofing temperatures at peak; not annual national uplift. Roof-area module coverage about 30% of 1300m² cited reporting; 319 panels with 14k native plants. Source https://www.uts.edu.au/news/2026/08/bradfield-city-biosolar-roof-sets-new-standard-for-climate-resilient-cities .
Applying +11.1% to annual U.S. solar generation is an **illustrative research-transfer scenario** and likely an overstatement in cold climates or winter. Rain/stormwater load, green roof saturated weight, vegetation watering/maintenance, module clearance, hazard zoning, salt spray, hurricane uplift, roof warranty, waterproofing and facility fire/explosion setbacks matter.

## National rooftop PV technical baseline
National Renewable Energy Laboratory's 2016 residential+commercial/industrial *technical* potential **1,118 GWdc, 1,432 TWh/year**, from ~8.13 billion m² suitable U.S. rooftop area. These are theoretical PV-at-all-suitable roof estimates NOT existing rooftop solar installed generation or economic buildout; https://research-hub.nlr.gov/en/publications/rooftop-solar-photovoltaic-technical-potential-in-the-united-stat-2/ . At the unverified transfer of +11.1% UTS summer uplift on identical installed suitable PV without loss of panel space, uplift is:
- **10%** of NREL technical PV generation enabled and bio-solar: 143.2 TWh PV + **15.895 TWh/year incremental** versus conventional PV.
- **25%** enabled: 358 TWh PV + **39.738 TWh/year incremental**.
- **100%** enabled: 1,432 TWh PV + **158.952 TWh/year incremental**.
Physical full-tech-potential is not expected realized energy. UTS was summer-only, national annual counterfactual needs regional weather and long-duration controlled studies; 158.95 TWh cannot be claimed as verified gain. Even 100% adoption would only be 11.1% uplift on rooftop PV, not all U.S. electricity generation.

## Combined value and separation
Example 25Mtpa process at hypothetical **2% process efficiency gain** yields 140.5 GWh annual savings gross, minus 1.051GWh example 100kW inferencing plant = 139.45GWh/year; hypothetical medium roof of 500k ft² and 65% suitability yields ~13.47GWh PV total including +1.345GWh bio-solar, for **152.92GWh combined** net onsite process-electric plus solar under those assumptions, excluding any LNG-cold savings. Of that only 1.345GWh is incremental *beyond conventional roof PV*; avoid counting full 13.47 as bio-solar-only uplift. Process optimization + solar cannot be double-valued as the same reduced load without careful metered baseline.
Grid independence requires firm gas backup/UPS, BESS and load control. PV output variable daytime; battery arbitrage/reliability not energy creation.
