## U.S. observed sulfur baseline (USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026, 2025 estimates)
- Elemental sulfur production **7.6 million metric tonnes**; Louisiana/Texas about **54%** of domestic production.
- Sulfur content national production all forms **8.1 million tonnes**; apparent sulfur consumption **9.1 million tonnes**.
- **Sulfuric acid imports 1.1 million tonnes** (in acid product units), **acid exports 65,000 tonnes** (2025 estimated). Elemental sulfur imports 2.0 million tonnes; elemental exports 1.8 million tonnes.
- USGS reports **34% of sulfur consumption supplied by imported sulfur and sulfuric acid**, while **net import reliance is 14%**; different denominators and categories. About 90% of sulfur is consumed as acid. **2.5–5 Mt/y spent acid commonly reclaimed**, an existing circular input; do not recapture as new incremental output without checking baseline.
- USGS reports **no federal sulfur government stockpile** in this series. Commercial or strategic acid inventories, grade, regional deliverability and days of cover require independent data.
- Source: https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2026/mcs2026.pdf (Sulfur chapter).

## Harbor Island as U.S. process-route candidate
Previously calculated approximately **0.733 Mt/y gypsum equivalent** at **theoretical seawater-chemistry ceiling** for **proposed/not operating** Harbor Island design. At an *illustrative* archived Chinese *design ratio* 0.4 t acid/t gypsum, **up to 0.2932 Mt/y acid design-equivalent**, not expected or actual acid production; any real conversion is subject to brine ion assays, gypsum precipitation/purity, conditioning, process energy (calcination and acid separation), yield and industrial plant economics. A 100-identical-design scenario would arithmetically multiply that to 29.32 Mt acid design equivalent, but **no evidence of a corresponding 100-plant U.S. fleet** and this must NOT be called available acid, national production capacity or an import offset. Direct agronomic gypsum use **competes** with conversion-to-acid; do not count the same tonne twice.

Optimize verified total delivered system cost and risk reduction across these competing paths. No automated export restrictions.
