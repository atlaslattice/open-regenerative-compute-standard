## Highest relevance: Hebei Tangshan Sanyou Blue Ocean Technology (Caofeidian)
October 12 South China Morning Post reports plant in *trial production* doing seawater concentrate treatment. Reported transformation of 3t brine → 2t freshwater + 1t 120 g/L concentrated solution for industrial salt. Claims >60% electricity saving compared with baseline (baseline/detailed meter data not independently established). This establishes trial production and *reported* concentration performance, not measured recovered gypsum, potash, bromine, MgO or deuterium. The article reports millions of tonnes of potentially usable brine; verify facility exact dated throughput and metered energy prior to importing into quantitative model. https://www.scmp.com/news/china/science/article/3370356/chinese-desalination-plant-cuts-60-energy-use-turning-waste-brine-industrial-salt
Secondary Oct12 trade coverage: https://www.desalination-news.com/2026/10/12/chinese-desalination-plant-cuts-energy-use-by-60-while-turning-waste-brine-into-industrial-salt/

## Official policy (July, not new overnight)
China NDRC, Ministry of Natural Resources and Ministry of Water Resources issued Seawater Desalination Industry Development Action Plan **2 July 2026**. It encourages technological demonstrations of salt, bromine, potassium and rare/strategic seawater elements, including lithium and uranium. **Policy encouragement is distinct from facility operating proof**. 2030 desalination production target 4.5 million tonnes/day reported by Aquatech Oct8; confirm official source attachment in following work. https://zfxxgk.ndrc.gov.cn/web/iteminfo.jsp?id=20646 and https://www.aquatechtrade.com/water-stories/desalination/chinas-ambitious-desalination-plans-go-beyond-water-security

## Comparative engineering model entry
Site: Caofeidian, industrial plant, *trial production reported*, first technical validation priority. Add two-stage freshwater + salt concentration output to brackish/seawater model as **PROCESS VALIDATION EXTERNAL CHINA**, not part of 91 U.S. facilities. Measure 3→2→1 tonnes by feed/water mass balance and salinity input/output; 120 g/L concentrated solution must be assigned a separate chemical spec from dry saleable industrial salt. No arbitrary “2 t fresh + 1 t dry salt” (the third tonne is concentrated liquid).
Unknown: total line throughput, power per t input product, salt conversion, material balance on gypsum/K/Br/Mg, capex/opex, outlet purity and environmental permit.
