# ORCS v0.8 — Brine and Waste-Mineral Recovery under Existing DPA Authorities

**October 14, 2026 | PROPOSED | EVIDENCE-GATED | ZERO REALIZED CREDIT | ADDITIVE ONLY**

## Decision
ORCS should measure qualified waste-brine mineral recovery as a **potential domestic supply and ecological-risk-reduction pathway**, and separately evaluate how the **existing Defense Production Act (DPA)** could support eligible industrial resiliency investments. Domestic sufficiency, geographically reachable reserves, affordable critical products, and environmental safeguards precede any modeled export candidacy. Do not assert that existing DPA authority imposes that entire regime already.

## Real precedent: DPA is not a new statute
- **Title I:** prioritization and allocation for qualifying approved programs, subject to statutory and regulatory limitations. Department of Commerce DPAS (15 CFR 700): https://media.bis.gov/about-bis/bis-leadership-and-offices/SIES/defense-priorities-allocations-system-program-dpas
- **Title III:** investment incentives, loans, purchase commitments or purchases to expand productive capacity for national defense: https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-25-108497
- **Title VII:** industrial assessments and voluntary industry coordination.
- The **October 13, 2026 Texas announcement** planned DPA support for Delek US Tyler refinery expansion; the *announcement* is confirmed, not a verified rated order, executed contract, commissioned new refinery throughput or nationwide invocation. https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/trump-administration-expand-delek-texas-refinery-using-defense-production-act-2026-10-13/ ; https://www.rrc.state.tx.us/news/christian-praises-trump-s-announced-texas-refinery-expansion/
- DPA does **not automatically waive the Jones Act, override state environmental law, ban exports, or establish a $3/gal ceiling**. Each needs a separate legal pathway, findings and economic analysis.

## Brine recovery evidence and constraints
- UNEP cites approximately **142 million m³/day** of global desalination brine in a 2019 study; this is a global historical estimate, **not U.S. recoverable production**. Concentrated saline discharges can pose ecological risks, but mitigation and magnitude are site-specific. https://www.unep.org/news-and-stories/story/towards-sustainable-desalination
- [Existing Corpus/Harbor Island brief](../ORCS_CORPUS_HARBOR_ISLAND_ZLD_DOMESTIC_RESILIENCE_BRIEF_v0_9_2026-10-13.md) reports proposed design basis of **up to 100 MGD finished water and 191.2 MGD discharge**; no operating recovery plant or mineral saleable stream is verified. Baseline source: https://www.permits.performance.gov/permitting-project/fast-41-covered-projects/harbor-island-seawater-desalination-facility
- Theoretical inventory = volumetric brine flow × assayed dissolved concentration. **Qualified saleable recovery** = technically separated output × purity × verified product-market acceptance, adjusted for downtime, losses, contaminants, processing energy, reagents, costs and environmental permits. Theoretical abundance is not output or profitability.
- Proposed recovery baskets: bulk sodium chloride where market exists; magnesium and calcium compounds as site assays/markets permit; trace lithium/bromine/other minerals as **pilot/assay-dependent**, never assumed viable. Evaluate environmental effects against actual discharge baseline including diffuser, ecological sensitivity, added energy/emissions and residual brines.

## Route from existing powers to proposed national policy
A **federal DPA program** could potentially prioritize or help expand specific eligible domestic capacity and critical materials; an economy-wide domestic-first export rule for all commodities is **an additional proposal**, not something already activated. Assess DPA Title I/III/VII scope, applicable NEPA/CWA constraints and other laws, jurisdiction, payment, property/contract impacts, trade retaliation, refinery investment and consumer effects. Never issue operational directives, export restrictions or emergency orders autonomously.
