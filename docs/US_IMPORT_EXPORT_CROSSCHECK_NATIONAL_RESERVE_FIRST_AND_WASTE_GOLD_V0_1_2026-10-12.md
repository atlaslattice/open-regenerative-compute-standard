## Source-verified first-pass trade cross-check
1. **USDA 2025 agricultural international trade**: imports **$212B**, exports **$171B**; **−$41B net dollar trade balance**, but this is not a physical calorie or crop-nutrient shortage and does not mean individual US commodities lack surplus. US exports grains, feeds, soybeans, meats, tree nuts; imports tropical goods, seasonal produce and processed foods. Source: [USDA ERS 2026 agricultural trade update](https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/ag-and-food-statistics-charting-the-essentials/agricultural-trade). [USDA FATUS calendar-year commodity, country and annual trade files](https://www.ers.usda.gov/data-products/foreign-agricultural-trade-of-the-united-states-fatus/calendar-year) provides matched exports and imports. Trade values must not be summed over nested commodity categories.
2. **US distillate (diesel/heating oil) contrast**: EIA Sept 24 2025 reported **1H2025 exports average 1.2 million b/d**, even with forecast year-end inventories at multi-year lows, refinery shutdowns and seasonal harvest/heating exposure. Source: https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php/detail.php?id=66124 . A new Oct 2026 weekly EIA-linked report said inventories around **105.1 million barrels** October 2, 2026, with the East Coast and Midwest especially exposed. Historical inventories are not a quantified reserve-days requirement and do not in themselves legally trigger exports suspension. https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/us-crude-stocks-distillate-inventories-fall-gasoline-inventories-rise-eia-says-2026-10-07/
3. **Minerals/metal scrap**: [USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries 2026](https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/mcs2026) systematically lists domestic production, trade, stocks, apparent consumption, net reliance and 2025 recycled scrap by mineral/grade; this is the correct synchronized import/export/stock denominator, not imported customs value alone. USGS Sept 2026 [data release](https://www.usgs.gov/data/mineral-commodity-summaries-2026-data-release) contains downloadable mineral series. Copper export/re-export and grade-specific scrap processing must be reconciled with domestic refined copper demand and verified usable commercial/government inventory. Reuters Oct 12 2026 reported ~1.1m metric tons of copper held commercially; **not automatically a government strategic reserve**. https://www.reuters.com/commentary/reuters-open-interest/us-has-built-strategic-copper-reserve-now-can-it-keep-it-2026-10-12/
4. **National USITC export and import flows**: [USITC DataWeb](https://dataweb.usitc.gov/) is the HS-level common product framework for 2025, with 2026 YTD separate. Imports-for-consumption and domestic exports vs reexports must be separated; origin, value, mass, grade, US customs district. Annual import–export *values* do not establish physical interchangeability or regional need. **This v0.1 has not downloaded every HTS line and does NOT claim a complete item-by-item export cross-check.**

## User's strict commodity gate
Commodity `c`, qualification/grade `g`, region `r`, season `t` and stress horizon `h`:

```text
usable_domestic_supply = measured_domestic_production
                       + verified_recycled_and_regenerated_supply
                       + lawful_draws_from_uncommitted_inventories
                       - processing_losses_and_nonfungible_grades
net_national_need = domestic_civilian_and_industrial_demand
                  + protected_essential_service_and_food_security_need
                  + scheduled_reserve_replenishment
                  + forecast_disaster_surge_needs
national_usable_margin = usable_domestic_supply - net_national_need
export_allowed = (actual_import_dependency_for_matching_grade == 0)
              AND (all_regions_and_seasons_can_access_usable_supply)
              AND (reserves_achieve_and_sustain_audited_resilience_targets)
              AND (national_usable_margin > 0)
              AND (legally_authorized_and_human_approved)
export_max_tonnes = MAX(0, MIN(verified_margin, contractually_available_free_surplus))
```

Under **strict user objective**, positive exports are not unlocked merely because a country is a net exporter of dollars or other grades while still importing the matching commodity. Track special counterexamples: crude grades, diesel winter grades, KCl vs N fertilizer, refined copper vs incompatible scrap, seasonal food grades. For products impossible/inefficient to grow domestically (coffee/cocoa/tropical goods), zero-import strict gate may remain permanently closed unless explicit policy exemptions are adopted; this is a constraint/choice, not an assertion of economic efficiency. Imports that help close actual domestic shortfalls should remain allowed until self-sufficiency is achieved; blocking trade prematurely can worsen shortages.

**Reserves must be operational**: segregate strategic-vs-commercial stocks; auditable physical location, accessible/quality-compatible inventory, emergency release rate, days-of-coverage at stressed daily consumption, distribution lanes, seasonal geography, dual sourcing, catastrophic event/replenishment, carrying/rotation cost, climate/ecology impacts, stock freshness and periodic tests. *Inventing '90 days' for all goods is scientifically indefensible.* Supply chain criticality determines horizon. Food/perishables need resilient productive capacity and cold-chain redundancy, not warehouses alone.

**Legal/political status**: Sovereign nationwide export restrictions would require authorized law and agency action, may impact treaty and contractual commitments, foreign food security, allied reciprocity, economic welfare and producer incentives. Atlas can propose a conditional control architecture and voluntarily prioritize its own supply, not claim national authority. Human governance overrides safeguard decisions.

## Waste stream "gold" gates
Classify each source as A **currently disposed or incurring treatment charge**, B **already market-valorized**, C **hazardous/contaminated or unproven**, D **soil/nutrient-functionally necessary**, or E **unknown**. **Only genuinely incremental (A) fractions** are candidates for gross extra value; B may have process-upgrading value, not full original material value again. C must not enter fertilizer/human food without contaminant pathway validation. D cannot be exported from a field at cost of long-term productivity. Track **source facility, volume, existing destination, baseline disposal invoice, chemistry, usable product grade, CAPEX/OPEX, transport, permits, local demand and inventories**.

## Formulas / policy distinction
- `net_import_dependency_t = imports_t - reexports_t - matched_exports_t` is **not sufficient** by itself: stocks, in-state consumption, processing grade and intermediate conversions alter real physical deficit. Use the normalized national material identity.
- `domestic_apparent_use = domestic_production + imports - exports + inventory_drawdown` for a matched stage/grade and unit, adjusted for transformation and losses. A positive net trade surplus is not a reserve.
- `recovery_incremental = actual_certified_product_from_waste - product_recovery_that_would_have_happened_without_project`; **avoided export of scrap** may help domestic supply only if domestic refining capacity exists.
- `value_incremental = baseline_avoided_disposal_and_logistics + genuinely incremental saleable_resource_value + avoided_import_cost_verified - recovery_CAPEX_annualized - OPEX - new logistics - residual controls - foregone_existing_value`. **Do not add avoided imports and sale value twice for the same output**; treat avoided purchase as benefit to user, sales income as transfer from purchaser.
- export unlock requires *real measurements*, not enough theoretical waste resources or installed nameplate factory output.
