## Critical research separations
1. **Projected ARC values:** Existing Beyond Q v1.1 comparison must preserve **Reactor B = 1.13 GW D-T fusion power** and **Reactor A = hypothetical 20 MW primary D-D reference**. Do not substitute older 525 MW ARC architecture, or conflate 400 MW planned *net electric* with fusion thermal power. The CFS announcements do not validate our own CAPEX or fuel-cycle economics.
2. **He-3 supply alternatives:** Include Topaz extraction as a *contingent* scenario alongside inventory/recycling, other terrestrial production, possible D-D coproduct and alternative quantum modalities. Require volume and concentration, separation efficiency, energy/cost, impurities, operational risk, contracting and actual delivered isotope availability. **Low ppb concentration alone does not show commercial supply.**
3. **Muon-enhanced p-11B:** Cross-section alone is an insufficient figure of merit. Separate catalyst inventory, muon source energy/cost, capture efficiency, sticking/loss, cycling count, fuel-plasma conditions, alpha energy capture, bremsstrahlung and total wall-plug balance.
4. **Acoustic hypothesis:** EXL-50U H-mode and muon theory do **not** demonstrate that mechanical acoustic pretreatment increases fusion yield. Preserve sham-controlled shot-success/jitter/target conditioning metrics as separate research gates; don't claim demonstrated effects without controls.
5. **Centrifugal shear:** Defensive publication may be prior art or simulation claim, but must undergo parameter extraction, numerical reproducibility, boundary conditions, stability, radiation losses and feasibility audit before model integration.
6. **PG acid and brines:** Compare mass balance, real operating recovery and substitution value, not announced nameplate outputs. A planned investment is evidence of commercial interest, not proven operating margin or overall societal ROI.
7. **Iron Empire narrative connection:** The real-world analogy of a machine dependent on scarce purchased inputs works well for imperial nutrition and isotope dependence. The story may explore why extraction/conquest becomes institutionally entrenched, **not** presume supply scarcity causes conquest or that economic cooperation guarantees peace.
