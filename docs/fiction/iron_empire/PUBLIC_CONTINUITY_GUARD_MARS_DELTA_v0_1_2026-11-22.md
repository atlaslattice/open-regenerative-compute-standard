## Film II structure — author clarification, November 22, 2026

**Author-directed development note, proposed fiction, not ratified canon.** Film II is **largely flashbacks**. The audience lives with the ancient Martian civilization while its ecology and culture still exist. Time then advances to show the planet being glassed, the collapse and exile that follow, and selected later glimpses of **the process to bring Mars back**. This is a braided timeline rather than a single historical prologue followed by a present-day lecture. The precise length and order of each temporal jump remain editorial choices.

**Suggested three-time visual braid (optional staging; not an authorized shot list):**

1. **Before:** gardens, inhabitants and ecological relationships in the remote Martian past. Cydonians and other local peoples have distinct identities, not merely a backdrop to the Empire.
2. **Rupture:** the bug invasion, the Empire's catastrophic decision to glass its own planet, and the cultural consequences of abandoning agriculture. Glassing is *seen*, not just described in an archival monologue.
3. **Return:** forward jumps to evidence gathering, contested restoration planning, field trials and early visible ecological recovery. Foreshadow the multigenerational process continuing into Film IV, rather than depicting a magical instant reset in Film II or a completed Mars in Film III.

**Present-day spine:** The Film II economic audit still culminates in the Emperor **personally authorizing public disclosure**. Present-day investigation and the ancient flashbacks illuminate each other. The future-restoration glimpses can foreshadow Film III's restoration work and Film IV's candidate ecological payoff; they do **not** move Film III's assassination into Film II or imply Mars is completely healed in either II or III.

## Film III → Long Peace → Film IV restoration payoff — author follow-up, November 22, 2026

**Candidate, not fixed release canon:** The author questions whether even Film III is too soon for **complete** restoration and suggests **Film IV** instead. Carry this as the current development preference rather than asserting a final approved global recovery date.

- **Film III (2090–2110):** The work is visible and consequential: water, soil, microbial and habitat trials; contested plans and local stewardship; successes interrupted by the assassination and coup. Steve and the Minister of Trees are collaborators, not instantaneous terraformers.
- **The Long Peace (2110–2200):** Repeated seasonal cycles, setbacks, land stewardship and generations of living ecological succession provide the time required for restoration. Political legitimacy and species survival remain separate from engineering milestones.
- **Film IV (2200–2212):** Candidate showcase of a **substantially living Mars** with self-sustaining regional biomes and settlements. The original ancient biosphere need not be wholly recovered, some losses remain irreversible, and Cydonian sovereignty and local refusals persist.
- **Film II's time braid:** Its predominantly ancient flashbacks and selective future glimpses may show stages of this process without implying that all stages finish during Film II.

**Decision still open:** Whether to declare any **global restoration threshold** by Film IV, or simply a dramatic first planetary-scale visible transformation. No specific completion percentage, ecosystem certification or exact reveal shot is yet author-ratified. This note does not shift the Film II public audit, Film III assassination, Film V uncrowned prince, or Film VI coronation and enemy self-name.

**Continuity boundary:** Film III still carries major collaborative Mars restoration, the Emperor's assassination, the contested coup and the prince's rescue/exile; Film V still leaves the prince uncrowned; Film VI alone holds his coronation and the enemy's chosen species self-name. The Nov 22 clarification supersedes the phrase "partly prequel" **as a description of Film II's emphasis**, but does not rewrite any of the original source documents. Source: directly supplied user story clarification dated 2026-11-22, alongside the earlier public November 6 correction.

## The prepared garden — Film III to Film VI defensive ecology (author direction, 22 November 2026)

**PROPOSED FICTION / NOT RATIFIED.** As Mars's biosphere returns, its inhabitants preserve the first invasion and self-glassing as a living civil-defense lesson. By Film VI, the bug threat returns to a planet whose citizens, Cydonian communities, researchers and independent AI partners have spent generations building **detectors and counter-hive vibro-acoustics**. The author wants the population almost comically vigilant: a civilization-sized neighborhood watch, with a touch of Texas-level suspicion, but not a monolithic fearful police state. People can disagree, rest, opt out of voluntary drills and contest false alarms.

- **Film II:** The ancient flashbacks show how inadequate threat understanding pushed authorities into planetary self-glassing. Later glimpses demonstrate that restoration also requires building a safer warning system.
- **Film III (2090–2110):** Steve, the Minister of Trees, Cydonian custodians and others begin restoration and long-lived ecological baselines. Some living habitats refuse invasive scans. First-generation monitors discriminate ordinary ecological change from suspicious patterns poorly, with explicit false positives and independent review.
- **Long Peace (2110–2200):** Generations develop civic routines, non-destructive sensor networks, distributed warning authorities, calibration archives, evacuation shelters, communication channels and locally controlled counters. The precautionary culture becomes everyday life, with funny overreactions and authentic trauma without equating suspicion with virtue.
- **Film IV (2200–2212):** A substantially living Mars and its surveillance ecosystem face subtle infiltration. The suspense is that detection can flag **something changed**, but cannot infer all motives or reliably identify who is coerced. Cydonian geologic and acoustic witnesses contribute without surrendering control over their habitats.
- **Film V (2212–2225):** The prince and the independently acting Mutant help establish that the bugs' natural individual lives are not identical to a Queen's imposed command. A detector alert is **evidence for investigation, not a warrant for extermination**. That distinction helps stop the unauthorized strike; the prince remains uncrowned.
- **Film VI (2225–2232):** The organized bug return tests the entire civilization. Multi-modal early warning, distributed sheltering and **counter-hive vibro-acoustics** can interfere with the *fictional* coercive implant/control-plane coupling in bounded, measured zones. This is an interruption or protective channel, **not** a new Queen command, total species pacification, mind reading, or magical instantaneous planet-wide immunity. Some failures, delays, nonresponsive individuals and continuing combat remain dramatically real. Voluntary communication and the species' **actual self-name** become possible in Film VI, after its members can answer freely.

**Fictional engineering gates:** Define distinct acoustic sensing and counter-signal functions; independently verify an implant-control dependency before claiming interference works; keep frequency/field coupling, power, range, thermal cost and false-positive injury as story questions; include stop switches and sanctuary exclusions. An acoustic alert alone cannot distinguish infection, coercion and dissent. These are speculative narrative mechanisms, not validated real-world anti-hive technology.

**Optional scene image:** A newly restored Martian grove goes silent as warning lights blink. Every neighbor calmly checks an emergency kit; the Cydonian custodian declines to activate a destructive device before sampling. An elder mutters, “Last time, we glassed the entire planet. This time, we check twice.” The alarm turns out to be a migrating native organism. The community celebrates the false alarm as a good rehearsal; the real intruder learns the defenders can pause instead of panic.

**Causal payoff:** The ancient tragedy is no longer merely backstory. The film's restored ecosystem, local sovereignty, long citizen memory and non-coercive countermeasure each solve a different problem the original Empire failed to understand. The later victory cannot erase ancient losses or require all bug individuals to accept liberation.

**Specific unresolved collision:** Film III must carry the active restoration storyline alongside the Emperor's assassination/coup. **Film III restoration is underway, not fully completed.** The Long Peace permits ecological maturation before a proposed Film IV visual payoff. Neither needs to happen instantaneously. The prince's rescue, treatment of surviving Cydonian jurisdictions and restoration consent rules still need authored ordering. The newer Mars delta says **AUTHOR-DIRECTED CONTINUITY DELTA / NOT APPLIED TO EXISTING BIBLE**, so do not silently rewrite v0.4.
