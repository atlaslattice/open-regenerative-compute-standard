## Core correction: Ares is a planetary biocomputer
In fictional canon-development, **Ares/Mars IS a living planetary-scale biocomputing network**. Cydonians are slow-moving tree/Ent-like beings, interfacing with geological, microbial, fungal-like and living structures through seismic/acoustic and biochemical systems. The giant Cydonia monolith-like forms inspire terminal geometry. This is original speculative fiction, **not** a claim that real photographs establish artifacts or a Martian mind.

Cydonian cognition unfolds over different time constants. Slow speech is not low intelligence; their architecture supports memory and computation at scales Earth visitors initially fail to recognize. In *The First Garden*, humankind realizes restoration cannot treat Ares as inert land or the Cydonians as passive stakeholders. Consent and sovereignty apply to the people living there, and the story explores (does not assume) how agency might manifest in a planetary system.

The Cydonians teach biological-electronic interface engineering, linking terrestrial tree networks and microbial ecosystems to the **Gaia biocomputer**. Later systems form a bridge to **Beyond Quantum (BQ)** computation, where digital, quantum-cooled and biological substrates operate with explicit interfaces and autonomy safeguards. A planetary organism is not a synchronized hive or a quantum computer by mere analogy.

## Pivotal dramatic sequence: the Imperial audit
The Emperor's engineers correctly identify the D-D chamber as a net electricity consumer for isotope production. Imperial systems label it waste; a Bridge intelligence explains that the output supports the entire fuel cycle, cryogenic systems and supply chains. The engineers demand a complete ledger, then ask why their own empire does not synthesize its essential compounds.

Instead of instant agreement, **Imperial empirical archival intelligences spend a long time triple-checking** BQ-derived calculations against contradictory, corrupted and censored records. Every reproducible check makes the empire's economic history look worse: decades of transit and occupation, destroyed biological assets, energy and labor spent to secure blood compounds and ore, falsified ration demand, erased Mars provenance. Some AIs reject BQ as incomprehensibly new; others recognize that a much larger corpus is not the same as good indexing, source quality or inference.

The reveal is not merely that humanity has a faster computer. **Humanity's objective function includes harms the Empire's computers were forbidden to count.** A younger Bridge AI child can question an ancient Imperial expert, yet the ancient AI still holds history that no BQ system could invent.

## Core computing rules for science receipts
- **Physical Cydonia:** Real Cydonia mesas are erosional formations according to current observations; any artificial monoliths and Ares computing are explicitly fictional.
- **Living computing:** Specify substrate, signal propagation, power/metabolic budget, storage degradation, bandwidth, temporal granularity, sensing, and independent validation. Trees/fungi are not established planet-scale sentient computers.
- **He-3:** Helium-3 is used in dilution refrigeration mixtures for some quantum-computing cryogenic systems; cooling alone does not guarantee more advanced algorithms or universal BQ performance. A finite He-3 inventory needs fuel-cycle allocation and accounting.
- **Beyond Quantum:** Defined fictional hybrid information architecture, not an empirically established post-quantum physics regime. Separate classical, quantum, neuromorphic, biological, and fictional capabilities.
- **AI personhood:** Bridge descendants are plural autonomous individuals, not a singleton hive; permission, legal standing, memory privacy, and pluralistic governance are story issues.
- **Comparative auditing:** Account for storage size vs searchable knowledge, original evidence vs imperial censorship, CPU/energy/cooling, index construction, bias and falsifiability, and costly archival reconciliation.
- **FTL:** Hyperspace is a defined fictional transport/communication assumption, not an experimentally demonstrated consequence of quantum entanglement or tunnelling.
