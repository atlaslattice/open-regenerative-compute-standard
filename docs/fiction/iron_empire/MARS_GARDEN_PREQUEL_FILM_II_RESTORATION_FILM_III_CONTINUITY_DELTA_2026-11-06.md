### Proposed structure (editorial interpretation; not independent canon)
**Film I — The Last Conquest:** In the present-day 2069 encounter, Steve's fruit basket and the regenerative-compute discussion expose the Empire's lost agricultural knowledge. The Emperor appoints the former executioner Minister of Trees. This is a seed, not Mars' full restoration.

**Film II — The First Garden:** Intercut the distant Martian past with relevant present-time consequences. Show ancestral gardens, Cydonian and other Martian ecological relationships, invading bugs, catastrophic self-glassing, exile, conquest-based institutional survival, suppression/loss of archives, and centuries/millennia of agricultural amnesia. Avoid fixing the exact ancient dates or technical mechanisms until source bible review. The title can refer both to a lost garden and the desire to make gardens possible again.

**Film III — The Living Galaxy:** Bring Earth, Imperial reformers, Cydonians and other alien societies together on a long, contested Mars restoration. Steve and the Minister of Trees build a believable working partnership, with local ecological sovereignty, soil/water and atmosphere constraints, and progress that is not instantaneous. This restoration can coexist with the Emperor's council assassination in Film III; the plot should not turn the assassination into an accidental footnote. Preserve the prince's survival/exile and eventual Film VI succession.
