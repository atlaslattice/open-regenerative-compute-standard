# The Iron Empire — proposed fusion/quantum coevolution receipt
**2026-10-30 | Fiction science receipt; not locked Continuum canon**
Real-world reference: Google Willow error-corrected magic-state cultivation (Science 2026, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42879532/) and ORNL/IBM hybrid quantum calculations of FLiBe tritium binding (https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.30402). Both are research demonstrations, not an independently operated quantum-optimized fusion power plant.
Narrative implication: The Imperial engineers' blindness to whole-system accounting can be contrasted with Earth/Bridge AIs treating computation, cryogenic logistics, blanket chemistry, isotope supply and grid stability as coupled design variables. A quantum-computing system can help investigate chemical configurations even while its own infrastructure draws on fusion-derived energy and cryogens.
Counterfactual constraint: improved fragment energies are not accurate full-cluster tritium free energies (source paper reports ~110 kcal/mol reconstruction bias); cultivation error reduction does not mean proportionate energy savings.
Dramatic mechanism should be the **iterative testing and falsification**, not instantaneous success. Preserve diverse AI agents, scientific disagreement, material and energy ledger, time to develop technology, and historical prerequisites. Link technical work separately to Beyond Q appendices.
