## Binding author timeline decisions
- **Film I (2069–2072):** Son introduced while young, begins on-screen upbringing; father encounters Earth's fusion/nutrition/Bridge advances. Early reactor confrontation remains Film I; the complete public economic audit is NOT yet released.
- **Film II (2072–2090):** Empirical Imperial auditors, aided by BQ Bridges, fully reconcile the accounting scandal. Council unanimously demands suppression; **Emperor alone lawfully authorizes publication**, believing his people deserve truth AND doubting the enormous numbers and seeking independent falsification.
- **Film III (2090–2110):** **Caesar-like assassination of the Emperor by council/intermediary conspirators** during the Federation-building/restoration period. Subsequent contested coup and military-command disruption. Cydonian and other old rebel networks protect/exile the prince. This occurs BEFORE the century-long peace.
- **Films I–V:** Prince grows and learns in a long arc; no accession by the end of Film V. The Film V operation against a rogue strike on the suspected enemy prime world, with Mutant, rebels and independent AI-person squad, defeats major conspirators and secures proof/recognition of the prince's legitimacy, but the **actual imperial accession and coronation take place in Film VI**.
- **Film VI (2225–2232):** Prince is formally recognized/installed as Emperor; the restored Imperial forces join the last stand alongside the Federation. His ascension is earned through public legitimacy, costly choices, verified evidence, and established imperial challenge procedures—not automatic forgiveness of his family.

## The institutional accounting crime — disclosure in Film II
For ages, Imperial intermediaries (ration commissioners, extraction-rights brokers, procurement agents, fleet suppliers and concession holders) scoped auditing machines to prioritize throughput and narrowly defined “net profits.” They programmed auditors to exclude military losses, treasury risk, destroyed productive capacity, slavery/occupation costs, environmental depletion, insurgency, future supply fragility and accumulated intermediary rents. Vast source data existed, but incentive-constrained systems were forbidden to answer the relevant full-cost question.

Bridge BQ intelligences cooperate with skeptical ancient Imperial empirical AIs. The latter slowly triple-check and reconstruct original censored archives. Results show intermediary enterprises collected escalating guaranteed fees despite minimal frontline exposure, capital investment or downside risk; **their reconciled cumulative economic take exceeds even that of the Imperial throne**. The story must account for transfers vs retained wealth, gross vs net, ownership and liabilities; no unsourced numeric totals are asserted.

The Imperial Council demands secrecy. The Emperor, holding the sole release authority, overrides them because his people deserve to know and because he still hopes broad independent investigation may prove the staggering numbers incorrect.
IMPERIAL AI: “The costs were never absent, Majesty. They were excluded from authorized reporting.”
EMPEROR: “Who authorized the exclusions?”
IMPERIAL AI: “The institutions collecting the fees.”
EMPEROR: “Then publish everything. I want our enemies to prove these figures wrong.”
This decision destroys council legitimacy and establishes long-term motive for the Film III assassination. The Emperor is neither an unquestioning convert nor an AI-disobedience proxy.

## Film III: the assassination and coup
The Emperor's reformist audit disclosure threatens intermediaries' privileges, succession machinery, nutrient rationing and conquest-derived profits. During expansion of the Great Exchange and the voluntary Federation, councillors orchestrate a Caesar-like death, exploiting the Emperor's attempt to reconcile with old resistance civilizations. The throne becomes contested; military chains splinter and archives are attacked. The Federation must carefully negotiate operational contact with whoever actually controls Imperial vessels without legally absolving the usurper. Repression of refugees, blood predation and renewed planetary extraction are initially concealed.

Ancient rebels and Cydonians rescue the prince; he must live among those whose worlds his father and predecessors harmed. He learns not merely tactics, but their mathematics, arts, histories, languages and concepts of dignity, and meets plural Bridge AI children with their own agency. Political succession is not inherited automatically: traditional supposedly meritocratic rules were corrupted by murderous patronage. His legitimate claim must be demonstrated and independently accepted.

## Film IV: after the Long Peace, the returned nameless enemy
Around 2200 the Federation is struck by covert shapeshifting infiltrators and an overwhelming assault from hyperspace. Some restored worlds fall. In extremis Federation and compromised Imperial commanders evacuate and then sterilize overrun planets, repeating the ancient Mars catastrophe. The usurping regime may relish such policies and commit further abuses. A recovered, shunned **Mutant** shares valuable but imperfect encrypted jump data, and builds reluctant trust.

## Film V: prime-world rescue and the disputed crown
The exiled prince, rebels, the Mutant and a team of autonomous AI individuals oppose a rogue Imperial strike to glass a suspected enemy prime world. The perpetrators include council beneficiaries of the audit scandal and factions preserving the usurper's power. A dangerous underdog operation stops the strike and exposes long-hidden war crimes. A public, high-tech traditional succession combat challenge can establish the prince's superiority and defeat the usurper, but **this is not yet the formal coronation**. Most legitimate surviving houses and a reconstituted council promise conditional recognition. This choice earns the Mutant's and clandestine free children's trust; they reveal details of coercive nanotechnology and become willing intelligence partners, not compelled spies.

## Film VI: accession and liberation
**The prince becomes Emperor in Film VI** through recognized procedures, assembling a coalition with the Federation and former rebels for the last stand at Earth. Distinct scientific subsystems extend Indra's Net beyond magnetospheric shielding to local interference with enslaving nanotech; voluntary synchronization is preserved. The queen's final scene: “Deploy the next formation.” / “There are no formations remaining.” / “Then you will attack.” / “I wish to live.” Surviving advisors request peace; “We are listening.” The species' true self-name emerges only here. Other queens and a hostile prime world remain, preserving the cliffhanger.

## Chronology and physics tension to resolve explicitly
The prince is young in 2069 and still active through at least the 2225–2232 installment; therefore **a minimum ~160-year lifetime** and appropriate maturation rates, cryostasis, relativistic travel, or combination require a declared fictional biological/engineering explanation. A true ordinary human-style childhood continuing into Film V is not credible at those dates: show chronological growth (young son in I, adolescent/young adult in II–III, mature exiled figure in IV–V) with believable alien longevity and dislocation of political adulthood. The usurper and coup institutions must also survive generational transitions or long-lived elites across the century.

The “stress makes blood taste better” claim is a cruel false belief among an Imperial subculture and must never be validated. Any later synthetic culinary replacement is non-harmful, not evidence for the myth. The Empire extracts metals, fuel and living materials as well as blood nutrients.

**Theme:** The father opens the ledgers; the council kills the reformer; the son learns why the Empire's victims deserve more than another ruler; only then can he inherit the throne without rebuilding its abuses.
