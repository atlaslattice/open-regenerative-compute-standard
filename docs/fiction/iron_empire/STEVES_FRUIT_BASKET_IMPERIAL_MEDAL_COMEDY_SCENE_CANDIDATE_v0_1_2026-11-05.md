### INT. IMPERIAL FLAGSHIP — DIPLOMATIC RECEPTION — 2069

A ceremony of terrifying architectural elegance. Twelve rows of armored IMPERIAL OFFICERS watch a human delegation approach a black marble pedestal.

On the pedestal sits a wicker basket.

Apples. Pears. Two peaches. One handwritten card.

The IMPERIAL QUARTERMASTER circles it like an unexploded bomb.

**IMPERIAL QUARTERMASTER**  
We have analyzed your gift.

**HUMAN DIPLOMAT**  
Excellent. Do you like it?

**QUARTERMASTER**  
It is a basket of fruit.

**DIPLOMAT**  
Yes.

**QUARTERMASTER**  
How many citizens were required to harvest it?

**DIPLOMAT**  
One, I think. His name's Steve.

**QUARTERMASTER**  
How many conquered territories supply Steve?

**DIPLOMAT**  
None. He's got a greenhouse.

The quartermaster turns to the BRIDGE INTELLIGENCE, an independent diplomatic advisor.

**QUARTERMASTER**  
A private agricultural empire?

**DIPLOMAT**  
No. It's a hobby.

**QUARTERMASTER**  
HE PRODUCES FOOD WITHOUT A STRATEGIC OBJECTIVE?

**BRIDGE**  
That appears to be the case.

**QUARTERMASTER**  
I must report this to the Emperor.

**DIPLOMAT**  
Would you like a peach first?

**QUARTERMASTER**  
Is it a weapon?

**BRIDGE**  
Only if thrown.

The quartermaster places the peach into a sealed diplomatic evidence container.

**QUARTERMASTER**  
How does Steve defend his agricultural holdings?

**DIPLOMAT**  
He's got a dog.

**QUARTERMASTER**  
What rank?

**DIPLOMAT**  
Mostly asleep.

### INT. IMPERIAL AUDIENCE HALL — LATER

STEVE, an unassuming gardener with soil still beneath one fingernail, stands before THE EMPEROR.

An attendant approaches with a gold medallion the size of a dinner plate.

**EMPEROR**  
For providing nourishment without tribute, requisition, or conquest, we award you the Imperial Medal of Extraordinary Agricultural Independence.

**STEVE**  
Uh. Thanks. The tomatoes were better last year.

An IMPERIAL GENERAL looks faint.

**GENERAL**  
HE REFUSES TO CLAIM OPTIMAL YIELD.

**BRIDGE**  
He is reporting seasonal variance.

**STEVE**  
Exactly. Also, the peaches were a little early.

The Emperor studies him.

**EMPEROR**  
You grew these things because you wished to?

**STEVE**  
Yeah. I like watching stuff grow.

A pause. A notion impossible to express in the Empire's standard requisition forms.

**EMPEROR**  
And what does your civilization demand in return?

**STEVE**  
You could give the basket back. My wife likes that one.

The Emperor solemnly orders the basket returned.

**EMPEROR**  
Your restraint is astonishing.

**STEVE**  
It's wicker.

Steve produces a second small, clearly labeled botanical gift.

**STEVE**  
Oh, and I've got something from the medicinal and recreational herb collection. But I don't know your biology, so please don't consume anything until your medical folks check it.

The Emperor turns to the chief BIOCHEMIST.

**EMPEROR**  
Is it compatible?

**BIOCHEMIST**  
Unknown. We do not authorize ingestion.

**EMPEROR**  
Then we shall admire the plant.

**STEVE**  
Honestly? That's most of gardening.

The Emperor laughs once, unexpectedly. Everyone freezes.

**GENERAL**  
SIRE, WAS THAT AN ORDER?

**EMPEROR**  
No. I believe it was a laugh.

CUT TO:

### INT. IMPERIAL FLAGSHIP — CULTURAL EXCHANGE THEATER — LATER

The Emperor, entirely sober, is studying archival Earth entertainment as an exercise in empathy. A vintage space-opera film plays on a massive holo-screen: earnest heroes, an elaborate villain entrance, an excessively dramatic corridor shot.

The Emperor starts laughing.

Everyone looks at him. The scene onscreen is not a comedy.

**QUARTERMASTER**  
Sire, the antagonist has threatened planetary destruction.

**EMPEROR**  
Yes. He has an entire war fleet and has chosen to announce himself to a single teenager in a hallway.

A long pause.

The Emperor laughs harder.

**GENERAL**  
Should we declare the youth a military asset?

**BRIDGE**  
No. This is a film.

**EMPEROR**  
And the soldiers march in spotless armor through a ship with no handrails!

The general begins taking notes.

**GENERAL**  
Our own ships also lack handrails.

The Emperor stops laughing.

**EMPEROR**  
Install handrails.

A beat.

The Emperor laughs again.

Across the room, Steve quietly eats a peach.

**STEVE**  
Told you fruit helps.

**BRIDGE**  
Causal relationship unproven.

**STEVE**  
You sound like my greenhouse monitor.

**EMPEROR**  
Steve, what is your rank?

**STEVE**  
I told you. Gardener.

**EMPEROR**  
Then I have been speaking to the wrong people for ten thousand years.

The projector continues. Outside, a fleet that arrived to conquer Earth waits in orbit.

For once, nobody is issuing orders.

FADE OUT.
