### INT. IMPERIAL FLAGSHIP — GRAND STRATEGIC COUNCIL — 2069

A holographic galaxy turns above the throne. Stripped worlds burn red.

THE MINISTER OF DATA kneels.

**MINISTER OF DATA**  
Your Majesty! Sector Seven refuses to surrender its freshwater reserves to our computational installations!

**EMPEROR**  
THEY DARE RESIST OUR DATA?!

**MINISTER OF DATA**  
They claim our extraction has destroyed their agriculture.

**EMPEROR**  
THEN THEY SHALL BE TAUGHT THE VALUE OF PROGRESS!

The war council roars. The Emperor eats popcorn coated with nacho-cheese powder.

**CRUNCH. CRUNCH. CRUNCH.**

STEVE, human gardener, quietly raises his hand, still holding his wicker fruit basket.

**STEVE**  
Uh... excuse me? Why don't you grow stuff there?

The entire court gasps. The executioner reaches for his axe.

**GENERAL**  
HERESY!

**STEVE**  
I mean, solar panels where they work. Crops underneath if the site allows. Treat and reuse cooling water. Maybe build enough extra power that the locals get help with their bills instead of losing their water.

**MINISTER OF DATA**  
HE QUESTIONS THE DATA!

**STEVE**  
No, man. I'm asking what the data is *for*. People might actually want you there if they benefited.

The Emperor stares at Steve.

Five seconds. Ten. Twenty. Thirty.

Steve is sweating.

**STEVE**  
Or... not. Just an idea.

The Emperor takes another handful of popcorn. Then—

**EMPEROR**  
HA! HAHAHAHAHAHAHA!

The courtiers laugh in terror. The executioner raises his axe.

Suddenly the Emperor becomes deadly serious.

**EMPEROR**  
SILENCE.

He rises. Turns to his retinue.

**EMPEROR**  
**WHY DIDN'T WE THINK OF THAT?!**

**MINISTER OF DATA**  
Our resource capture figures were correct, Majesty!

**BRIDGE**  
Within the accounting boundary the Empire selected. Benefits to residents, the loss of ecosystems, security expenses and long-term production were absent from the objective function.

**EMPEROR**  
WE CONQUERED TEN THOUSAND WORLDS AND NONE OF YOU SUGGESTED PLANTING A TREE?

**MINISTER OF DATA**  
Trees do not provide computational throughput!

**STEVE**  
They provide shade, food, and, sometimes, cooling benefits. But you'd still need to check the water and power math.

**EMPEROR**  
AND THE PEOPLE?

**STEVE**  
They're people. Ask them what they want. They get to say no.

**BRIDGE**  
Consent must precede deployment. Restoration is not permission to extract.

A beat. The Emperor looks at the executioner.

**EMPEROR**  
PUT AWAY THAT AXE.

The executioner does.

**EMPEROR**  
PICK UP A SHOVEL.

**EXECUTIONER**  
A shovel, Majesty?

**EMPEROR**  
YOU ARE NOW THE MINISTER OF TREES.

**EXECUTIONER**  
BUT I KNOW NOTHING OF AGRICULTURE!

**STEVE**  
It's cool, man. I'll show you.

**EXECUTIONER**  
...Do trees surrender?

**STEVE**  
Nah, dude. They grow.

The Emperor stares at the small potted seedling Steve has placed on the table.

**EMPEROR**  
Then learn how to keep it alive.

### INT. IMPERIAL MINISTRY OF DATA — SIX HOURS LATER

An empire-wide notice appears:

```text
IMPERIAL ORDER 004
SUBJECT: REGENERATIVE COMPUTATION — FEASIBILITY REVIEW
REQUIRED: WATER / ENERGY / ECOLOGY / RESIDENT CONSENT / FULL COST LEDGER
EXTRACTION RIGHTS: NOT CONFERRED BY A RESTORATION OFFER
OLD METRIC: CAPTURED RESOURCES
NEW QUESTION: WHAT DOES THE ENTIRE SYSTEM COST — AND WHO BENEFITS?
AXE PROCUREMENT: SUSPENDED
SHOVEL PROCUREMENT: PENDING SOIL REVIEW
MINISTER OF TREES: TRAINING WITH STEVE
```

An analyst approaches Bridge.

**ANALYST**  
What happens if the local population refuses our data center?

**BRIDGE**  
Then we do not build it there.

**ANALYST**  
...We can do that?

**BRIDGE**  
You can now.

### INT. EMPEROR'S PRIVATE CHAMBERS — LATER

The Emperor watches a human gardening tutorial. Steve explains watering frequency.

**EMPEROR**  
THE PLANTS HAVE WATER LIMITS TOO?!

The former executioner appears wearing an awkward straw hat.

**EXECUTIONER**  
Your Majesty. I have my first seedling.

**EMPEROR**  
How many have you executed today?

**EXECUTIONER**  
None.

**EMPEROR**  
And the seedling?

**EXECUTIONER**  
Still alive.

The Emperor nods with more reverence than any victory report has ever earned.

**EMPEROR**  
Then today you have accomplished something.

FADE OUT.

### Character payoffs and guardrails
- **Steve:** A civilian gardener and understated teacher, never a secretly omniscient savior. His power is to ask ordinary questions the Empire no longer knows how to ask.
- **Former executioner / Minister of Trees:** Starts as a real enforcer of Imperial brutality, not a harmless joke. Agriculture is a path toward accountable change, **not absolution**. Returns to work with Steve during the long restoration project.
- **Emperor:** This is an early flicker of reform; he retains responsibility for conquest. His later assassination is not erased.
- **Bridge:** Must independently test engineering claims; voluntary consent and stewardship cannot be replaced with central-command philanthropy.
- **Scientific boundary:** Agrivoltaics, reclaimed water and community energy are site-specific possibilities, not guaranteed net-positive offsets. This story beat cannot be cited as engineering evidence.
