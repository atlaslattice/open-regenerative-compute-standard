## Executive finding
The proposed Harbor Island desalination facility is a large potential domestic resource feedstock, **not merely a water plant**. Model resource recovery alongside desalination, refinery retrofits, low-cost domestic industrial supply, commodity-specific reserves, audited surplus exports, marine protection, and reinvestment. Do not equate gross mineral inventory with technically recoverable or qualified market output.

## Verified public baselines
- Harbor Island federal permitting dashboard: **up to 100 MGD finished water; anticipated 191.2 MGD waste discharge** at design basis, offshore diffuser. **Proposed, not operating.** https://www.permits.performance.gov/permitting-project/fast-41-covered-projects/harbor-island-seawater-desalination-facility
- Proposed discharge equivalent **~264.2 million m³/year** if run 365 days/year.
- Reuters Oct. 13, 2026: Vice President announced proposed **Defense Production Act** support for expansion of Delek’s existing **Tyler, Texas refinery (~74,000 barrels/day)**; no verified expansion size or completion schedule. https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/trump-administration-expand-delek-texas-refinery-using-defense-production-act-2026-10-13/ . This does **not** alone prove a blanket formally declared emergency, project adoption of ORCS, or lawful bypass of all environmental approvals.

## Design targets, not results
1. **Zero routine liquid discharge (ZLD)**; separately minimize ecological intake effects, high-energy processing emissions, toxic solid residuals, land impact and public costs. Strict environmental veto and independent monitoring.
2. **Domestic qualified supply first**, then commodity-specific resilient reserves, then export only lawfully eligible and genuinely surplus tonnes; low-cost domestic offtake and barter valued at transparent fair equivalent. Export cash finances more desalination/refinery retrofits only when actually realized after operating, financing and reserve expenses.
3. **Digitally auditable provenance**: producer lots, lab assays, scales, transfer and transformation records, signed timestamps and independent checks. Permissioned blockchain optional; physical verification remains mandatory. For discounted domestic contracts, contractual clawbacks and suspension after due process; no private system invents fines or criminal penalties.
4. Track separate **enterprise cash**, **industrial buyer savings**, **gross imports replaced**, **strategic disruption-loss reduction**, **water benefit**, and **environmental changes**. Never count a single avoided dollar twice or pretend all buyer savings are liquid reinvestment revenue.

## Dongjiakou cold cascade — import-vs-export LNG correction
Evidence source: https://github.com/atlaslattice/open-regenerative-compute-standard/blob/main/docs/notion-mirrors/2026-10-12-dongjiakou-lng-cascade-v05.md
Techno-economic corrections: https://github.com/atlaslattice/manus-artifacts/blob/main/public_candidate_bundle_0001/china_research/notion_archive/044-3f20c1de73d9818f9e2ce8ba0a98149f.md
Dongjiakou LNG **regasifies imports**, supplying a potential cold sink for staged power, ASU, experimental freeze-desalination and compute cooling. Corpus Christi **liquefies for export**: it is an electricity-consuming refrigeration load, **not** a free equivalent cryogenic cold source. Its refineries may offer useful waste heat, which needs temperature-matched utility integration; computer waste heat may aid ice melting, not spontaneous ice freezing.

*Screen-only* freeze concentration: assume hypothetical 25% NET extra freshwater yield on a separately identified **150 MGD RO concentrate** flow: +37.5 MGD water, ~112.5 MGD mother liquor and ideal ~1.33× solute concentration with no solids loss. **NOT experimentally established for Harbor Island brine**; depends on washes, chemistry, energy and ice purity. Freeze latent heat alone ~92 kWhth/m³ of clean water, equating to ~1.31 GWhth/day cooling duty for the hypothetical added 37.5 MGD. Cooling-system power and costs are unknown until actual coefficient of performance and heat sinks measured.

Prior Dongjiakou review integrated-process sensitivity ~23 kWh/m³; blind full proposed Harbor Island waste-volume transfer ~6.08 TWh/year, ~694 MW average electrical draw, exclusive of omitted loads. **Not a credible Harbor Island engineering estimate.** Do not conflate stage energy with whole-plant energy, nor count same heat in multiple products.

## Physical ceiling screen — not commercial product yields
Prior generic seawater calculation using proposed ~301.4 MGD process throughput, 365 days and **100% elemental theoretical availability** generated **indicative ceilings**:

|---|---:|---|
| NaCl equivalent | ~11.1 million tonnes | Not independently marketable purified salt |
| MgO equivalent | ~0.93 million tonnes | Not Mg metal; specialty grades require separate processing |
| Bromine elemental | ~27,900 tonnes | Requires selective recovery; real accessible fraction unknown |
| Potash K₂O equivalent | ~196,000 tonnes | Not finished certified fertilizer |
| Gypsum equivalent | ~733,000 tonnes | Depends on precipitation, Ca and sulfate balances |
| Strontium elemental | ~3,330 tonnes | Product grade and economics unknown |
| Lithium elemental | ~75 tonnes | Trace concentrations and cost constraints |
| Boron elemental | ~1,870 tonnes | Separation grade and off-take unknown |

These inputs are **not site assays**; exact chemistry, annual utilization, chemistry compatibility, product saleability, ZLD energy and environmental performance all unknown. Imports require **identical chemical/grade** normalization. 2026 USGS: https://www.usgs.gov/publications/mineral-commodity-summaries-2026 .

**Deuterium**: natural seawater contains the hydrogen isotope; reverse osmosis *does not extract it*. Previous theoretical gross inventory ~14,100 tonnes of deuterium element/year / ~70,000 tonnes heavy water equivalent assumes perfect isotopic separation: **NOT output, revenue or import offset**, and a dedicated highly specialized separation process would require independent safety, legal and energy scrutiny. Research lane only.

## Four-way investment experiment
(A) existing planned desal + permitted discharge;
(B) selective minerals + appropriately reduced residual discharge;
(C) selective minerals + freeze concentration/added water, real cooling input;
(D) full ZLD + qualified useful solids and verified sustainable domestic reserve/export allocation.
Compare **capital + life-cycle OPEX + actual variable avoided outfall costs + avoidable unbuilt infrastructure + ecological monitoring + avoided imports + discounted domestic buyer cost + cash-positive export receipts**.
Test physical recovery at 25, 50, 75 and 100% as adjustable hypotheses, not assumed technology results. Build pilot first with independent seawater assays, crystallization and product qualification, water/energy meters, coastal habitat baseline and permitting review.
**Residual salts/sludge are not automatically construction-safe after 'cleaning'**; demand leachability, chloride content, structural strength, aging and hazardous-waste classification.
**All economic, environmental, import replacement and schedule benefits remain unverified until site-specific measurements.**
