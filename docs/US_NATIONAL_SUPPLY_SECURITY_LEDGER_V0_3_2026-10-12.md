## Primary objective: grade-matched import replacement
Import offset screening does not require national consumption data. The primary denominator is gross imports of the specific replacement product/grade. Demand and consumption accounting is a separate, later requirement for national sufficiency/reserve/export eligibility; missing consumption does not block a waste-to-import substitution study.

For each waste stream, calculate eligible incremental feedstock × recoverable material fraction × process yield × qualified grade fraction × feasible domestic delivery/offtake fraction. Compare that output with matching imports; actual displacement additionally requires evidence that imports were replaced, not merely that production increased. Missing assay, grade fit or capacity is UNKNOWN, not zero. Do not apply the 25/50/75 capture fraction again if already included in eligible feedstock.
