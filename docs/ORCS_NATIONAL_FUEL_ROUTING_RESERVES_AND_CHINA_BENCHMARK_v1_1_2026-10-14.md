## Scenario architecture (not adopted law)
Domestic needs served → physical reserves restored by grade and delivery geography → lawfully exportable verified surplus. Hypothetical import-replacement rule must preserve supply during transition. Legal DPA analysis, Jones Act waiver bounds, freight, quality, ownership, marine risk, domestic jobs and fiscal subsidy costs are independent ledgers. The simulator cannot grant authority, enforce trade restrictions or operate a live control system.
## Upgrade gates
EIA monthly PADD flows and refinery quality/slate, BTS freight analysis framework, actual pipeline/rail/truck/marine rates and **available capacity**, domestic employment and BEA input-output VA ratios, 50-state allocations, reserve usability/stress simulations, refinery efficiency options and costed ORCS thermal/water symbiosis. Counterfactuals should show cases where domestic routing is more costly or unavailable, not suppress them.
