## Harbor Island physical opportunity (screen only)
Proposed seawater desalination, **100 MGD** freshwater capacity and approximately **191.2 MGD** planned discharge; **not operating**. Previous rough seawater-inventory screens at ~301.4 MGD process intake and 365 d/y suggested upper bounds ~**0.93 Mt/year MgO equivalent**, **27,900 t/year bromine content**, **196,000 t/year K2O-equivalent potash**, plus salt and other ions. **No product is yet recovered.** Theoretical ceilings assume typical seawater composition and full capture; site assays, separation yield, grade and project flow reconciliation are required. ## Price/value policy
Domestic industrial supply under auditable low-cost contracts, product-specific reserve targets, and commercially priced **legally exportable verified surplus**. Distinguish (a) enterprise actual sales and reinvestable free cash, (b) American buyer savings relative to comparable delivered inputs, (c) qualified import displacement and import-dependence risk, (d) avoided variable waste/outfall costs, (e) ecological outcomes. Avoid double counting and distinguish avoided expenditure from new GDP and financial cash flow. Dual prices require enforceable lawful contracts, physical custody checks, quality tests and anti-diversion auditing; a private ledger cannot create criminal offenses or fines.

## Zero liquid discharge and marine protection
**Zero routine liquid discharge (ZLD)** is the aspirational design constraint; include ecological impacts from seawater intake, residual solids, chemical reagents, energy demand, air emissions and abnormal operations. The proposed outfall and its effects are a legitimate environmental alternative to analyze, not proof of measured fishery losses. No claim that all cleaned sludge is safe for construction: qualify each material with leaching, chloride, structural and toxicity tests.

## Dongjiakou cold cascade applicability
Test ice-assisted freeze concentration, washed-ice clean-water recovery and mineral concentration, with heat/mass balances and realistic refrigeration COP. Dongjiakou LNG *import regasification* cold energy cannot be transplanted directly to Corpus Christi LNG *export liquefaction*, which consumes refrigeration. Prior 25% hypothetical water recovery from 150 MGD RO reject gives +37.5 MGD, 1.33x ideal concentrate; not demonstrated. Measure cooling energy, net water recovery, cleaning, precipitates and plant uptime. Source prior v0.9: docs/ORCS_CORPUS_HARBOR_ISLAND_ZLD_DOMESTIC_RESILIENCE_BRIEF_v0_9_2026-10-13.md .

## Policy and implementation
Prioritize national self-sufficiency for critical qualified materials and strategic reserves, but test against comparative advantage, production costs, ecological constraints, import diversification and legal trade obligations. Reserve-first exports require product-specific gate design and lawful authority. Start with independently assayed pilot, metered energy/water flows, costed ZLD alternatives, downstream qualification and transparent dashboard. No claims of government adoption or completed deployment.
