# ORCS White Paper — AI for Public Industrial Abundance, Oversight and Resilience v1.0

**2026-10-14 | PROPOSAL / NON-CANON | NO OFFICIAL DEPLOYMENT**

## Purpose
A public, source-linked, state-resolved, reproducible industrial supply-security dashboard is a testable civilian use of AI: optimize *real production and logistics* rather than benchmark scores or model training capacity. Benefits hypothesized: import substitution where physically and economically justified, cheaper domestic fuel and essential inputs, reliable strategic reserves, fewer waste streams, useful domestic employment, and measurable water/marine protections.

## Human-in-the-loop institutional boundaries
AI systems ingest validated public data and owner-authorized private records. AI may **propose** transport allocations, refinery retrofit options and reserve forecasts but may not autonomously direct refinery/LNG safety controls, override owners, change public laws, impose fines, revoke rights or grant export permissions. Audited source provenance, cybersecurity, dispute process, independent laboratory quality receipts, safety veto, public consultation and rollback authority are essential. Blockchain can timestamp provenance but cannot verify physical tonnes without metering, scales and independent checks.

## Evaluation protocol
Publish baseline and scenarios with independently reproducible tests and confidence bands. Main KPI: $/year physically verified imports displaced *net of exports forgone*, $/year domestic value added and employment, consumer gasoline and diesel prices, commodity-specific days of reachable reserve, resilience disruptions avoided, energy/CO2, water/solid waste and ecological stress. Secondary display 1.5× macro multiplier only as an illustrative hypothesis, carefully separated from gross output and GDP. Operator margins and public support are constraints rather than citizen-facing success metrics. Critically, **a U.S. merchandise deficit is not intrinsically GDP loss**, and China trade figures must be normalized by reporting period and accounting definitions.
