AI mapping, drought and wildfire early-warning, humans directing fuel treatments and prescribed burns.

### Sensitivity / investment gate
At 450k base finished short tons, a $100/t drop in base sales price cuts EBITDA $45M. If $70M premium sales fail to materialize but $30M costs remain, advanced EBITDA falls from $93.5M to $23.5M, yielding negative post-debt annual cash (after sustaining), despite fictional cheap credit. For realism, run price-demand elasticity, grades, cannibalization, product certifications, planned downtime, actual capex quotes and independent mass balance. Fictional cheap loans improve debt service, but don't manufacture product demand.
