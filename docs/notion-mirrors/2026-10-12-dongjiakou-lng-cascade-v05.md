## Main finding — an actual Dongjiakou LNG cold-use build, not yet the Atlas full loop
Two **separate** real initiatives share the Sinopec Qingdao LNG receiving-terminal industrial context:
1. **¥108 million cold-energy cascade demonstration**, finalized tender May 19, 2026, began detailed design and construction: low-temperature single Rankine cycle for power plus an intermediate heat-exchanger loop for *terminal/building air conditioning*. Reported future 26 GWh/y electricity generation and 8 GWh/y avoided purchased cooling electricity. These numbers are projected, NOT delivered. [Official Qingdao West Coast government June 3](https://www.xihaian.gov.cn/ywdt/tsxq/202606/t20260603_10624706.shtml); [Qingdao Daily June 2](https://epaper.qingdaonews.com/qdrb/html/2026-06/02/content_144591_3494474.htm); [Sinopec paper May 27](https://www.cpnn.com.cn/news/nyqy/202605/t20260527_1890734.html); [April 2026 EPC notice with base engineering and 854-day scheduled works](https://www.qianlima.com/bid-590286404.html).
2. **¥230 million \~660 t/day LNG-assisted air separation**, Sinopec Qingdao LNG and Sichuan Air Separation agreement signed Sept 2, 2025, intended LIN, LOX and liquid Ar; no independently established commissioning or transfer to item 1. [Qingdao Daily Sep 2](https://www.dailyqd.com/guanhai/407396_1.html) and [China General Machinery Industry Association](https://www.cgmia.org.cn/Web/News/Detail/23623).
The named investments are separate. DO NOT add their projected savings as though both projects are commissioned and co-optimized. The ASU project is not confirmed operating, and neither official announcement identified an integrated new data center, freeze-desalination water-production block or mineral recovery facility receiving cascaded LNG cold.
## Recovered primary Atlas cold-cascade design and corrections
Original full synthesis: <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3f20c1de73d9813e8b50c94c2cd372ad"/>. Original stages LNG regas -\> ASU -\> freeze desal -\> DLC data center/HIL -\> ambient rejection. Refines the user's remembered list: a **cold carrier exiting a data-center heat sink is warmed**, so it cannot simply flow afterward to a colder-required ice-making step. Correct temperature ordering is thermally cold-demand-intensive ASU / ice-making first, lower-grade server cooling later, with facility-side CDU segregation, backup cooling, and optional reuse of **server waste heat to melt washed ice**. Freeze/concentrate brine simultaneously branches downstream to chemistry for minerals; material solids must be tested separately for mortar/aggregate/ceramics. Final contaminated residues need hazardous-waste classification and lawful disposal rather than blanket burning or sealing.
Original <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3f20c1de73d9810c82f0cbacf1a13278"/> and <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3f20c1de73d9817da4d7e5fd04808b42"/> expressly downgrade the aspirational 14 million m3/year freeze-desal and zero-cost MVR displacement claims. At \~6% modeled salinity, ice forms before NaCl salt crystals; NaCl/water binary eutectic \~23.3 wt% and -21.1°C. A -20°C source alone cannot achieve eutectic with finite heat-transfer approach. Other salts and antiscalants complicate true brine path. In a batch controlled experiment, [Chang et al. Water Research 2016](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.watres.2016.06.046) froze seawater with modeled LNG-relevant cooling and \~50% wash water vs raw-ice mass, reaching \~300 mg/L TDS in meltwater; TDS alone is not potability. A 2017 prototype paper [Lin et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijhydene.2017.04.176) reported \~150 L/h and only \~50% single-pass salt removal, not potable. No Dongjiakou-specific deployed ice desalter confirmed.
**Illustrative engineering bound:** to freeze 1 m3 water, latent cold duty about 91.7–92.8 kWh thermal (water mass density and latent basis). Aspirational 14 Mm3/y product would require \~1.28 TWh thermal/y freezing and roughly same heat to melt ice, before wash/recycle, cooling inlet, process losses. Average \~146.6 MW thermal duty. A 40 MW continuously fully heat-captured IT load generates at most 350.4 GWh/y heat, enough for ideal melting \~3.8 Mm3/y (27% of 14 Mm3). Real useful heat is less. This is a bound, not Dongjiakou performance. Available LNG regasification cold is not continuously measured, so thermal delivery/cold-SLA stays UNKNOWN. Distinguish heat/melting from cold/freezing; don't count energy twice.
## Mineral and solid-waste pathways: differentiated maturity
- The official [2026 West Coast government marine strategy](https://www.xihaian.gov.cn/ztzl/wgk/jcgk/zdjc/hyjjfz/index.shtml) calls out Dongjiakou's existing 100,000 m3/day desalination as a strategic anchor, promotes marine desal machinery and *research/innovation* in potassium, bromine and magnesium extraction. This is a policy plan, NOT proof of a Dongjiakou commercial RO-brine mineral recovery line.
- <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3f40c1de73d9814bb6b7fff1b2c4c475"/> notes prototype magnesium-hydroxide economics and warns about distinct feed chemistry, salts remaining dissolved, lithium dilute feed, bromine mass conservation and avoided-disposal overclaim.
- [Qingdao 2025 solid-waste disclosure, published June 2026](https://www.qingdao.gov.cn/zwgk/xxgk/sthj/gkml/gwfg/202606/t20260610_10638285.shtml) records **citywide** WWTP sludge 827,500 tonnes (80% water basis), 728,500 tonnes incinerated, 96,000 tonnes land-applied, **3,000 tonnes building-material utilization**. Not all Dongjiakou, not identical to desal brine sludge. City also reports \~27.88 million tonnes C&D waste, actual reuse \~14.653 million tonnes citywide, not automatically local node input.
- [Huicheng Environmental corporate industrial portfolio](https://hcpect.com/about/industrial.html) reports a Dongjiakou facility commissioned in 2022 for 40kt/y FCC catalytic materials and 30kt/y spent catalyst regeneration/reuse; operator claims are not a product-transfer receipt to the desal node.
- Crucial failed-operations counterexample: [July 15, 2026 Qingdao court restructuring notice](https://pccz.court.gov.cn/pcajxxw/pcgg/ggxq?id=7bfd52b14b0349e689f2e9ab492b29b9) for Qingdao Kangnier Dongjiakou environmental resource treatment center: installed design 80kt/y industrial/medical hazardous waste: 60kt/y incineration + 20kt/y physical-chemical treatment, but company **ceased operation** and entered restructuring. Permits and buildings alone do not imply available residue handling service or a commercially functioning circular loop. Check hazard classifications, disposal capacity and bankruptcy status before credit.
- Sludge-to-mortar must be characterized for heavy metals, organics, chlorides, strength, durability, leachability, and certification; contamination concerns trump circularity. Incineration can oxidize organics but does not destroy inorganic salts/metals; stabilized hazardous residues may need permitted secure disposal. No generic 'burn all toxic sludge'.
## Cold cascade as corrected reference architecture
```plain text
LNG receiving/regasification (reported operating)
  |-> low-temp ORC power (project in detailed design/construction)
  |-> ASU liquid oxygen/nitrogen/argon (signed separate project)
  |-> intermediate subzero cooling loop (candidate)
       |-> RO-brine ice crystallizer (proposed/pilot science)
       |     -> washed low-salt ice -> clean melting, heat recuperation, polishing -> qualified municipal/industrial water
       |     -> concentrated mother liquor -> assay -> Mg/K/Br/CaSO4/NaCl conditional recovery
       |           -> qualified mineral sales/industry or agriculture
       |           -> physical/chemical residuals -> suitable cementitious materials OR lawful hazardous treatment
       |-> higher-temperature branch/CDU -> compute-cooling candidate -> server heat recovered to ice melt or other suitable heat sink
       |-> independent ambient thermal backup and LNG interruption bypass
```
Independently preserve RO primary desal baseline (2016 plant), industrial steel-wastewater reuse, genuine public 2026 cold-use construction. No site-level claimed heat, mineral, freeze or data-center transfer credit without metered linkage.
