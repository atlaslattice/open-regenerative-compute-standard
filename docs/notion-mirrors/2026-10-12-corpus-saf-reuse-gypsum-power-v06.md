## Permaculture-style gypsum cohabitation AND chemical conversion
Reference <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3f30c1de73d981bea155f9e14eb1753e"/> and <mention-page url="https://app.notion.com/p/3f40c1de73d981a98101fd4c3233b06f"/>. The original 2026 research uses mechanized functional crop diversity, agronomic soil testing, and measured allocation decisions. Qualified recovered gypsum can help some sodic soils and address calcium/sulfur deficits, but default dose is ZERO where soil EC/SAR/ESP/Ca/S/soil water and drainage do not justify it. [AgriLife salinity guidance](https://agrilifeextension.tamu.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/E60-managing-soil-salinity.pdf).
Gypsum (CaSO4·2H2O) to sulfuric acid is a DIFFERENT, energy-intensive chemical plant pathway needing strong heat/reductant, permitted off-gas/SO2 processing and qualified feed. [ACS 2025 study](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/full/10.1021/acssuschemeng.4c08838) discusses 900–1200 C conversion process. Low-grade server heat cannot run this kiln. [EPA Valero site](https://ghgdata.epa.gov/ghgp/service/facilityDetail/2022?ds=E&et=&id=1008110&popup=true) reports existing refinery sulfur recovery plant; comparing commercial recovered sulfur/sulfuric acid supply may beat brine-gypsum acid economics.
Only ONE physical tonne allocation: agriculture OR acid feed OR certified construction material OR warehouse, not all of them; balance Ca, sulfur, water, reagents and residual pollution with measured assays. Original Dongjiakou production estimates are scenario ceilings, not site receipts. Sulfur import substitution requires real replaced imports/receipts.

## Dedicated cost-plus compute power — correct scales
Dongjiakou cold project future PROJECTIONS: 26 GWh/y generated electric + 8 GWh/y avoided purchased cooling electricity, distinct energy accounting, NO realized Atlas credit. Reference 40 MW continuous IT at PUE 1.25 demands \~50 MW facility and 438 GWh/y. The 26 GWh/y generator = \~5.94% of facility annual demand (2.97 average MW), and 8 GWh/y conservation = additional \~1.83%; combined potential effect \~7.76%, conditional and not firm.
Corpus Christi is LNG-export/liquefaction-dominant, no nominal free regas-cold as Chinese reference. Create 0%, 25%, 50%, 100% campus-electricity supply cases with dispatchable dedicated generation plus renewables, storage, black start, firm interconnection and weather-rated backups. Cost-plus rate must include full allocated CAPEX/depreciation, operator O&M, actual fuel, network/interconnection, compliance, financing, reserves, disaster maintenance and transparent third-party audit. Full onsite supply a PROPOSAL, not a consequence of Chinese 26GWh projection.
