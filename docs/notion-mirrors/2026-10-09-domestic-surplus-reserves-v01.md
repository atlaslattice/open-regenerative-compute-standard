## DeepSeek translated assessment, user definition correction
DeepSeek supports an at-cost internal cooperative exchange, a system-surplus gate before outside sale and governance-defined allocation of legitimate external sale surplus. It identifies four ambiguous comparison baselines (imports, no production, previous period, Atlas-only demand) and originally recommends Atlas-only demand. **User explicitly clarifies:** systemwide means **CHINA-WIDE domestic needs fully met, national imports for that commodity eliminated, and production exceeding national requirement**. This supersedes the narrower network-only interpretation. Market-rate external opportunities do not displace a domestic at-cost transfer obligation.
## Two distinct gates
**NATIONAL_COMMODITY_SUFFICIENCY_GATE:** commodity-specific / grade-specific / time-window: verified usable domestic production + legitimate inventory withdrawals + net domestic availability, against total national demand, legally committed exports, losses, seasonality and resilience reserves. Synthetic short-form `available_domestic_output - total_domestic_demand - required_reserve = surplus` is a simplification only: actual model includes stock changes and accounting identities. Commercial exportable surplus only if `national_surplus_t>0`, no regional/seasonal/quality shortfalls and public-trust restrictions satisfied.
**NATIONAL_IMPORT_ELIMINATION_GATE:** actual relevant customs import volumes zero for defined commodity specification/reporting period, not merely modeled capability or theoretical self-sufficiency; distinguish `IMPORT_REPLACEMENT_CAPACITY_VERIFIED` from `ACTUAL_IMPORTS_ZERO_VERIFIED`. National policy and private foreign trade are not controllable by Atlas; treat national full elimination as *objective*, not something node management can assert unilaterally.
**ATLAS_ATTRIBUTION_GATE:** quantify delivered attributable Atlas domestic supply and incremental import displacement separately, with source provenance; never credit Atlas for the national total or ordinary non-Atlas production. Partial import-gap closure remains reportable progress, but **not** systemwide-export gate satisfaction.
## Transfer rules and external sales
**INTERNAL_AT_COST remains invariant even if an external marketplace exists**: fully loaded auditable direct/indirect production + distribution cost, no transfer markups between Atlas nodes; node-to-node accounting receipts cancel in consolidated Lattice books. Distinguish non-Lattice domestic purchasers from Lattice recipients; national need can be served via outside buyers before any export. A national export/surplus rule needs lawful export permits, binding contracts and contingency safety, and should be established by GOV01 and subject to local/national law.
External surplus sold at market price **only on quantity authorized after national domestic commitments and reserves**; calculation of **NET DISTRIBUTABLE SURPLUS = external cash receipts minus audited incremental production, handling, delivery, selling, taxes, compliance and mandated reserve contributions**, subject to authorized governing agreement. One illustrative 1 kg case: ¥2 production, ¥6 external selling, ¥1 additional marketing/delivery =\> ¥3 before any further taxes/reserve costs; 50% producer/25% distributor/25% common pool only as governance *illustration*, not policy. No invented sale credits.

## Cautions before promotion
China is a large domestic producer/exporter of certain fertilizer products already; baseline genuine net import dependence must be checked by product/grade/year, not assumed for Mg, nitrogen, phosphorus, salt, K or lithium. National demand is not limited to Atlas node orders. No claim of control over national import policy, supply quantities or independent audit. **GitHub PR282 currently models at-cost internal transfers but does NOT yet implement national needs/imports/reserves/external-sales gates; queue schema + tests for a new revision after governance review**, do not imply current code already enforces it.
**Keeper:** A national-surplus cooperative first closes measured domestic deficits and actual imports; then sells only qualified reserve-adjusted excess outside; retains intra-Lattice at-cost flows; allocates net external proceeds by approved rules; and publishes tamper-evident physical/financial receipts.
