## Abstract
Atlas Lattice is a forkable method for mapping local resource flows, evaluating biological and circular recovery pathways, and measuring outcomes without treating theoretical potential as achieved performance. The central question is whether a qualified, legally available input can be processed safely and economically, whether this changes a defined dependency or service, and whether people and ecosystems benefit compared with a credible counterfactual.
This synthesis adopts GitHub v0.6’s five-ledger framework—financial/domestic value, strategic resilience, Eden/QOL/ecology, Global Replication Value (GRV), and resource integrity—and carries forward the Notion papers’ China 2.0 resource portfolio, calculation corrections, citations, and provenance. It makes the user’s scope explicit: base-case biofuel pathways use wastes, residues, and byproducts; dedicated energy crops are excluded. “Waste does not exist” remains a resource-search hypothesis, not a claim that all residuals are safe or recoverable.
The public Dongjiakou simulator remains a research candidate. This paper does not change its topology, arithmetic, source hashes, 23 original site-specific data requests, six unresolved ecological vetoes, or zero realized credits.

Evidence class and value domain are separate. Policy, capacity, construction, measured operation, modeled scenario, synthetic test and unknown must not be conflated. A policy target does not close a site request; a fork does not establish operating replication; gross exposure is not avoided loss.
## 2. Waste and residuals
For base-case fuels, allow qualified waste, residue, or byproduct inputs only. Purpose-grown fuel crops are out of scope. Corn-grain ethanol may remain in a separately labeled comparator, not in this base case. A waste stream still has collection, storage, pretreatment, conversion, labor, energy, finance, residual-management and maintenance costs. Gate-fee income and avoided disposal costs are separate and count only when actually received or avoided.
“Waste does not exist” means: investigate prevention and safe useful recovery for every residual, and document why a material remains unusable. It does not mean every material is safe, ecologically removable, legally available, economical, or recoverable. Sludge and ash construction uses require source-specific treatment, contaminant assays, leaching and durability tests, product acceptance and end-of-life review. Immobilization does not destroy contaminants.
A feedstock lot’s allocatable dry matter is qualified dry matter less soil/ecological retention, existing obligations and collection/storage losses. Record current uses, but do not subtract them twice if already included in obligations. No biomass tonne may simultaneously earn SAF, methane, feed and soil-return credits.
## 3. China 2.0 resource lanes
**Agriculture and protein.** Combine crop diversification/intercropping, genetics, rhizobia/biological nitrogen fixation, precision management, and feed-protein substitution as separate levers. Their effects overlap; do not multiply headline gains without factorial or interaction evidence, adoption rates, suitable land, water and farmer economics. NBS reports 2025 calendar-year soybean output of 20.91 Mt and imports of 111.83 Mt. The 2025/26 consumption estimate of 121.52 Mt and 108 Mt imports are a different marketing-year basis. The reported 1.642 t/ha intercropped soybean yield is a study scenario input, not national incremental output or proof maize yield stays constant on all converted land.
Mycoprotein and other microbial proteins are possible feed or food substitution pathways, not tonne-for-tonne soybean-meal replacements. Compare digestible protein and amino acids, energy, organism/process/substrate approval, safety, feed trials, dry/wet basis, coproducts, CAPEX/OPEX and local substrate logistics. Cooperative lease financing shifts payment timing; it does not erase total cost. Decentralized pretreatment/bioreactors are an option to model against regional hubs.
**Methane and natural gas.** NBS’s 2025 natural-gas import figure is mass (127.87 Mt); it cannot be compared directly with biomethane bcm without composition, density, standard state and energy-value conversion. The official 2019 biomethane guideline sets targets above 10 bcm/year by 2025 and 20 bcm/year by 2030. NEA’s 2026 report states roughly 1 bcm/year of built biomethane capacity across 146 projects at end-2025. Target, capacity and actual production are distinct. Residues compete with soil retention, feed and materials. Low-concentration methane recovery and chemical conversion remain separate engineering questions.
**LNG components and helium.** Ethane recovery from imported LNG changes product mix and may reduce separate ethane-supplier exposure; it does not automatically reduce methane imports. Count replacement heat and separate product ledgers. Helium BOG recovery at a terminal and closed-loop recovery at semiconductor fabs are complementary but different boundaries. Dongjiakou-specific helium concentration/flow is unknown. SHRU is a proposed open integration specification, not one validated product. Acoustic/piezo sensors can detect or localize leaks; they do not themselves seal them. Cryogenic self-healing containment requires helium-specific material, cycle, safety and repair verification.
**Sulfur, nutrients, brines and cold energy.** Gypsum-to-acid transforms sulfur-bearing feed; it does not create elemental sulfur. The reported 0.4 t acid/t gypsum is a project reference, not a universal yield. State dry feed, moisture, acid strength, impurities, energy, residues and offtake. Distinguish elemental K, K₂O, KCl and brine bases. Crop-residue K supporting soil fertility is not spare merchant K; struvite is principally N/P recovery, not KCl. Qingdao’s reported 26 GWh/year projected generation and 8 GWh/year avoided cooling power are separate outcomes, not 34 GWh generated electricity.
**SAF and marine fuels.** Preserve the airport-node SAF concept, but count only eligible waste/residue/byproduct feedstocks in the base case. HEFA from qualifying waste oils, cellulosic ATJ, biomass conversion, bio-LNG, renewable marine fuels and green methanol need separate chain-of-custody, conversion, lifecycle, certification, energy, product specification and offtake accounting. NEA capacity is national context, not local production or displacement.
## 4. Resilience and economics
A 5% chance of at least one event over ten years is not a 5% annual probability. Under stationary independent annual hazard, p = 1 − 0.95\^(1/10) ≈ 0.5116% per year. This translates an assumption; it does not estimate actual risk.
At \$19.5T GDP, a modeled 0.812% loss is \$158.34B if that scenario occurs; a separate hypothetical 5% GDP-loss scenario is \$975B if it occurs. Neither is Atlas-avoided loss. An avoided-loss estimate needs event probability, duration, severity, recovery, physical coverage, protected sector and counterfactual.
Do not use 2× or 8.01× as China-specific net-GDP multipliers. Report gross output separately from incremental domestic value added, household income, imported inputs, opportunity cost, distribution, taxes/transfers and displaced activity. Imported supply also supports domestic port, terminal and distribution value.
## 5. Eden/QOL, GRV and AI provenance
QOL is a first-class outcome vector: worker safety and exposure, heat/noise, wages and training, green-space access, water safety, landscape comfort, biodiversity, maintenance burden, community participation and distribution. Beauty is a design ambition. Measure baseline, water quality, survival, maintenance, user experience and ecological function. Aesthetic score cannot offset ecological vetoes. Personal motivation/individual QOL belongs in a companion paper, distinct from worker/community metrics.
GRV advances through AVAILABLE → REPRODUCIBLE → INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED → PILOT DEMONSTRATED → OPERATING REPLICATION → MEASURED BENEFIT. Public code creates availability; stars or forks alone do not demonstrate adoption.
Atlas Prime/Lattice Guide, DragonSeek/AtlasSeek, Continuum OS, Aluminum OS, UWS, Alexandria 2.0, and Sheldonbrain/Solbrain belong in a linked AI-first knowledge/governance companion. Their role here is source-to-claim provenance, versioned canon, review gates and reusable knowledge—not evidence of industrial control or resource yield. Preserve raw model outputs as attributed inputs and record corrections, reviewer decisions, dates, and receipts.

## 7. Additive schema proposals and implementation gates
The proposed resource-allocation schema adds waste-only eligibility, feedstock class, current-use allocation, constituent balances, source receipts, sludge/ash treatment and leaching gates, and agronomy overlap references. The claim-measurement schema adds source independence metadata, LNG/natural-gas volume normalization, declared standard state and energy basis, and trial-level agronomy overlap groups.
Synthetic fixtures demonstrate the expected data shape, not local evidence. The test plan rejects dedicated crop allocation in the waste-only base case, mass-conservation failures, unresolved normalization, overlapping yield stacking without evidence, unsupported policy-to-production promotion, unqualified sludge/ash use, and any positive credit behind an unresolved veto.
The simulator adapter remains a later separate PR. Preserve original outputs and requests; require successful CI, independent review, source-to-claim traceability, negative tests and no regression before merge. Field operation requires operator/regulatory authority and consent.
